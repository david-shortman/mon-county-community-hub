# Life areas coverage map

Which parts of a good life the site covers, which it covers in part, which wait for David, and which are missing. It merges three lists: the life areas in epic #222, the charter's topic areas, and the 17 resident groups in [`docs/groups/`](../groups/). Built for #222 in sprint wave 4 (#529) on October 31, 2026, from the pages on `main` at commit `401d670` and the 80 open PRs that day.

**Status words**

- **Covered:** a page on `main` answers the main questions.
- **Partly:** a page exists, but a common question in the area has no answer.
- **Waiting for David:** the page or fix is built, in an open `for:david` PR (high stakes: housing, money, food, crisis lines, or another reason in `docs/COORDINATION.md`).
- **Missing:** nothing on the site, and no open PR.

Review this table monthly with the #222 table. When a PR merges, move its row.

## 4. Missing areas, ranked

Scored the way the needs register in #984 scores needs: severity, reach, and room to help, 0 to 3 each, plus whether a page can ship without David. "Reach" uses the best public number we could read on October 31, 2026. Where the only number is national, it is a national rate, not a Mon County count, and it says so. None of these areas has an entry in the needs register yet, and none is asked about in the findability question set (`content/findability/questions.yaml`), so they were invisible to both.

| Rank | Area | Reach | Severity | Room to help | Total | Ships without David? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal help that is not eviction (small claims, clearing a record, name change, jury duty, free lawyers) | 3 | 2 | 3 | 8 | Yes |
| 2 | Pets and animals (adopt, dog tags, lost or found pets, strays, rabies shots) | 3 | 1 | 3 | 7 | Yes |
| 3 | Birth certificate, ID, marriage license | 3 | 1 | 3 | 7 | Yes |
| 4 | Being a family caregiver | 2 | 2 | 2 | 6 | Yes |
| 5 | When someone dies (death certificates, wills and estates, Social Security) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 6 | Yes |
| 6 | Parking in Morgantown | 2 | 1 | 2 | 5 | Yes |
| 7 | Banking, credit and debt help | 2 | 2 | 2 | 6 | No, topic `money` |

**Where the reach numbers come from** (all read October 31, 2026):

- **Legal help:** 74% of low-income U.S. households had at least one civil legal problem in the past year, and 77% of rural households (Legal Services Corporation, 2022 Justice Gap study, https://justicegap.lsc.gov/the-report/executive-summary/). National, not a county count.
- **Pets:** 43.7% of U.S. households own a dog and 34.1% own a cat (AVMA, 2026 pet ownership survey, https://www.avma.org/resources-tools/reports-statistics/us-pet-ownership-statistics). Applied to Mon County's 42,652 households (County Health Rankings 2025, the count used in #984), that would be roughly 18,600 households with a dog. This is a national rate applied to a local count, so treat it as a rough size, not a fact about Mon County.
- **Caregivers:** 63 million Americans are family caregivers (AARP and the National Alliance for Caregiving, Caregiving in the US 2025, https://www.aarp.org/pri/topics/ltss/family-caregiving/caregiving-in-the-us-2025/). National.
- **Birth certificate and ID, when someone dies, parking:** no public count found. Reach is scored from how widely the need comes up: almost everyone needs a birth certificate for a REAL ID, a passport or some jobs, and every family deals with a death at some point. Parking reach is a judgment, not a number.

**Built in wave 4 (October 31, 2026):** ranks 1 to 5, as `/legal-help/` (#1002), `/pets/` (#1007), `/ids-and-records/` and `/when-someone-dies/` (#1018) and `/caregivers/` (#1011). Rank 6 (parking) is next. Rank 7 needs David, because any money page waits for him.
