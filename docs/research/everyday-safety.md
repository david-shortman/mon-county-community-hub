## 1. Scams

**Where to report**
- WV Attorney General, Consumer Protection Division: hotline 1-800-368-8808, online complaint form, email consumer.complaint@wvago.gov. https://ago.wv.gov/file-complaint-consumer-protection-division
- The AG's Latest Scams page: "Call 800-368-8808 to reports a scam or email scams@wvago.gov." https://ago.wv.gov/consumer-protection/latest-scams
- FTC: "Report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov." https://consumer.ftc.gov/features/scam-alerts

**What hits West Virginians**
- The AG's 2025 top ten, in order: Medicare, robocalls, Social Security or government imposters, loans, sweepstakes and prizes, debt collection, service disconnection, computer and phishing, tax relief, home warranty. https://ago.wv.gov/article/attorney-generals-office-warns-top-scams-2025-medicare-scams-lead-list (January 16, 2026)
- July 2026: "the Consumer Protection Division received 176 scam complaints," led by Medicare imposters. https://ago.wv.gov/article/top-scams-reported-west-virginia-attorney-generals-office-july-2026
- Nationally, the FTC says "about $16 billion was reported lost in 2025, the highest on record," including $3.5 billion to imposter scams. https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/06/ftc-data-show-people-reported-losing-3-point-5-billion-imposter-scams-2025

**Utility shutoff scams (Mon Power)**
- FirstEnergy: "Our utilities do not accept prepaid debit cards or wire transfers as payment." "They will not demand payment over the phone, in person, or at a particular physical location." "If your account is past due, you will receive a written notice." "FirstEnergy and its utilities will never require payment to restore power." https://firstenergycorp.com/help/safety/scam-info/known-scams.html

**Benefits (SNAP EBT skimming)**
- DoHS: "Update your PIN at the beginning of each month before your benefits are issued." If a card may be compromised: Office of EBT 304-558-4126 or customer service 877-716-1212. https://bfa.wv.gov/article/dohs-urges-ebt-cardholders-take-steps-now-protect-benefits-skimming (April 17, 2025)

## 3. Home safety

**Free smoke alarms**
- American Red Cross Central Appalachia region (all 55 WV counties): "home fire education sessions on home fire safety and FREE smoke alarm installations. Please click below to sign up. A member of our Home Fire Campaign team will follow up with you to schedule an in-person appointment." Request form: https://americanredcross.jotform.com/261896562727876. Page: https://www.redcross.org/local/west-virginia/about-us/our-work/home-fire-campaign.html
- The Morgantown Fire Department page lists public education but no smoke alarm program. https://www.morgantownwv.gov/208/Fire-Department

**Carbon monoxide** (CDC, https://www.cdc.gov/carbon-monoxide/about/index.html)
- CO is "an odorless, colorless gas that kills without warning." Symptoms: "headache, dizziness, weakness, upset stomach, vomiting, chest pain, and confusion."
- Battery or battery backup CO detectors near every sleeping area; never run a generator inside a home or garage; outside, more than 20 feet from windows, doors and vents.

**Winter heating** (US Fire Administration, https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/prevent-fires/heating/)
- "Keep anything that can burn at least 3 feet from all heat sources." "Turn space heaters off when leaving the room or going to bed."

**Lead** (Monongalia County Health Department, https://monchd.org/services/lead-testing/)
- Finger-stick testing "available for anyone 1 or older." Insurance is billed; without insurance the test is grant funded. Appointments: 304-598-5181.
