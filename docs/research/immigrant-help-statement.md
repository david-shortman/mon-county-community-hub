# Immigrant and refugee help: what is local, as of October 30, 2026

For issue #229. Every line below was checked on the group's own page on 2026-10-30.

## Confirmed

- **Catholic Charities West Virginia still runs refugee resettlement.** Its program page calls it "the only Refugee Resettlement agency in West Virginia" and lists who may qualify under federal law (refugees, asylees, certain SIV holders, trafficking victims, Cuban and Haitian entrants, certain humanitarian parolees). Its locations page lists "Refugee Resettlement and Immigration Services 304.343.1036" under Monongalia County services. It also offers low-cost immigration legal services (family visas, green cards, work permits, DACA renewals, TPS, citizenship). [Program page](https://www.ccwva.org/programs/west-virginia-immigration-refugee-resettlement-services/), [locations](https://www.ccwva.org/locations/). Entry: `ccwva-immigration-services`.
- **Mountain State Justice** gives free legal help, including immigration cases (family visas, asylum, citizenship, U and T visas, VAWA, removal defense), to West Virginians with low income. Morgantown office: 1029 University Avenue, Suite 101, 304-326-0188. Intake line: 304-344-3144 or 800-319-7132. [Immigration](https://mountainstatejustice.org/our-work/immigration/), [get help](https://mountainstatejustice.org/get-help/). Entry: `msj-immigration-legal-help`.
- **MTEC adult education** offers English classes for adults. Entry: `mtec-adult-education-esl` (already on the site).
- **State courts** give free interpreters in court cases. Entry: `wv-court-interpreters`.
