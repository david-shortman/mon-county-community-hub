# Court-ordered community service hours: what official pages say, and what they do not

Last checked: 2026-11-17 (UTC)
For issue #2048 (week 4 plan, queue item 5). Research only: no outreach. Every fact below was read on the linked page on the checked date. No staff names.

## The question

"Community service hours" was a real search with no answer on the site (findability pass, `docs/build-log/2026-11-15-0345-findability-nov-14-pages.md`). A person searching this usually has been told by a court to do hours, and wants to know where to do them, who approves the place, and how to show they did them.

## What official pages say

**State law (WV Code §62-11A-1a), for adults.** https://code.wvlegislature.gov/62-11A-1A/
- A court may sentence a person to "a number of hours or days of community service work with government entities or charitable or nonprofit entities approved by the circuit court."
- "Regarding any portion of the sentence designated as confinement, eight hours of community service work is to be credited as one day of the sentence imposed."
- "Regarding any portion of the sentence designated as a fine, the fine is to be credited at an hourly rate equal to the prevailing federal minimum wage at the time the sentence was imposed."
- "A person sentenced under this program may be required to provide his or her own transportation to and from the work site, lunch, and work clothes."

**The local probation office.** The West Virginia Judiciary's page for Monongalia County probation lists the office at the Monongalia County Justice Center, 75 High Street, Suite 16, Morgantown, WV 26505, phone 304-291-7217. https://www.courtswv.gov/node/32716 The page does not mention community service.

**Youth.** The WV Division of Corrections and Rehabilitation's Monongalia County Youth Reporting Center (325 Willey St., Morgantown, 304-225-0548) "serves both status offenders and youth who have been adjudicated delinquent." Its list of services includes "Community Service". https://dcr.wv.gov/facilities/Pages/youth-reporting-centers/Monongalia-County.aspx

## What we could not find

- No Monongalia County, circuit court, magistrate court or City of Morgantown page that says how a person finds an approved place to do court-ordered hours, or who keeps a list of approved places.
- No official page on how hours are logged or proved (a form, a signature, who it goes to).
- No current official page for an adult day report center or community corrections program in Monongalia County. The newest mention we found is a state news release dated June 14, 2021, saying the Monongalia County Commission got $190,000 for "the continued operation of a community corrections program in Monongalia County" (https://dhs.wv.gov/News-Announcements/Pages/Governor-Justice-announces-latest-WV-Community-Corrections-grants.aspx). It is five years old and says nothing about community service.
- Nothing on hours ordered by Morgantown Municipal Court.

## What this means for the site

A page that only quotes state law would not answer the question people bring ("where do I do my hours?"). The steps that matter are set by the court in each case and by the office that supervises it, and no page we could read says how that works here.
