## Gaps and open questions

- Big Brothers Big Sisters of North Central WV: a person should search the [WV Secretary of State business search](https://apps.sos.wv.gov/business/corporations/) by name. The search form could not be run automatically.
- Mountaineer Boys and Girls Club: news reports (seen only in search results, not opened) say Boys and Girls Clubs of America revoked the club's charter in 2019. A dated article page could not be opened. The Secretary of State record would settle whether the corporation still exists.
- Bartlett Housing Solutions: whether the corporation itself is dissolved is not known. Its IRS record is not revoked (ProPublica, newest filing tax year 2022).
