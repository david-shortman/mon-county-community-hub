# More ways to act, from the official steps we read on November 1, 2026

Part of #411 (ideation: more ways to act, beyond the top 12). Sprint wave 9 (#529) spent the day reading the forms and pages state and local offices publish about applying for help. Several of those pages name a small action a resident can take that our pages do not yet make easy. This note lists them as options, neutrally, with the source for each. None is built yet. Each would cost $0 to build, needs no outside account or contact, and uses only what the source says.

Scores use the charter's five measures, 1 to 3 each: **Ser**ious problem, **Reach**, **Amp**lifies existing work, **Fast** to ship, **Safe**. "Waits for David" means the page it would go on is high stakes (food, money, housing or a crisis line).

## Top picks

- **1 and 3** score highest. Both go on high-stakes pages, so each would be a short PR that waits for David. Option 3 is time sensitive (deadline November 25, 2026).
- **5** shipped with this note, on `/identity-theft/`. **6 and 7** can ship without David and are one line each.
