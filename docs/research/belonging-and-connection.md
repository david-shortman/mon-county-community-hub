## 1. What the data says about loneliness here

CDC PLACES (County Data, 2025 release, from the 2023 BRFSS survey) gives model-based estimates for Monongalia County adults ([dataset](https://data.cdc.gov/resource/swc5-untb.json?stateabbr=WV&locationname=Monongalia)):

| Measure | Mon County, crude | Mon County, age-adjusted (95% range) | United States, age-adjusted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loneliness among adults | 35.2% | 33.3% (28.7 to 38.1) | 33.2% |
| Lack of social and emotional support | 20.9% | 20.7% (17.1 to 24.4) | 24.1% |

- About 1 in 3 adults here report loneliness, close to the national figure.
- Across West Virginia's 55 counties, age-adjusted loneliness runs from 32.0% to 39.0%. Mon County (33.3%) is among the lowest in the state: 52nd of 55.
- These are model estimates, not a count of people. The ranges are wide.

The issue also mentions the Mon Health 2025 needs assessment and the WV MATCH survey. Neither was read in this pass.

## 2. Free ways to meet people that already exist

Groups with entries on the site, each checked on its own page:

- **Senior centers:** Senior Monongalians (meals and activities, Mountaineer Mall), Westside Senior Center in Westover (members 50 and older, $10 a year).
- **Youth:** 4-H clubs and camps (no fee to join), The Shack Neighborhood House.
- **Arts:** the Arts Council's monthly First Friday for the Arts, Monongalia Arts Center classes and events.
- **Parks:** BOPARC community centers.
- **Community centers and halls:** being added by another sprint lane (see #529).

No single list of clubs, newcomer meetups or civic groups was found.

## Needs register candidate

- **Need:** about a third of adults report feeling lonely.
- **Evidence:** CDC PLACES 2025 release, Monongalia County, loneliness among adults 35.2% crude, 33.3% age-adjusted (28.7 to 38.1); lack of social and emotional support 20.7% age-adjusted ([source](https://data.cdc.gov/resource/swc5-untb.json?stateabbr=WV&locationname=Monongalia), read 2026-10-30).
- **Trend:** unknown; PLACES added this measure recently.
- **Who works on it:** senior centers, the library, BOPARC, 4-H, faith groups, United Way's volunteer program.
- **Severity note:** Mon County is near the national level and lower than most WV counties, so this may rank below needs where the county is worse off.
