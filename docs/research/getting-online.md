## Lifeline

Lifeline is a federal discount of up to $9.25 a month off phone, internet or bundled service. Only one discount per household. Recertify each year within 60 days of being asked, or the discount stops. ([about](https://www.lifelinesupport.org/about-lifeline/))

**Who qualifies:** someone in the household gets Medicaid, SNAP, SSI, Federal Public Housing Assistance, a Housing Choice (Section 8) Voucher, project-based rental assistance, or the Veterans Pension and Survivors Benefit; or household income is at or below 135% of the 2026 federal poverty guidelines ($21,546 for 1 person). ([how to qualify](https://www.lifelinesupport.org/how-to-qualify/))

**How to apply:** online at lifelinesupport.org, through a phone or internet company, or by mail to Lifeline Support Center, PO Box 1000, Horseheads, NY 14845. People with a disability who need help, or want a paper form mailed, can call 1-800-234-9473. ([how to apply](https://www.lifelinesupport.org/how-to-apply/))

### What to bring (from USAC's supporting documents page)

Every document must show your name (or your dependent's name). ([source](https://www.lifelinesupport.org/supporting-documents/))

- **If you qualify through a program:** a benefit award letter, statement of benefits, benefit verification letter, or a screenshot of the online benefits portal. It must show the program name, who issued it, and a date within the last 12 months or a future end date.
- **If you qualify by income:** last year's tax return, a current income statement from your job, a Social Security benefits statement, an unemployment or workers' comp statement, a divorce decree or child support award, or pay stubs for three months in a row. It must show your yearly income and a date within the last 12 months.
- **Proof of identity, if asked:** for date of birth, an unexpired driver's license, birth certificate, passport, or government or military ID. For your Social Security number, a Social Security card, SSA-1099, a W-2 from the last 2 years, or last year's tax return.
- **Proof of address, if asked:** a driver's license or other ID, a utility bill (not from your internet company), a mortgage or lease statement, or your latest W-2 or tax return. Or use the map tool in the online application.

## Pass 3, November 14, 2026 (sprint wave 16, #529)

What was still open after the October and November 1 passes: free or cheap devices, and low-cost plans.

### Added

- **Find a company that takes Lifeline** (`lifeline-companies-near-me`). USAC's Companies Near Me tool searches by ZIP code or city. USAC says the list may not show every company. This answers "where do I use my Lifeline discount" without the site naming or ranking any company. ([source](https://www.lifelinesupport.org/companies-near-me/), read 2026-11-14)
- **Low-cost refurbished computers by mail** (`pcs-for-people-low-cost-computers`), a national nonprofit. Who qualifies, in its words: buyers "must generally meet the 80% Area Median Income (AMI) guideline or participate in a qualifying assistance program." It needs a photo ID and proof of a program or income, dated in the last 12 months, and no proof of citizenship. Orders "include shipping within the U.S. (excludes PO Boxes, AK, HI, and PR)" with a one-year warranty. Stores are only in Colorado, Illinois, Maryland, Minnesota, Missouri and Ohio. ([eligibility](https://www.pcsforpeople.org/eligibility/), [get tech](https://www.pcsforpeople.org/get-tech/), read 2026-11-14). Prices were left off because they change often; the card points to the store.

Both show on `/internet/`. The Lifeline card makes the page a money page, so the PR waits for David.
