# Starting a business in Mon County, by location

Last checked: 2026-10-30
For issue #19.

Where your business is decides which local office you deal with. Every business also registers with the state. Each step below links to the page it comes from. The matching entries are in `content/resources/`.

## Everyone: register with the state

1. Get a federal Employer Identification Number (EIN) from the IRS.
2. Register on the One Stop Business Portal. One filing covers the Secretary of State (for LLCs, corporations and some other types), the State Tax Department and WorkForce West Virginia. The state says every business must register with the Tax Department before doing business.
3. Secretary of State fees depend on the business type: $100 for an LLC or for-profit corporation, $25 for a nonprofit corporation, plus a $1 online fee. Some veteran-owned businesses and young entrepreneurs (WV residents age 18 to 29) may have the first fee waived.

Sources: [WV Secretary of State](https://sos.wv.gov/register-new-wv-business), [One Stop Business Portal](https://business4.wv.gov/startmybusiness/Pages/Register-Your-Business.aspx), [State Tax Department](https://tax.wv.gov/Business/BusinessRegistration/Pages/BusinessRegistration.aspx). Entry: `wv-one-stop-business-registration`.

## Selling food

All of Mon County goes through the Monongalia County Health Department:

- **Restaurant, food truck or shop:** plan review, then an inspection. Every food business needs a Certified Food Protection Manager, and workers need a food handler's card. ([source](https://www.monchd.org/services/food-and-retail-establishment-permits))
- **Fair, festival or one-time event:** a temporary food permit, applied for at least seven days ahead, good for up to 14 days. The fiscal year 2026-2027 fee schedule lists $72. ([source](https://www.monchd.org/services/temporary-food-worker-cards))
- **Farmers market:** a farmers market permit for foods that must be kept hot or cold, or cooked at the market. Produce and packaged foods that do not need temperature control do not need it. ([source](https://www.monchd.org/services/farmers-market-vendor-inspections))

This answers findability question q11 ("How do I get a permit to sell food at a farmers market or event?").
