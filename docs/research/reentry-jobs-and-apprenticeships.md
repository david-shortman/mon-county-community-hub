# Jobs after jail or prison, and getting into an apprenticeship

Last checked: 2026-10-30
For issue #215. First pass; this issue stays open.

## What the site already has

- **Federal Bonding Program, WV** (`federal-bonding-wv`): free bonds that protect employers who hire people with records.
- **Jobs and Hope WV** (`jobs-and-hope-wv`): job training and support for people in recovery.
- **WorkForce WV Morgantown office** (`workforce-wv-morgantown-office`): the local American Job Center.
- **Registered apprenticeship in WV** (`apprenticeships-wv`): how to find a registered program.

## Added in this pass

- **Step Up for Women Construction** by West Virginia Women Work: a free 12-week construction pre-apprenticeship. Open to anyone 18 or older ("created with women in mind"). No experience needed. Classes Monday to Thursday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., in Fairmont (205 10th Street) or Dunbar. No rides, but mileage is paid back for in-person days when funds allow. [Source](https://westvirginiawomenwork.org/step-up-construction/). Entry: `wvww-step-up-construction`. Fairmont is the closest site to Mon County.

- Pierpont Community and Technical College programs offered in Morgantown.

## Pass 3, November 14, 2026 (sprint wave 16, #529)

What was still open: a local reentry program, union apprenticeship windows, Pierpont in Morgantown, and a needs candidate.

### Added

- **DCR Reentry Planner** (`wvdcr-reentry-planner`), on `/reentry/` under "Work and your record". A free 128-page workbook from the Division of Corrections and Rehabilitation, linked from its [offender services page](https://dcr.wv.gov/services/offenderservices/Pages/default.aspx). Its Employment chapter (printed pages 76 to 93) covers job applications and how to talk about a record in an interview. The PDF was made in June 2024 and was still linked on 2026-11-14. The same page links a "Jails Reentry Handbook" PDF, but its text could not be read (it is images), so it was not added.
- **Step Up** (`wvww-step-up-construction`) now shows on `/find-a-job/` under short trainings. Re-read 2026-11-14: taking applications for fall 2026 classes; sites in Fairmont and Dunbar; Monday to Thursday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.; free; 18 or older; mileage paid back when funds allow. ([source](https://westvirginiawomenwork.org/step-up-construction/))
