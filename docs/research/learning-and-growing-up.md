## 1. Adult reading, math and computer skills

Already on the site, from earlier checks:

- **MTEC adult education** (`mtec-adult-education-esl`): high school equivalency, reading and math, and English classes.
- **Literacy Volunteers of Monongalia and Preston Counties** (`lvmpc-adult-literacy`): free one-on-one tutoring.
- **MTEC evening classes** (`mtec-adult-evening-classes`): paid classes, including computers.
- **WorkForce WV Morgantown office** (`workforce-wv-morgantown-office`).

## 2. Youth programs active today

- **4-H, WVU Extension Monongalia County.** No fee to join; enrollment opens October 1 and stays open all year; Cloverbuds for ages 5 to 7, clubs up to age 21. The page lists 2027 county camps (Cloverbud Day Camp June 1 to 3, 2027, and two residential camps in June 2027). Phone 304-291-7201. [Source](https://extension.wvu.edu/monongalia/4h). Entry: `mon-county-4h`.
- **The Shack Neighborhood House** (`the-shack-neighborhood-house`): before, after and out of school care, summer camp, teen programs. [Source](https://the-shack.org/).
- **SteppingStones** (`steppingstones`): recreation for children and adults with disabilities at Mylan Park.
- **BOPARC** (`boparc`): city parks, pools, ice arena and community centers.

The landscape map says Big Brothers Big Sisters of North Central WV dissolved in 2014 and the Mountaineer Boys and Girls Club has no recent public filing. Those were not re-checked here; do not list them as active without a current source.

## 3. Child care: finding a slot and paying for it

- **Child Care Resource Center, Morgantown** (run by Catholic Charities WV), 20 Scott Avenue, Suite 302, 304-292-7357, helps families find care and apply for help paying. It is one of six child care resource and referral agencies in the state. [Source](https://ccrcwv.org/). Entries are in separate PRs because child care help is a money topic and waits for David.
- The state's child care assistance rules: [WV Bureau for Family Assistance](https://bfa.wv.gov/bfa-programs/child-care-assistance).

## 4. Early childhood: one path by age

The pieces are on four agencies' pages. In age order:

| Age | Program | Entry |
|---|---|---|
| Pregnancy to 3 to 5 | **WV Home Visitation Program**: a trained visitor comes to the home. The state's Mon County page lists Early Head Start, the HAPI Project, Right From The Start and Starting Points. 1-800-642-8522. [Source](https://www.wvdhhr.org/wvhomevisitation/counties/Monongalia.html) | `wv-home-visiting` |
| Birth to 3 | **WV Birth to Three** for children with a delay or at risk of one | `wv-birth-to-three-referral` |
| Birth to 5 | **Starting Points** family programs | `starting-points-family-programs` |
| 4 | **Pre-K and Head Start** through Monongalia County Schools | `mcs-prek-and-head-start` |
