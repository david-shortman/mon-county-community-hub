## Gaps: no public count found

- **Mosaic grants awarded per round.** The program page lists dates and rules but not awards.
- **Street trees planted per year, and a canopy goal or urban forest plan.** The Tree Board page names a tree inventory and planting grants from the WV Division of Forestry, but gives no counts. No canopy goal or urban forest plan was found.
- **Active adoption agreements.** Neither the city's Adopt-A-Street announcement nor the state's Adopt-A-Highway and Adopt-A-Stream pages publish counts for Monongalia County.
- **Morgantown 311 totals.** David's decision on #300 allows a few big-picture totals with credit to SeeClickFix, but the SeeClickFix API was not reachable from our sandbox (#86).
