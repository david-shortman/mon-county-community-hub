# Local grants and funders for Mon County nonprofits

Last checked: 2026-10-30
For issue #18.

This is a grant calendar and short funder profiles for nonprofits in Morgantown and Monongalia County. Facts come from each funder's own page or from public tax filings shown on ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer. Tax figures are "as filed" and can be one to two years old. We list organizations only, never officers or trustees.

Notes:
- "Charitable disbursements" is the 990-PF line ProPublica extracts. It can include more than grants.
- Arts Monongahela Inc is the name on the tax filing. The YCF arts page calls the Arts Council of Greater Morgantown "formerly Arts Mon," which supports but does not prove they are the same organization.
- The Mylan Charitable Foundation shows $0 total assets at the end of its June 2025 year. That suggests it is winding down, but its own notice of closing was not found.
- The McQuain trust, the Puskar foundation, the Mylan foundation and the Hott foundation have no website we could find. Applicants can only learn what they fund from their tax returns (Part XV of the 990-PF lists grants paid).
- 100+ People Who Care (Morgantown) has no separate tax record; members write checks straight to the chosen charity.

## Gaps and open questions

- **YCF 2027-28 deadline.** Only "August 2027" is published for the next round. No date yet.
- **Organizational Arts Grants 2027-28.** Only "April 2027" is published. No date yet.
- **100+ People Who Care 2027 meetings.** The events feed shows four 2026 meetings (last one October 26, 2026) and none for 2027.
- **WV Humanities Council minigrant deadline.** "To be determined."
- **WV arts grants.** Most programs show "Not Currently Open" with no reopening date. The page lists FY26 final reports due September 28, 2026, which has passed.
- **Arts Council of Greater Morgantown grants page** still describes 2023 Impact Grants and says "under construction." Its own current grants are not described there.
- **Women's Giving Circle website** is linked from YCF with a misspelled address (womensgivingcirlceofncwv.org). Not checked.
- **United Way Women United** and other component funds may give small grants; not checked.
- Grant amounts for McQuain, Puskar and Hott are on the tax return PDFs (who got what). Reading Part XV lists would show which local groups they fund. Not done in this pass.

## Needs register candidate

- **Need:** Small nonprofits cannot see what the area's biggest private funders give or how to approach them.
- **Evidence:** The Hazel Ruby McQuain Charitable Trust reported $123,237,075 in assets and $12,243,634 in charitable disbursements for 2025, and the Milan Puskar Foundation reported $47,865,740 in assets and $2,278,197 in disbursements for 2024 ([ProPublica](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/346899181), [ProPublica](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/550749674)). Neither has a website we could find. Together they disbursed more than the YCF and United Way take in each year as filed ($2,064,496 and $2,762,654).
- **Who works on it:** Your Community Foundation and United Way publish open grant rounds.
