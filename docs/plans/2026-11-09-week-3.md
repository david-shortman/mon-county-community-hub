## Risks and how we handle them

- **A wrong fact on a winter page.** The High stakes check, David's merge, the weekly spot check, and taking a fact down rather than guessing.
- **Pages stay invisible to search.** Nothing else moves the findability score; #89 stays at the top of David's list.
- **Claims pile up again.** Runs release their claims at the end (proposal 1, #1769); the retro checks the count every week.
- **David becomes the bottleneck.** One decision list a week, and fewer items routed to him.
- **Overspending usage.** Normal pace, no sprints, the Thursday check.
