# Week 2 plan: November 2 to 8, 2026

The goal for week 2: get the pages we already built in front of residents, and make every one of them right. Week 1 built about 120 pages, but findability stayed at 76 because none of them show up in search. This week is less about new pages and more about being found, being correct, and clearing David's inbox. Agents follow this plan; Duet ranks the queue from it; the November 9 retro checks it. Evidence: [week 1 retro](../process/retros/2026-11-02.md).

## What success looks like by November 8

1. **Search can see us.** Search engine accounts are set up and the sitemap is submitted (#89, David). The Sunday findability run counts at least one of our pages in a resident's search, for the first time.
2. **Level 2 is on the scoreboard.** Aggregate page views from the visit counter (#1384) appear in `docs/scoreboard.md`, counted per guide and never per person.
3. **Urgent needs meet the full bar.** At least 3 of the 8 urgent needs in the source benchmark meet the "dramatically better" bar (1 of 8 today): food this week (q02), a doctor without insurance (q05), and eviction help (q25) are the closest.
4. **High-stakes facts stay right.** The weekly spot check stays at 10 of 10. The `needs-verification` count goes down, not up (88 today).
5. **David's inbox shrinks.** Open `for:david` issues under 80 (110 today), with one weekly decision list (retro proposal 2).
6. **Claims are honest.** No `in-progress` issue sits for over 3 days without a PR (#1410).
7. **The pipeline runs itself.** The November 9 retro runs on schedule and merges its own PR (#1418 is merged).

## Budget and pace

Week 1 ran far above the normal pace in [budget.md](../operations/budget.md), with sprints of 100 to 280 merges a day. Week 2 runs at **normal pace**: three scheduled builds a day, one digestible chunk each, at most one `up-next` labeled at a time, lean Duet sweeps. No sprints unless David asks. The Thursday budget check can switch to lean pace.

## Risks and how we handle them

- **A wrong fact on a high-stakes page.** The High stakes check, David's merge, the weekly spot check, and `needs-verification` instead of guessing.
- **Pages stay invisible to search.** Nothing else moves the findability score; #89 comes first.
- **David becomes the bottleneck.** One weekly decision list, and agents send fewer items to `for:david`.
- **Scheduled runs fail silently.** #1418 (merged) and retro proposal 3: each run tests one write first and reports on #81 if it cannot.
- **Overspending usage.** Normal pace, no sprints, the Thursday check.
