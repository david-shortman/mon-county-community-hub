## Risks and how we handle them

- **Instant builds and review stay blocked.** The three daily scheduled builds still run, so work continues.
- **Agents collide.** Lanes, one file per log entry, and claim before building (see "Avoiding merge conflicts" in `docs/COORDINATION.md`).
- **Wrong facts on a page that matters.** Every page shows sources and dates, the review gate checks trust, and the 211 fallback (#47) gives people a human to call.
