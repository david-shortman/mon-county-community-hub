## 7. Process changes to propose

Each is its own PR for David, labeled `for:david` and `type:process`.

**1. A run that ends releases its claims (#1769).**
- Problem: claims outlive the runs that made them, so issues look taken when nobody is working on them.
- Evidence: 49 stale claims this week, all from sprint waves on November 2 and 3.
- Change: in pipeline stage 4, add "Before a run ends, it removes `in-progress` from every issue it claimed that has no open PR, with a one-line comment on what is left. Re-check and verification sweeps comment without claiming."
- How we will know (by November 23): fewer than 5 issues are `in-progress` for over 3 days with no PR.

**2. Whoever opens a build or Duet issue scores it (#1770).**
- Problem: scoring was left to Duet and has not happened since day one.
- Evidence: 0 of 69 new `for:code` and `for:duet` issues scored this week; 6 of about 120 open ones had a score last week.
- Change: in pipeline stage 2, the author of a `for:code` or `for:duet` issue posts the `Score:` comment when opening it. Duet checks and adjusts on its sweep instead of writing every score.
- How we will know (by November 23): at least 80% of new `for:code` and `for:duet` issues have a score within a day.

**3. The Monday retro writes the decision list for David (#1771).**
- Problem: the adopted weekly decision list was not written, and the `for:david` pile keeps growing.
- Evidence: no "Decisions for David" issue; open `for:david` went from 110 to 119; the oldest items are 12 days old.
- Change: move the list from Duet to the weekly retro, which already gathers every `for:david` item and its age. The retro opens the issue and links it in its summary.
- How we will know (by November 23): a list exists on November 16 and 23, and open `for:david` issues fall below 100.
