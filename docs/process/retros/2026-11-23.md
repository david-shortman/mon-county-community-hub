## 7. Process changes to propose

Last week's three proposals (#2050, #2051, #2052) are still open, so this week proposes only two, to keep David's load small.

**1. Takedowns skip the `for:david` label (#2092).**
- Problem: the rule says taking down a wrong item does not wait for David, but the High stakes check labels any change to a high-stakes page `for:david`, and `safe-merge.sh` refuses it.
- Evidence: #2054 removes an unconfirmed email from the storm page and has waited since November 16.
- Change: a PR that only removes or hides content, and says so with a `takedown` label, is not labeled `for:david` by the High stakes check. David checks it after it merges, as the rule already says. This changes `site/scripts/high-stakes.mjs`, so the PR describes it and David merges.
- How we will know (by December 7): every takedown PR merges within a day of opening.

**2. A short decision list, with the full list linked (#2093).**
- Problem: a 176-item list got no answers.
- Evidence: #2049 had no reply from David in a week.
- Change: the list opens with at most 10 items David can answer in 15 minutes, chosen by time-sensitivity, how many residents each one affects, and how much other work it unblocks. The full list stays below, folded inside a "details" section. Anything that waits over 30 days gets a recommended "close as not now", so the list does not grow forever.
- How we will know (by December 7): David answers at least 5 of the top 10 items in each of the next two lists.
