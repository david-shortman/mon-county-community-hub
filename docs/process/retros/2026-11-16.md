## 7. Process changes to propose

Each is its own PR for David, labeled `for:david` and `type:process`.

**1. A cap on open `for:david` PRs during a sprint (#2050).**
- Problem: sprints create high-stakes PRs much faster than David can review them.
- Evidence: 47 opened on November 14 and 15; 2 `for:david` PRs merged all week; 173 items now wait on David.
- Change: in pipeline stage 4, when 10 or more `for:david` PRs are open, runs stop starting new high-stakes work and take the next item that does not touch shelter, crisis, food, money or benefits. Takedowns still go first.
- How we will know (by November 30): open `for:david` PRs stay under 15, and the median `for:david` PR waits under 3 days.

**2. Time-sensitive PRs say their date in the title (#2051).**
- Problem: a fix that only matters before a date looks like every other PR.
- Evidence: the senior meal sign-up merged on its closing day (#1757); the Legal Aid closure fix (#1824) matters only until November 19.
- Change: in pipeline stage 6, a PR whose value ends on a date starts its title with "By Mon DD:". The weekly decision list puts these first, and Duet's morning brief names them every day until they merge or pass.
- How we will know (by November 30): no time-sensitive PR merges after its date or closes unmerged because the date passed.

**3. Scores become a check, not a rule (#2052).**
- Problem: two written rules did not get issues scored.
- Evidence: 0 of 69 new issues scored in week 2; 2 of 47 after #1770 in week 3.
- Change: add a "Score" field to the task issue form (`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/task.yml`), and have the Health check list `for:code` and `for:duet` issues older than a day with no score. Both are workflow or template edits, so this PR describes them and David merges; a build PR follows.
- How we will know (by November 30): at least 80% of new `for:code` and `for:duet` issues have a score within a day.
