# DMV does not list which services need an appointment

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while checking newcomer facts for #622 (sprint wave 10, #529).
- **What we found:** The DMV's appointment page says "Appointments are only available for certain types of transactions" but does not list them, and the Morgantown office page does not list the services it handles, such as out-of-state title and license transfers.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [DMV Morgantown office](https://dmv.wv.gov/offices/morgantown), [Make an appointment](https://dmv.wv.gov/make-appointment).
- **Why it matters:** New residents may not know if they can walk in to transfer an out-of-state license.
- **Who keeps it:** WV Division of Motor Vehicles.
- **Status:** open. Call 304-558-3900.
