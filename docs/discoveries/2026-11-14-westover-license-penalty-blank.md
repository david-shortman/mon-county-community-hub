# Westover's business license form leaves the late penalty amount blank

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while turning the City of Westover's business license form into a phone page for #393.
- **What we found:** the form says that failing to get a new license before August 1 makes a business subject to "a maximum penalty of per day after notification by the City Clerk." The amount is missing from the sentence. The New Businesses page that links the form does not give it either.
- **Evidence:** City of Westover, Business Licence Application (PDF dated 4/27/2023), https://www.westoverwv.org/_files/ugd/d2114f_50052056caff40fc91000a6134690e2d.pdf, read 2026-11-14; New Businesses page, https://www.westoverwv.org/new-businesses, read 2026-11-14.
- **Why it matters:** a business owner reading the form cannot tell what a late license could cost.
- **Who keeps it:** the City of Westover (City Clerk).
- **Status:** open. Our page `/westover-business-license/` quotes the sentence as printed and says the amount is not on the form.
