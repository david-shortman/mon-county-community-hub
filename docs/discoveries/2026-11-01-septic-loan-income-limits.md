# Two income charts for the county's septic repair loan

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, re-checking health, family and benefits listings (#529)
- **What we found:** The health department's Septic and Wells page shows an income chart for the Wastewater Revolving Loan Fund that starts at $23,107 a year for one person. The loan application PDF has a chart "Effective from July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027" that starts at $29,526 for one person.
- **Evidence:** https://www.monchd.org/services/septic-and-wells and the application PDF it links, https://cdn.sanity.io/files/95aan9ec/production/11bcda20239b36efab39cb61f8f143ddadb900b4.pdf, both read 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** A household reading the web page could think it earns too much to apply when the current chart says it qualifies. Our listing gives neither number.
- **Who keeps it:** Monongalia County Health Department and the Monongalia County Commission
- **Status:** open
