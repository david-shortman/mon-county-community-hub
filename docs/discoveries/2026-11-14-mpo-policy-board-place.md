# Transportation planning board: two places named for its meetings

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while adding the MPO Policy Board to `/have-your-say/` (sprint wave 16, ways to act, #529)
- **What we found:** The Morgantown Monongalia MPO's home page says "Policy Board Meetings are held at Monongalia County Commission Chambers at 243 High Street." Its Policy Board page says they "are held at the City Hall Chambers at 389 Spruce Street." The November 19, 2026 agenda lists the City of Morgantown, 389 Spruce Street.
- **Evidence:**
  - MPO home page, https://www.plantogether.org/, read 2026-11-14
  - MPO Policy Board page, https://www.plantogether.org/policy-board-1, read 2026-11-14
  - November 19, 2026 Policy Board agenda, https://www.plantogether.org/_files/ugd/613794_53dfa2c7bf454c4994b352de5f595966.pdf, read 2026-11-14
- **Why it matters:** Someone who wants to comment in person could go to the wrong building. Our card names no place and says each agenda gives the date, time and place.
- **Who keeps it:** the Morgantown Monongalia MPO
- **Status:** open
