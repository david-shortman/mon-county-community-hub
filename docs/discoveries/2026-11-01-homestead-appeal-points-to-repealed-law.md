# The homestead appeal law points to a repealed section for the circuit court step

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while building the after-you-apply page for the homestead exemption (#1221, #1223)
- **What we found:** W. Va. Code §11-6B-6(b) says the Assessor or the claimant may apply to circuit court to review a County Commission homestead decision "in the same manner as is provided for appeals from the county commission in section twenty-five, article three of this chapter." That section, §11-3-25, is shown as repealed. No other official page we read says how or by when to take that step.
- **Evidence:** https://code.wvlegislature.gov/11-6B-6/ and https://code.wvlegislature.gov/11-3-25/ ("[Repealed.]"), both read 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** A homeowner who loses a homestead appeal at the County Commission has no clear official statement of the next step or its deadline.
- **Who keeps it:** West Virginia Legislature
- **Status:** open
