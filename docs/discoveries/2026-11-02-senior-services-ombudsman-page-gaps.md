# The state ombudsman page links two brochures that are gone, and counts its regions two ways

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while building the nursing home and assisted living page for #222 (sprint wave 11, #529).
- **What we found:** The WV Bureau of Senior Services' Long-Term Care Ombudsman page links an Ombudsman brochure and a "How to Report Adult Abuse and Neglect" brochure. Both links returned "not found." The page says the program has nine regional ombudsmen, but the coverage list it links (updated October 4, 2024) shows eight regions, and Lewis County appears in two of them. No page we read says whether the ombudsman's help is free.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program](http://www.wvseniorservices.gov/StayingSafe/LongTermCareOmbudsmanProgram/tabid/81/Default.aspx) (links `/Portals/0/pdf/brochure-ombuds.pdf` and the adult abuse brochure, both not found), [coverage list](http://www.wvseniorservices.gov/LinkClick.aspx?fileticket=twbgtHAqKXc%3d&tabid=81).
- **Why it matters:** A family with a problem at a care home cannot read the brochures, and cannot be sure which regional office covers a county listed twice. Monongalia County is listed once, under the Morgantown office.
- **What we did:** `/choosing-a-care-home/` gives the statewide number from the program page and the Morgantown region from the coverage list, with its date. It does not say the program is free.
- **Who keeps it:** WV Bureau of Senior Services.
- **Status:** open. A person could check whether the brochures moved and whether a newer coverage list exists.
