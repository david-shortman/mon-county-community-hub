# BOPARC's sign-up page names one office; its home page says that office is open one day a week

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while moving the /parks/ office sentence into a fact record for #122.
- **What we found:** BOPARC's home page lists two customer service desks: the Wiles Hill Community Center (287 Eureka Drive) on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, and the Marilla office (705 East Brockway Avenue, upper Marilla Park) on Wednesday, both 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. with a lunch break from noon to 1 p.m. Its "How to register" page names only the Marilla office for signing up in person. Our /parks/ page had said Marilla on Monday, Wednesday and Friday and Wiles Hill on Tuesday and Thursday (read October 30, 2026), so the days changed in the last two weeks.
- **Evidence:** https://www.boparc.org/ and https://www.boparc.org/programs.html, both read 2026-11-14.
- **Why it matters:** a person who goes to the Marilla office to sign up on a Monday may find it closed.
- **Who keeps it:** BOPARC.
- **Status:** open. /parks/ now shows the home page's days (content/facts/parks/boparc-office.yaml). The program sign-up card (`content/resources/boparc-program-registration.yaml`) still follows the programs page; a person should re-read both pages at its next re-check.
