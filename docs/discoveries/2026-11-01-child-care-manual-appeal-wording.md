# The state child care manual gives the appeal deadline in different ways, and reuses form numbers

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while building the after-you-apply page for child care help (#1207, #1223)
- **What we found:** The WV DoHS Child Care Subsidy Policies and Procedures Manual (dated October 1, 2024) states the hearing deadline three ways. Section 11.3.2.1 gives applicants "13 days from the date of the notification letter." Section 11.3.2.3 lets families already getting care ask "up to 60 days after the action was taken." Section 6.6.1.2 says "within 60 days of the notification," and in the same sentence mentions requests made "after 13 days but within 30 days." The manual also sets no number of days to decide an application. Form numbers are reused: DAY-0176 is called both a "Parent Notification Letter" (§2.4.3.2) and the "Child Care Certificate" (§6.1.2.3), and §2.4.2.4 calls the certificate DAY-0167.
- **Evidence:** https://bfa.wv.gov/media/1461/download?inline (linked as "Child Care Assistance Policy and Procedure Manual" from https://bfa.wv.gov/bfa-policy-and-plans/child-care-policy), read 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** A parent reading the manual can't tell whether 60 days counts from the letter or from the date care stops, and can't match a letter to its form number.
- **Who keeps it:** WV Department of Human Services, Division of Early Care and Education
- **Status:** open
