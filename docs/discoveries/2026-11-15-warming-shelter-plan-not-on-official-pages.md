# This winter's warming shelter plan is not on any current official page

- **Found:** 2026-11-15, while re-checking warming shelter sources for #219 (sprint wave, #529).
- **What we found:**
  - **No official announcement.** As of the morning of November 15, 2026, no official page we could read gives an opening date, hours or rules for the 2026 to 2027 Morgantown warming shelter. Catholic Charities West Virginia (the planned operator) has no post about it. Its newest news post is from October 28, 2026, about a benefits enrollment center. In 2024 it did post the opening on its news page ("Winter Shelter Opens Early", December 12, 2024).
  - **A target date only appears in City Council minutes.** The draft minutes of the August 25, 2026 City Council regular meeting (in the September 1 agenda packet) say the Mayor "shared that the MCR met recently to discuss hosting a warming shelter at Hazel's House, pending adequate funding from Catholic Charities, private donors, and the Mon County Commission, with an anticipated opening on November 15." No later official page we found confirms or changes that date.
  - **The building's own warming shelter page describes a past season.** Hazel's House of Hope's page "Warming Shelter" says the shelter "opened for the season Thursday, Dec. 1" and "will be open until March 31, 2023." It does not say it is about the 2022 to 2023 season until the end date. A second address, hazelshouseofhope.org, shows only a blank WordPress "Hello world!" post.
  - **The county's funding decision is not in the public record we could read.** News reports say Catholic Charities asked the Monongalia County Commission for funding at its regular meeting in the week before August 24, and that a work session would follow. The links to the August 5, 12, 19 and 26, 2026 regular meeting agendas on the Commission's meeting page answer "404 Not Found". The 26 agendas that did open (August work sessions and special meetings, and every agenda from September 2 to November 18) do not mention the warming shelter or Catholic Charities. The meeting page lists no 2026 minutes.
  - **211's search could not be read automatically.** search.wv211.org loads its results by script, so a person needs to search it in a browser.

- **Why it matters:** People who sleep outside, and the people helping them, need to know when and where the warming shelter opens as the nights get cold. Right now the only dated plan is in a line of council minutes, and the building's own page shows a season that ended in 2023.
- **Who keeps it:** Catholic Charities West Virginia (operator), Morgantown Community Resources (Hazel's House of Hope), the City of Morgantown and the Monongalia County Commission (funders and their meeting records), and West Virginia 211.
- **Status:** open. The site says "not announced" with today's date on both /tonight/ and /stay-tonight/. The human follow-up is in the needs-verification issue linked from the #219 PR.
