# WVCHIP's income table stops at 7 people but its note starts at "over 8"

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while adding WVCHIP income limits to `/no-insurance/` for #1534.
- **What we found:** WVCHIP's eligibility page lists monthly and yearly income limits for family sizes 2 through 7, then says "Add $3,400 (annually) for each additional member over 8." There is no row for a family of 8, so a family of 8 or more cannot work out its limit from the page. The table also has no row for a family of 1, and the page does not say which year's poverty levels it uses.
- **Evidence:** https://chip.wv.gov/eligibility, read 2026-11-14.
- **Why it matters:** a large family may rule itself out, or not know whether to apply.
- **Who keeps it:** West Virginia Children's Health Insurance Program (WVCHIP).
- **Status:** open. Our page shows sizes 2 to 7 as given and tells bigger families to call the WVCHIP helpline.
