# The pages that listed the WVDOH road problem email are gone

- **Found:** 2026-11-16, while checking `wvroads@wv.gov` on /after-a-storm/ for #2047.
- **What we found:**
  - The WV Department of Transportation's Contact Us page lists "(833) WV ROADS (833-987-6237)", a general email (dot.info@wv.gov) and a "Submit a Roadwork Request" link. It does not list `wvroads@wv.gov`.
  - Search results still describe `wvroads@wv.gov` as the road repair email, pointing to a WVDOT FAQ page and the Governor's Road Maintenance page. Both now answer 404 (https://transportation.wv.gov/about/faqs and https://governor.wv.gov/constituent-services/Pages/Road-Maintenance.aspx). One other FAQ address redirects to a 404 page.
- **Evidence:** https://transportation.wv.gov/contact-us and the two 404 addresses above, all read 2026-11-16.
- **Why it matters:** after a storm, someone with a tree or a slip on a state road may find the old email in search results and not know whether anyone reads it.
- **Who keeps it:** West Virginia Department of Transportation, Division of Highways.
- **Status:** open. /after-a-storm/ now gives only the 1-833-WVROADS number (#2047).
