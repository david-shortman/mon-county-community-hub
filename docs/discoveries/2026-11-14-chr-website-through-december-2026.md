# County Health Rankings says its website stays up only through December 2026

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while re-checking the needs register's sources (#988, sprint wave 17, #529)
- **What we found:** The County Health Rankings & Roadmaps program FAQ says "The CHR&R website will stay up through December 2026" and that the timeline "may be extended" if new funding is found. It asks users to download datasets and technical documents "before the end of the year". A later blog post (October 20, 2026) says new funding means county-level data "will remain available and continue to be updated", but it does not say at what address. The two pages do not say the same thing about where the files will be in 2027.
- **Evidence:** https://www.countyhealthrankings.org/findings-and-insights/blog/faq-chrr-program-status-and-funding (read 2026-11-14), https://www.countyhealthrankings.org/findings-and-insights/blog/uwphi-secures-new-multi-year-funding-to-evolve-chrr-beyond-2026 (read 2026-11-14). Earlier entry: `2026-11-01-chr-no-2026-release.md`.
- **Why it matters:** CHR&R is the only free source that puts many county health numbers in one file. Local reports, both hospitals' needs assessments and our own needs register (eight needs) and How is Mon County doing? page link to its files. If the site goes down, those links stop working and the numbers become hard to check.
- **Who keeps it:** University of Wisconsin Population Health Institute (County Health Rankings & Roadmaps)
- **Status:** open
