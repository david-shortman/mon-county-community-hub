# Two state pages give the Morgantown-area WorkForce office different towns and ZIP codes

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while re-checking #379 (verification sweep 4, #529)
- **What we found:** WorkForce West Virginia's office list gives "40 Commerce Drive Suite 200 Westover, WV 26501." The job center contact list from the same agency gives "40 Commerce Drive ... Morgantown, WV 26505." The phone 681-404-6377 on that list is also listed on the Region VI workforce board's page as one contact line shared by the Clarksburg, Elkins and Morgantown job centers, so it is not a local Morgantown line. No official page we found gives walk-in hours.
- **Evidence:** https://workforcewv.org/about-workforce-wv/local-office-locations/ (read 2026-11-01); https://regionviwv.org/american-job-centers/locations-and-contacts/ (read 2026-11-01).
- **Why it matters:** Job seekers using a map or a ZIP search may not be sure they have the right office, and there is no local number or hours to check before going.
- **Who keeps it:** WorkForce West Virginia and the Region VI Workforce Development Board.
- **Status:** open
