# No Narcotics Anonymous page names Mon County

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while adding support meetings to Help with drugs or alcohol for #1764.
- **What we found:**
  - Alcoholics Anonymous has a clear path: A.A. Area 73's Meetings page names District 16 for "Monongalia, Preston, Taylor, Tucker," and District 16 posts its own meeting lists.
  - For Narcotics Anonymous, the Mountaineer Region's site says it covers "Central and Southern West Virginia, and part of the Mid-Ohio Valley." We found no NA region or area page that names Monongalia County or Morgantown.
- **Evidence:** https://www.aawv.org/meetings and https://www.district16wv.org/, read 2026-11-14; https://www.mrscna.org/, read 2026-11-14.
- **Why it matters:** a person in Mon County looking for an NA meeting cannot tell from these pages which NA area or helpline serves them, so the site names A.A. and Al-Anon only.
- **Who keeps it:** Narcotics Anonymous service bodies (the region or area that serves north central West Virginia).
- **Status:** open. A person could find which NA area serves Morgantown, from an NA page. Related: #1764, #1836.
