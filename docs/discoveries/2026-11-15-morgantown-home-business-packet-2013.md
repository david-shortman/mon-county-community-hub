# Morgantown's only home business guide is a 2013 scan

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while researching home businesses for #1985 (sprint wave 20, #529).
- **What we found:** the City of Morgantown's Steps to Starting Your Business page links a Home Occupation Application Packet, the only city document we found that explains whether a business at home needs approval. The packet is a scanned image (no searchable text), dated September 30, 2013 in its file details. No city web page explains home businesses in its own words, and the packet does not say whether its rules still match the current Planning and Zoning Code.
- **Evidence:** Steps to Starting Your Business, https://www.morgantownwv.gov/247/Steps-to-Starting-Your-Business, read 2026-11-14; Home Occupation Application Packet, https://www.morgantownwv.gov/DocumentCenter/View/314/Home-Occupation-Application-Packet-PDF, read 2026-11-14 (PDF creation date 2013-09-30, "PFU ScanSnap Manager").
- **Why it matters:** people starting a business at home cannot search or copy the rules, screen readers cannot read a scanned image, and a reader cannot tell if the rules are current.
- **Who keeps it:** the City of Morgantown Planning Division.
- **Status:** open. Our page `/start-a-business/#from-home` uses the packet, says it is from 2013, and points to the Planning Division.
