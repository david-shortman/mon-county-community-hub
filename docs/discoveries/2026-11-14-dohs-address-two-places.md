# The county DoHS office address maps to two places

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, measuring distances for the Nearest help box on town pages (#1814)
- **What we found:** The state lists the Monongalia County DoHS office at "114 S. High Street, Morgantown, WV 26507". 26507 is the ZIP code of its mailing address (PO Box 800). The Census Geocoder matches the street address to two points about 0.2 miles apart: "114 HIGH ST, MORGANTOWN, WV, 26505" and "114 S HIGH ST, MORGANTOWN, WV, 26501".
- **Evidence:** https://dohs.wv.gov/offices/monongalia-county-dohs-office, read 2026-11-14. Census Geocoder (geocoding.geo.census.gov, benchmark Public_AR_Current), searched 2026-11-14 for "114 S High St, Morgantown, WV 26505".
- **Why it matters:** Map apps may send a first-time visitor to the wrong block. For our distances it does not matter, since every rounded distance is the same from either point.
- **Who keeps it:** WV Department of Human Services
- **Status:** open
