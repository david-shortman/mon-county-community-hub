# Two CMS directory pages give different phone numbers for WV SHIP

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while building the after-you-apply page for Medicare Savings Programs and Extra Help (#529)
- **What we found:** CMS's contact directory has two entries for the West Virginia State Health Insurance Assistance Program. One lists toll free (877) 987-4463. The other lists toll free (877) 987-3646 and local (304) 348-0707.
- **Evidence:** https://www.cms.gov/about-cms/contact/directory/west-virginia-state-health-insurance-assistance-program-wv-ship/1562251 ("Toll Free: (877) 987-4463"), and https://www.cms.gov/contacts/west-virginia-state-health-insurance-assistance-program-wv-ship/general-beneficiary-contact/1554016 ("Toll Free: (877) 987-3646"), both read 2026-11-01. The program's own site, wvship.org, did not load on its secure address the same day (see `2026-11-01-wv-ship-site-down.md`), so it could not settle which is current.
- **Why it matters:** People looking for free Medicare counseling may call a number that is out of date. Our site uses (877) 987-4463, from the first page.
- **Who keeps it:** CMS (the directory) and the West Virginia Bureau of Senior Services (WV SHIP)
- **Status:** open
