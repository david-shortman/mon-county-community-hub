# County Health Rankings put its 2026 numbers in a separate file, with no annual release

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while re-checking the needs register's sources (#988)
- **What we found:** County Health Rankings & Roadmaps did not publish a 2026 annual release. It refreshed 34 measures in March 2026 in a separate "supplemental release" file. The main 2025 data file, which most users and our own How we're doing page read, was last changed November 19, 2025, so it still shows the older numbers. For Mon County, food insecurity is 12.6% in the main file and 14% in the supplement. The program's Robert Wood Johnson Foundation funding ends in December 2026; county data is to "remain available and continue to be updated" under new funding, but the program will change.
- **Evidence:** https://www.countyhealthrankings.org/findings-and-insights/blog/chrr-updates-data-for-34-measures-will-not-produce-a-2026-annual-data-release (March 30, 2026), https://www.countyhealthrankings.org/findings-and-insights/blog/uwphi-secures-new-multi-year-funding-to-evolve-chrr-beyond-2026 (October 20, 2026), https://www.countyhealthrankings.org/health-data/methodology-and-sources/rankings-data-documentation, all read 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** Local reports that pull from the main 2025 file, including both hospitals' needs assessments in future cycles, can quote numbers that a newer official file has replaced. Links to these files may change after December 2026.
- **Who keeps it:** University of Wisconsin Population Health Institute (County Health Rankings & Roadmaps)
- **Status:** open
