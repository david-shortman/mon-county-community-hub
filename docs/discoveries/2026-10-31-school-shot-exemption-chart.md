# The posted school shot chart and a 2025 court ruling disagree on exemptions

- **Found:** 2026-10-30, building School shots and sports physicals (#388)
- **What we found:** The state chart for new school enterers that Monongalia County Schools posts (revised 04/22) says state law "does not allow for non-medical exemptions." The Governor's office says a November 2025 court order requires religious exemptions to be allowed. No current official page says which applies now.
- **Evidence:** https://5il.co/2ng37 (county schools' link to the state chart), https://governor.wv.gov/article/governor-morrisey-applauds-major-legal-win-vaccine-exemption-case, both read 2026-10-30.
- **Why it matters:** Families get two different official answers.
- **Who keeps it:** WV Department of Health; WV Department of Education; Monongalia County Schools
- **Status:** open (#387)
