# No official page says how court-ordered community service works in Mon County

- **Found:** 2026-11-17, while researching the search "community service hours" for #2048.
- **What we found:** State law (WV Code §62-11A-1a) lets a court order community service with "government entities or charitable or nonprofit entities approved by the circuit court," and counts eight hours as one day of a jail sentence. No Monongalia County court, probation, county or city page we could read says how a person finds an approved place, who approves it, or how hours are reported. The Judiciary's Monongalia probation page gives only the office address and phone.
- **Evidence:** https://code.wvlegislature.gov/62-11A-1A/, https://www.courtswv.gov/node/32716, https://dcr.wv.gov/facilities/Pages/youth-reporting-centers/Monongalia-County.aspx, all read 2026-11-17.
- **Why it matters:** a person told to do hours has a deadline and a court date. Without a public page, the only way to learn the steps is to call.
- **Who keeps it:** Monongalia County Circuit Court probation office (WV Judiciary), and the courts that order the hours.
