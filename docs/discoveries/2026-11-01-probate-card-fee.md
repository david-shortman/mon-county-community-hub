# County Clerk probate pages give two different card fees

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, re-checking the probate listing (lane B re-check, #529)
- **What we found:** The County Clerk's page on opening an estate with a will says paying by card adds a $2 fee. The Clerk's Small Estate Affidavit form says card payments add 2.4%, with a $1 minimum.
- **Evidence:** https://www.monongaliacountyclerk.com/index.php/fiduciary-probate/16-general-info/108-decedent-had-a-will and https://www.monongaliacountyclerk.com/myfiles/probate_office/Small_Estate_Affidavit_Form_and_Instructions_Intestate_(No_Will).pdf, both read 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** Families settling an estate may bring the wrong amount. The two fees may apply to different filings, but neither page says so.
- **Who keeps it:** Monongalia County Clerk, Probate Office
- **Status:** open
