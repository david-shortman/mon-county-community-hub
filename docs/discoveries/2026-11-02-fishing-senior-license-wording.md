# Fishing rules summary gives a birth date that does not match its senior age rule

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while checking fishing license ages for #630 (sprint wave 10, #529).
- **What we found:** The 2026 fishing regulations summary says the Senior license (Class XS) is "Required for resident hunters and anglers who have reached 65 years of age on or after January 1, 2012 (those born before Jan. 1, 1947)." Someone who turned 65 on or after January 1, 2012 was born on or after January 1, 1947, so the note in parentheses does not match. The same page uses "born before Jan. 1, 1947" correctly for people who do not need a license.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [2026 Fishing Regulations Summary, page 28](https://wvdnr.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Pub_Regs_Fishing_2026_DNR_WILD_pp.pdf).
- **Why it matters:** An older angler could misread which license applies.
- **Who keeps it:** WV Division of Natural Resources.
- **Status:** open. Our listing gives the age rule without the birth date note (#1494).
