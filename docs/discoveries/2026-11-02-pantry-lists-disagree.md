# Public pantry lists disagree on Scott's Run and St. Ursula's details

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, during the monthly source benchmark in the weekly findability run ([run file](../../content/findability/benchmark/2026-11-01.yaml), need q02 "food pantry this week").
- **What we found:** Two widely shared resource lists give food pantry details that do not match each other or the provider's own page.
  - Scott's Run Settlement House: WVU Graduate Education's community resources page lists 41 Lady Bug Lane, Osage, 304-599-5020, Monday to Friday 8:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. Scott's Run's own pantry page gives 304-435-5098 and says pantry pickup is by appointment. The city's PATHWAYS to Help page lists weekday hours and does not say the pantry is by appointment.
  - St. Ursula's food pantry: the city's PATHWAYS to Help page lists the 1st, 2nd and 3rd Wednesday. The WVU Graduate Education page lists the 1st and 3rd Wednesday. We found no page of St. Ursula's own to settle it.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-01: [WVU Graduate Education community resources](https://graduateeducation.wvu.edu/resources/community-resources) (marked last updated August 1, 2024), [City of Morgantown PATHWAYS to Help](https://www.morgantownwv.gov/708/PATHWAYS-to-Help) (no date shown), [Scott's Run food pantry](https://www.srsh.org/food-pantry).
- **Why it matters:** a resident who calls the old number or walks in without an appointment may not get food that day. Students and residents are often handed these two lists first.
- **Who keeps it:** WVU Graduate Education (its page), the City of Morgantown (PATHWAYS to Help), Scott's Run Settlement House and St. Ursula's (their own details).
- **Status:** open. Our pantry details follow the provider's own page; open pantry hour questions are tracked in #932.
