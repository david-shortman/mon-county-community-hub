# A hospital page lists a Morgantown grief center whose website is parked

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while building the grief page for #222 (sprint wave 11, #529).
- **What we found:** Mon Health's Support and Counseling page lists the West Virginia Family Grief Center, which it says "helps children and families cope with the death of a loved one," with a phone number and a link to wvfgc.org. That address shows a parked "Coming Soon" page. No other page of the center's own could be read. We also found no official page for an in-person grief group that meets in Monongalia County; the nearest open group we could confirm meets in Bridgeport.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [Mon Health Support and Counseling](https://www.monhealth.com/main/support-counseling), [wvfgc.org](http://www.wvfgc.org/) ("Coming Soon").
- **Why it matters:** A family looking for grief help for a child finds a listing they cannot check, and local grief groups are hard to find on any official page.
- **What we did:** `/grief/` leaves the center off and lists the groups we could confirm. Needs-verification issue #1558 asks a person to call.
- **Who keeps it:** the West Virginia Family Grief Center; Mon Health for its listing.
- **Status:** open.
