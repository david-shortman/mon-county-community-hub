# Mountain Line's pages describe the reduced fare two ways

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while re-checking #559 and #577 (verification sweep 4, #529)
- **What we found:** Mountain Line's accessibility page says the fare for people with disabilities, seniors and Medicare cardholders "is half the regular fare." Its persons with disabilities page says "$0.35 cents on all regularly priced $0.75 cent fares." Neither page says what the reduced fare is on routes that cost more than $0.75.
- **Evidence:** https://www.busride.org/accessibility and https://www.busride.org/persons-with-disabilities, both read 2026-11-01. Related question: #983.
- **Why it matters:** Riders cannot tell the exact reduced fare for every route.
- **Who keeps it:** Mountain Line Transit Authority.
- **Status:** open
