# Small business coaching: no county map, and two numbers for one coach

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while checking small business facts for #638 (sprint wave 10, #529).
- **What we found:** The WV SBDC site has no map of which coach serves which county; its Locations link goes to a national locator. Its team page lists the Northern Region coaches with phone numbers, and the Morgantown Area Partnership's business resources page gives a different phone number for the same downtown coach.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [WV SBDC team](https://wvsbdc.com/about-wv-sbdc/our-team/), [Morgantown Area Partnership business resources](https://morgantownpartnership.com/chamber-of-commerce/business-resources/).
- **Why it matters:** A new business owner may not know whom to call first.
- **Who keeps it:** WV Small Business Development Center and the Morgantown Area Partnership.
- **Status:** open. Our listing names the offices, not individual coaches.
