# No state page tells college students which address to register at

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while checking college student facts for #598 (sprint wave 10, #529).
- **What we found:** The Secretary of State's elections FAQ says "you must have the address where you live on your voter registration" and lists a WV college photo ID as accepted ID, but no page we found says whether an out-of-state student can register at a dorm or off-campus address.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [SOS elections FAQ](https://sos.wv.gov/elections/voter-information/elections-frequently-asked-questions-faqs), [Be registered and ready](https://sos.wv.gov/be-registered-and-ready).
- **Why it matters:** Many WVU students may not know if they can vote here.
- **Who keeps it:** WV Secretary of State, Elections Division.
- **Status:** open. A call to SOS Elections, 304-558-6000, is on the call list.
