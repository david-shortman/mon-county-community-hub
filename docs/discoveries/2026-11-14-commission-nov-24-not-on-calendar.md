# County calendar does not show the Commission's move from Nov 25 to Nov 24

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while looking for County Commission agendas from November 16 to December 6 (sprint wave 18, legislators and boards lane, #529)
- **What we found:** The County Clerk's draft minutes of the November 10, 2026 Commission meeting record a motion, "unanimously carried", "to approve moving the Monongalia County Commission Meeting scheduled for Wednesday, November 25, 2026, to Tuesday, November 24, 2026." The County's online calendar still has only its standing entry, "Commission Meeting" every Wednesday at 10 a.m., with no change for that week. The Commission's Meeting Schedule and Agendas page had no agenda for either date yet; its newest agenda was for November 18.
- **Evidence:**
  - Draft minutes, read 2026-11-14 (Eastern): https://monongaliacountyclerk.com/myfiles/drafts/draft.pdf (item 9b), listed at https://monongaliacountyclerk.com/index.php/21-additional-info/48-commission-meetings-minutes-draft
  - County calendar data, read 2026-11-14: the calendar on https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/calendar.php lists "Commission Meeting" as a weekly Wednesday event starting 2025-06-18, with no exception for November 25, 2026, and no event on November 24
  - Meeting Schedule and Agendas, read 2026-11-14: https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/commission/meeting_schedule/index.php (newest agenda: November 18, 2026)
- **Why it matters:** Someone who checks the County calendar would expect a meeting on Wednesday, November 25. The minutes say it moves to Tuesday, November 24. The minutes are still a draft.
- **Who keeps it:** Monongalia County Commission (calendar and agendas); Monongalia County Clerk (minutes).
- **Status:** open (#1943). The site does not state the new date until the agenda for that week is posted on the Commission's page. When it posts, the daily Agenda guides check opens an issue for it, and the guide uses the date on the agenda.
