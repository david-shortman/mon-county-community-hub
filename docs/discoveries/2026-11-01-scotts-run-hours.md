# Scott's Run Settlement House pages give three different sets of hours

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, re-checking health, family and benefits listings (#529)
- **What we found:** The home page lists Fairchance Road open Monday to Wednesday and Lady Bug Drive open Thursday and Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. The Free and Fresh Market page lists 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. The food pantry and baby pantry pages list Fairchance Road Monday to Friday and Lady Bug Drive Thursday, 8:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m.
- **Evidence:** https://www.srsh.org/, https://www.srsh.org/free-market, https://www.srsh.org/food-pantry, https://www.srsh.org/baby-pantry, all read 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** People coming for food or other help can't tell when the doors are open.
- **Who keeps it:** Scott's Run Settlement House
- **Status:** open
