# WVDA's 2026 cottage food rule states its label section two ways

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, verification sweep 3 (#1232, #529)
- **What we found:** The emergency rule 61CSR42 (filed with the Secretary of State on June 1, 2026, https://agriculture.wv.gov/wp-content/uploads/Filed-Cottage-Food-Rule-Emergency-Rule-2026.pdf) says in section 6.1 that potentially hazardous cottage food products "shall carry product labels, regardless of whether the product is potentially hazardous or non-potentially hazardous." Section 9.4 says the sale of non-potentially hazardous food done under W. Va. Code §19-35-6 "is not subject to the provisions of this article." A reader cannot tell from the rule alone whether its label list applies to baked goods and other non-potentially hazardous food. §19-35-6(c) says only that such food must follow "the department's labeling standards."
- **Why it matters:** Home bakers selling at markets may not know which label items they need. The site gives the rule's list only for potentially hazardous food.
- **Who keeps it:** West Virginia Department of Agriculture
- **Status:** open
