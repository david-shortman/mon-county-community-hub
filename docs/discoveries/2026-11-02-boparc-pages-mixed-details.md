# BOPARC pages give two ZIP codes and mixed years for pools

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while checking park and pool facts for #630 (sprint wave 10, #529).
- **What we found:** BOPARC's White Park page gives 1001 Mississippi Street, 26501, and its ice arena page gives the same street with 26505. The pools page says pools are closed "for the 2026 Season," shows "2027" rate headings with no prices, and says "Reservations for 2026 pool rentals can be made beginning April 5th, 2027."
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [White Park](https://www.boparc.org/white-park.html), [Ice arena](https://www.boparc.org/boparc-sports-complex-ice-arena-indoor-turf.html), [Pools](https://www.boparc.org/pools.html).
- **Why it matters:** A wrong ZIP can send a map app astray, and pool families cannot plan for 2027 yet.
- **Who keeps it:** BOPARC (Morgantown Board of Parks and Recreation Commissioners).
- **Status:** open. Our listings leave the ZIP and 2027 pool prices off.
