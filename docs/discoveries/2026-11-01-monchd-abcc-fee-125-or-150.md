# Health department lists two amounts for the liquor license add-on

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while turning the Food Establishment Plan Review Application into a phone page (part of #393)
- **What we found:** The Monongalia County Health Department's Food Establishment Plan Review Application (no printed date; file saved January 15, 2026) has a fee table with a "Liquor License" column of $150 for every size, and a footnote that says "For facilities with an ABCC license, add $125 to the Permit Fee." The department's Permit Fees and Fees for Service form for fiscal year 2026-2027 says "ABCC Lic. Add $150".
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-01: https://cdn.sanity.io/files/95aan9ec/production/314943135511dce2b9e78860326f3f7924614741.pdf (page 1), https://cdn.sanity.io/files/95aan9ec/production/9af55503d7848dd4ca2ca580602065966a1d1bcf.pdf, both linked from https://www.monchd.org/departments-programs/environmental-health
- **Why it matters:** A restaurant or bar owner budgeting for a permit cannot tell from the papers whether the add-on is $125 or $150.
- **Who keeps it:** Monongalia County Health Department, Environmental Health
- **Status:** open (#1271)
