# Two county shelter pages list different adoption fees

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, re-checking pet listings (lane B re-check, #529)
- **What we found:** The county's adoption center fees page lists $125 for a dog and $85 for a cat. The shelter's own adoption website lists $150 for a dog and $100 for a cat.
- **Evidence:** https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/departments/canine_adoption_center/fees.php and https://monongaliawv.animalservices.website/shelterpronet/home.spn, both read 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** People planning to adopt can't tell what they will pay. Our listing leaves the amounts off for now.
- **Who keeps it:** Monongalia County Canine Adoption Center
- **Status:** open
