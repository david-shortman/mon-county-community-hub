# No public count or timing for state road repair requests

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while writing the street problems research note (#86)
- **What we found:** The WV Division of Highways takes road repair requests through its SWAT form and district offices, but we found no public page that shows how many requests come in for Monongalia County, how many are closed, or how long they take. The form itself names no response time.
- **Evidence:** https://webapps.transportation.wv.gov/swat/ ("Requests are handled Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 4:00 PM"; no timing or tracking shown), https://transportation.wv.gov/agencies/highways/districts/district-four (contact details and work zones only), both read 2026-11-01. The same page lists 34,940 of the state's 38,844 public road miles as state owned (https://transportation.wv.gov/agencies/highways, read 2026-11-01), so most roads outside towns fall under this process.
- **Why it matters:** Residents outside Morgantown city limits mostly report to the state, and cannot see what happens to a request or how long repairs usually take. Morgantown 311 requests, by contrast, are public.
- **Who keeps it:** WV Department of Transportation, Division of Highways
- **Status:** open
