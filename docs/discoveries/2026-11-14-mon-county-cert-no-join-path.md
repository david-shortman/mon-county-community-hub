# Mon County CERT: named on the health department's page, with no way to join

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while looking for volunteer programs run by public offices for `/volunteer/` (sprint wave 16, ways to act, #529)
- **What we found:** The Monongalia County Health Department's Threat Preparedness page says the program offers training in "Community Emergency Response Team (CERT)" and lists "Mon County Community Response Team (CERT)" under Resources, but that line is plain text with no link, and the page does not say how a resident joins, when training runs, or who to contact. The CERT link in the same paragraph goes to FEMA's national page. The state's ReadyWV CERT directory lists a Monongalia County CERT contact at the health department, with the phone 304-225-0264 (the number the health department gives for its Quick Response Team), but no dates, no sign-up steps and no date the listing was last updated.
- **Evidence:** https://www.monchd.org/departments-programs/threat-preparedness and https://ready.wv.gov/cert-programs-west-virginia, both read 2026-11-14
- **Why it matters:** CERT is a common way for residents to train for disasters and help neighbors. A resident cannot tell from either page whether the Mon County team is taking new members or when training runs.
- **Who keeps it:** Monongalia County Health Department, Threat Preparedness program
- **Status:** open. CERT is not on the site.
