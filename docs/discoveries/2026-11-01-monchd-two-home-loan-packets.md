# Health department page links two versions of the home loan evaluation packet

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while turning the home loan evaluation PDF into a phone page (part of #393)
- **What we found:** The Monongalia County Health Department's Septic and Wells page links two copies of the same packet. "Request for water and sewage evaluation" opens a PDF with a cover memo dated October 1, 2025. "Request for a home loan evaluation" opens an older copy on Dropbox with a cover memo dated August 8, 2016. Both list the same fees ($225, or $325 with a radon test) and the same form (SG-55). They name different program managers. The guidelines inside both (procedure H-12) are dated February 24, 2012.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-01: https://www.monchd.org/services/septic-and-wells, https://cdn.sanity.io/files/95aan9ec/production/e39c6b23f86e7bfcbcf6625ff51adb35955a4af4.pdf (memo "Date: October 1, 2025"), https://dl.dropbox.com/s/l4eb292wzlcp9ei/SG-55%20Home%20Loan%20Evaluation%20Report.pdf (memo "Date: August 8, 2016")
- **Why it matters:** Buyers, sellers and lenders may print the older copy. Today the fees match, so no one pays the wrong amount, but the two could drift apart if one is updated.
- **Who keeps it:** Monongalia County Health Department, Environmental Health
- **Status:** open
