# MTEC's 2026-2027 catalog labels its adult tuition table "2024 – 2025"

- **Found:** 2026-11-16, while turning the adult parts of MTEC's catalog into a phone page for #393.
- **What we found:**
  - The PDF is titled "2026-2027 Course Catalog". In its "Adult Daytime Programming" part, the tuition table is headed "TUITION: 2024 – 2025 (see following page for *)". It lists Practical Nurse at $1,500.00 per semester and $3,000.00 per year, plus a $100.00 registration fee. No page in the catalog says whether the 2024-2025 amounts still apply.
  - The testing part says "The GED® is currently in use in West Virginia as of Spring 2024" and calls MTEC "the only official GED® testing site in Monongalia County". The next paragraph asks people who need accommodations to request them "with the HiSET® and MTEC’s HiSET® Coordinator." So one section names two different tests.
- **Evidence:** https://files-backend.assets.thrillshare.com/documents/asset/uploaded_file/4695/Mtec/4d2a8fe4-4389-475e-be29-b79b10ef1c2b/Catalog-2026-2027.docx.pdf, read 2026-11-16.
- **Why it matters:** an adult planning to train as a practical nurse cannot tell from the catalog whether the price is current. A GED test taker who needs more time or a separate room cannot tell which test coordinator to ask.
- **Who keeps it:** Monongalia County Technical Education Center (Monongalia County Schools).
- **Status:** open. /mtec-adult-classes/ quotes both as printed, and labels the tuition with the table's own heading.
