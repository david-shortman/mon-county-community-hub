# County's Chestnut Ridge Park Santa event lists the Mason-Dixon Park address

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while checking holiday events (#529)
- **What we found:** The county's page for the "1st Annual Evening With Santa" says "Santa is coming to Chestnut Ridge Park on Dec. 19th," but its address line is "79 Buckeye Road, Core," which is Mason-Dixon Historical Park. The county calendar feed copies that address, so our feed card said the event was at 79 Buckeye Road.
- **Evidence:** https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/business_detail_T51_R47.php and the Dec 12 Mason-Dixon page https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/business_detail_T51_R26.php, both read 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** A family could drive to the wrong park.
- **Who keeps it:** Monongalia County Parks and Recreation
- **Status:** open. Our card now names Chestnut Ridge Park and says to check the page (hand-checked entry with the feed's slug).
