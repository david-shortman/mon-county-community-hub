# Official pages that are gone but still lead search results or links

- **Found:** 2026-11-08, during the weekly findability run ([results](../../content/findability/2026-11-08.yaml)). Most of these were first seen on 2026-10-28 and are still gone after two weeks.
- **What we found:** these official pages return "page not found" (404) but still show up in the top 3 search results for a resident's question, or are still linked from other local pages:
  - City of Morgantown, Waste and Sustainability (`morgantownwv.gov/687/Waste-Sustainability`): top 3 for trash pickup day (q19).
  - City of Morgantown, Recycling Guide (`morgantownwv.gov/688/Recycling-Guide`): top 3 for dropping off paint and electronics (q20).
  - City of Morgantown, Volunteer Opportunities with open board seats (`morgantownwv.gov/334/Volunteer-Opportunities`): first result for serving on a board (q31).
  - Morgantown Utility Board household hazardous waste pages (`mub.org/pages/hhwc`, `mub.org/hhwc`): top 3 for q20, and the Friends of Deckers Creek page and the county recycling site (`moncountyrecycling.com/how-do-i-dispose-of.html`) still send people to `mub.org/hhwc`. The county page also still says to check back "in summer of 2023".
  - Monongalia County Schools, 2025 test results article (`boe.mono.k12.wv.us/article/2354795`): first result for school test scores (q22).
- **Evidence:** each address was loaded on 2026-11-08 at phone width and with a fetch tool. Notes and best working pages are in the results file for each question.
- **Why it matters:** a resident who taps the first result gets an error page and may give up. People without a reliable way to call, or who search on a phone, are hit hardest.
- **Who keeps it:** the City of Morgantown, the Morgantown Utility Board, Monongalia County Schools, and the groups whose pages link to the old utility address.
- **Status:** open.
