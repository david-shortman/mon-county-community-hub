# The city lists a 2022 facade grant under "Current Grant Programs"

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while re-checking #700 (verification sweep 4, #529)
- **What we found:** The City of Morgantown's Development Services page lists the "ARPA Facade and Interior Improvement Grant Program Guidelines and Application" under "Current Grant Programs." The linked application is marked revised April 18, 2022, with a first deadline of June 2, 2022, and rolling review after that "based on the availability of funds." No city page says whether funds remain.
