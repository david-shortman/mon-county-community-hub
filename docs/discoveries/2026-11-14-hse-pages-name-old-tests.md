# State and MTEC high school equivalency pages still name the HiSET test; the state's test is the GED

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while writing the steps to a high school equivalency diploma (#1936, sprint wave 19, #529)
- **What we found:** the WV Department of Education says the State of West Virginia High School Equivalency Diploma comes from passing the four GED tests. Two official pages still use the name of a different test, HiSET. The state's own GED page gives its age rule as a HiSET rule. MTEC's "HS Equivalency Information" page is headed "HiSET Information" and links on to the state. The age rule also differs: the state page says 16, and GED Testing Service's West Virginia page says 19 unless you get an age waiver.
- **Evidence:** [WVDE, The West Virginia High School Equivalency Diploma](https://wvde.us/academics/adult-education/adult-education-student-information/west-virginia-high-school-equivalency-diploma), read 2026-11-14: "Individuals must take and pass all four subject area tests of the GED® exam". [WVDE, The GED Exam](https://wvde.us/academics/adult-education/adult-education-student-information/ged-exam), last updated January 26, 2026, read 2026-11-14: "All candidates must be at least 16 years old to be admitted to HiSET testing." [MTEC, HS Equivalency Information](https://mtec.mono.k12.wv.us/page/hs-equivalency-information), read 2026-11-14: "HiSET (High School Equivalency)". [GED.com, West Virginia](https://www.ged.com/policies/west-virginia.html), read 2026-11-14: "You must be 19 years old to test in West Virginia without an age waiver."
- **Why it matters:** an adult who searches for "HiSET" or reads the MTEC page may think they need a different test than the one the state gives. A 16, 17 or 18 year old may not know there are extra papers to bring.
- **Who keeps it:** the WV Department of Education, Adult Education (its GED page) and MTEC (its Adult Learning Center pages).
- **Status:** open. Our page `/high-school-equivalency/` names only the GED, and says people under 19 need papers, from GED.com, with the teacher's help. No claim about HiSET is on the site.
