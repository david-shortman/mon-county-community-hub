# Only Morgantown has an official way to look up a trash pickup day

- **Found:** 2026-11-15, while looking for an official pickup day source for each hauler and town for #2009 (sprint wave 21, #529).
- **What we found:**
  - **Morgantown (inside city limits):** Republic Services' Morgantown page has a "View my service schedule" button that opens Republic's national site, where a resident enters an address to see pickup and holiday days. The page loads by script, so a plain page read shows nothing; it reads in a browser.
  - **Granville:** the town's Community page says pickup "runs every Monday or Tuesday, depending on your location," but does not say which streets get which day.
  - **Westover:** the city's Garbage and Utilities page says the city picks up garbage and recycling on the same day, but lists no days or streets.
  - **Star City:** the town's Garbage page gives a phone number and a set-out time (5 a.m.), but no days or streets.
  - **Outside the towns:** no county or Solid Waste Authority page lists haulers or days by address.
  - Republic's Morgantown page links the City of Morgantown's Recycling Guide (`https://www.morgantownwv.gov/688/Recycling-Guide`), which answered 404 on 2026-11-15.
- **Evidence:** https://www.republicservices.com/municipality/morgantown-wv, https://townofgranvillewv.gov/community/, https://www.westoverwv.org/utilities, https://www.starcitywv.com/garbage-recycling/, https://www.monongaliacountyswa.com/recycling and https://www.morgantownwv.gov/688/Recycling-Guide, all read 2026-11-15.
- **Why it matters:** "When is trash pickup on my street?" is one of the first questions after a move. Outside Morgantown, the only way to find out is to call.
- **Who keeps it:** Republic Services, the City of Westover, the Town of Star City, the Town of Granville and the Monongalia County Solid Waste Authority.
- **Status:** open. /recycling/#pickup now links Republic's lookup for Morgantown and says plainly that we found no lookup elsewhere. Whether the Solid Waste Authority can tell a caller their day is still open in #901.
