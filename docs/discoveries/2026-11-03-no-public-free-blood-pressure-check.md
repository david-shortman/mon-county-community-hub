# No official page lists a standing free blood pressure check in Mon County

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while filling gaps for "Obesity, diabetes and heart health" (#1534, #1312, sprint wave 11, #529).
- **What we found:** We found no current official page that lists a regular, free blood pressure check in Monongalia County. The health department's A to Z services list has no blood pressure service. WVU Medicine's diabetes education page shows its content only when a browser runs its script, and WVU Extension's pages refused our read, so neither could be confirmed.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [Monongalia County Health Department, services](https://www.monchd.org/services), [Morgantown Public Library, Library of Things](https://mympls.org/books-music-more/library-of-things/) (lends a blood pressure monitor).
- **Why it matters:** High blood pressure often has no symptoms, so a free, walk-in check is a common first step. People without a doctor have no public list of where to get one.
- **What we did:** `/diabetes-and-weight/#blood-pressure` lists the library's monitor, which anyone with a card can borrow. Free checks stay off the site until an official page states one.
- **Who keeps it:** Monongalia County Health Department, the two hospital systems, and pharmacies.
- **Status:** open (#1312).
