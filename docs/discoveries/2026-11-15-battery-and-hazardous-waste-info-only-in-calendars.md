# Where to take batteries and household chemicals is mostly in yearly calendar PDFs

- **Found:** 2026-11-15, while adding local options for batteries, motor oil and Freon appliances to `/recycling/` (#1984, sprint wave 20, #529).
- **What we found:**
  - The list of what MUB's yearly household hazardous waste day takes is not on a current MUB page. `mub.org/hhwc`, which the Solid Waste Authority's pages link to, answers "Page not found" while MUB's site is rebuilt. The newest list we could read is in the Solid Waste Authority's 2025 calendar PDF.
  - No 2027 date for the household hazardous waste day is posted. The Authority's 2026 calendar says the 2026 date "could not be confirmed"; its event page and a blog post later gave Saturday, August 1, 2026.
  - The same blog post calls the day "one time a year in the fall", while the 2025 and 2026 days were in early August.
  - For single-use batteries the rest of the year, the Authority's 2026 calendar names Staples and mail-in programs. Staples' own West Virginia store list shows stores in Beckley, Clarksburg and Cross Lanes, none in Mon County.
  - Where to take fridges, freezers, window AC units and dehumidifiers is stated only in the 2026 calendar PDF (inside back page), not on the Authority's Disposal web page.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-14 (Eastern time).
  - https://www.monongaliacountyswa.com/calendars (links to the 2025 and 2026 calendar PDFs)
  - https://www.monongaliacountyswa.com/event-details/2026-household-hazardous-waste-collection-hhwc-day
  - https://www.monongaliacountyswa.com/post/2025-how-to-prepare-for-recycle-and-disposal-collection-events
  - https://mub.org/hhwc ("Page not found")
  - https://stores.staples.com/wv
- **Why it matters:** someone with old batteries, used oil or a dead fridge has to open a 32-page calendar to find the local answer, and the year-round battery option named there is about 40 miles away.
- **Who keeps it:** the Morgantown Utility Board (the event and its list), the Monongalia County Solid Waste Authority (its calendars and web pages).
- **Status:** open.
