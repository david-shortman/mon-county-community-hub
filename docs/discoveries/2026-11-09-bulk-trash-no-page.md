# No official page says how bulk trash pickup works in Morgantown

- **Found:** 2026-11-09, while answering "bulk trash" for #1764.
- **What we found:** Westover's utilities page says to call City Hall for a special bulk pickup. For the rest of the county we found no official page that says whether bulk pickup of furniture, mattresses or appliances is offered, how to ask for it, or what it costs. That includes Morgantown city limits, where Republic Services is the hauler, and Star City.
  - The county Solid Waste Authority's recycling and disposal pages name the haulers but do not cover bulk items.
  - The city's Public Works Streets page does not mention garbage or bulk items.
  - Star City's garbage page gives a number for questions only.
  - Republic Services' Morgantown page loads its text by script, so a plain read gets nothing. We have not read what it says.
- **Evidence:** pages read 2026-11-09. Links are in #1777.
- **Why it matters:** "bulk trash" is a common search. Someone moving out or replacing a couch has to call around, during office hours, to learn the rules.
- **Who keeps it:** the City of Morgantown, Republic Services, the Town of Star City, and the Monongalia County Solid Waste Authority.
- **Status:** open (#1777).
