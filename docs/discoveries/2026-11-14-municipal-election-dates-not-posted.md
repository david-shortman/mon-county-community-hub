# Town election dates are not posted ahead of time; Morgantown's 2027 deadlines are not posted yet

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while adding the next election file for /vote/ (sprint wave 19, #529)
- **What we found:** The City of Morgantown posts its next City Council election day (April 27, 2027) and poll hours, but not yet the registration deadline, early voting dates, absentee request deadline or canvass date. It states the registration rule and says early voting dates "will be posted here when they become available." The Westover and Star City websites post no election dates. Granville's public notices page names an election on "Tuesday June 10th" with no year (June 10 was a Tuesday in 2025).
- **Evidence:** [Morgantown, Election Information](https://www.morgantownwv.gov/157/Election-Information), read 2026-11-14: "The next Morgantown City Council Election takes place on April 27, 2027." [Morgantown, Voter Information](https://www.morgantownwv.gov/590/Voter-Information), read 2026-11-14. [Westover, Government](https://www.westoverwv.org/government), [Star City](https://www.starcitywv.com/) and [Granville, Public Notices](https://townofgranvillewv.gov/public-notices/), read 2026-11-14.
- **Why it matters:** people who live in a town can vote in its elections, but with no posted date they may not know an election is coming, or when to register.
- **Who keeps it:** each town's clerk (Morgantown City Clerk, Westover, Star City, Granville).
- **Status:** open. Morgantown's election day is on /vote/; the missing dates are tracked in #1958.
