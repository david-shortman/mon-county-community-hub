# No state page explains foreclosure help for most West Virginia homeowners

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, building the mortgage help page (#1188, part of #529)
- **What we found:** The WV Housing Development Fund says its mortgage help is for its own borrowers only. Its site no longer mentions the state's Homeowner Assistance Fund program, and that program's old site did not load, so no official page says what other homeowners can get from the state. HUD's counselor finder now only works with JavaScript; the plain list is reachable through the CFPB's ZIP code page. The WV Attorney General's site has no foreclosure or rescue scam page (site search finds nothing). USDA Rural Development refuses automated reads (403). CHANGE, Inc.'s housing page shows a phone number that differs from HUD's listing.
- **Evidence:** https://www.wvhdf.com/current-customers/help-for-homeowners/, https://www.wvhdf.com/?s=mountaineer+rescue, https://www.hud.gov/findacounselor, https://www.consumerfinance.gov/find-a-housing-counselor/?zipcode=26505, https://ago.wv.gov/search?search=foreclosure, https://www.rd.usda.gov/, https://changeinc.org/housing/, all checked 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** A homeowner behind on payments may not know whether any state help exists, and may not find a counselor without a modern browser.
- **Who keeps it:** WV Housing Development Fund; U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development; WV Attorney General; USDA Rural Development
- **Status:** open (#1236)
