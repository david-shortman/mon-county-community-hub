# The WV SHIP website does not load on its secure address

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, re-checking health, family and benefits listings (#529)
- **What we found:** https://www.wvship.org resets the connection, and returned 502 in a browser. The same site loads on plain http.
- **Evidence:** https://www.wvship.org, tried 2026-11-01 (connection reset, then 502).
- **Why it matters:** People looking for free Medicare counseling during open enrollment may find the site won't open.
- **Who keeps it:** West Virginia Bureau of Senior Services
- **Status:** open
