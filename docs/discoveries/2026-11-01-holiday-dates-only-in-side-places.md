# Some 2026 holiday dates are posted only in side places, or only on old pages

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while re-checking holiday programs and downtown dates (#1015, #529)
- **What we found:**
  - **Downtown Christmas Parade.** The only readable page with a 2026 date is the Monongalia County Commission's November 4 agenda. Item 11c is a request from Main Street Morgantown to record and televise the "Annual Christmas Parade on December 2, 2026." No page we can read gives the time or route. Main Street Morgantown's public events page describes its holiday events without dates, and its downtown calendar shows a block page to us.
  - **Downtown tree lighting.** Event listing sites quote a Main Street Morgantown and City of Morgantown tree lighting on Friday, November 20. We could not find that date on the organizers' own pages we can read (the City's November calendar lists only meetings), so it stays off the site.
  - **Mountain Line holiday service.** `busride.org/thanksgiving-schedule` and `busride.org/holiday-schedule` are still old press releases. The Thanksgiving one says regular service resumes "Thursday January 21," which fits 2021, not 2026. A rider reading them now could take them as this year's schedule.
  - **Now readable again.** `mountainglowwv.com` and `artsgm.org/first-friday/` loaded normally on November 1 (they returned "Error 406" earlier the same day, see `2026-11-01-event-calendars-block-automated-reads.md`).
- **Evidence:** https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/document_center/Agenda/REGULAR%20MEETINGS/2026/AGENDA%20November%204,%202026.pdf ; https://morgantownpartnership.com/main-street-morgantown/events-downtown/ ; https://www.morgantownwv.gov/calendar.aspx ; https://www.busride.org/thanksgiving-schedule ; https://www.busride.org/holiday-schedule . All read 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** Residents look for the parade, tree lighting and holiday bus service in early November. When the date sits only in a county agenda or an event listing site, or an old schedule page is still up, people can miss it or act on last year's information.
- **Who keeps it:** Main Street Morgantown (Morgantown Area Partnership), City of Morgantown, Mountain Line Transit Authority.
- **Status:** open
