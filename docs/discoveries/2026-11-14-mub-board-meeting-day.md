# The city's MUB page and MUB's own agenda give different meeting days

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while re-checking #1744 (sprint wave 15, verification sweep 6, #529)
- **What we found:** the City of Morgantown's Morgantown Utility Board page says the board meets on the second Tuesday of each month. MUB's own agenda for its November 2026 regular meeting gives Tuesday, November 17, 2026, which is the 3rd Tuesday. That agenda approves the minutes of the October 13, 2026 meeting, so no regular meeting was held on the 2nd Tuesday, November 10. Neither page says why November is different.
- **Evidence:** [MUB, Board of Directors Meeting, Tuesday, November 17, 2026](https://mub.org/newsroom/mub-board-of-directors-meeting), posted November 13, read 2026-11-14: "REGULAR MEETING Tuesday, November 17, 2026 ... 5:30 P.M. 278 Greenbag Road" and "Regular Meeting – October 13, 2026." [City of Morgantown, MUB page](https://www.morgantownwv.gov/263/Morgantown-Utility-Board-MUB), read 2026-11-14: "Second Tuesday of each month."
- **Why it matters:** a resident who goes by the city's page would arrive a week early for the November meeting.
- **Who keeps it:** the City of Morgantown (its board page) and MUB (its newsroom).
- **Status:** open. The site's board entry now gives the November 17 date and says to check MUB's newsroom for each month.
