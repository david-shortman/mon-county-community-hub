# Morgantown's water utility has no lead or water quality pages online during its rebuild

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while building the lead page for #222 (sprint wave 11, #529).
- **What we found:** The Morgantown Utility Board's lead page, its water quality report page and its news posts about the lead service line inventory all return "not found." The home page says the site is being rebuilt. We found no other official page where a resident can learn whether the pipe to their home is lead.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [mub.org](https://mub.org/) ("The full website is being rebuilt, so some pages are temporarily unavailable"); mub.org/lead and mub.org/ccr return 404.
- **Why it matters:** People who want to know about lead in their water have no official local page to read right now. The older general note is `2026-10-31-mub-site-old-pages.md`.
- **What we did:** `/lead/` gives EPA's tips and says to ask your water utility. Needs-verification issue #1574 asks a person to re-check.
- **Who keeps it:** Morgantown Utility Board.
- **Status:** open.
