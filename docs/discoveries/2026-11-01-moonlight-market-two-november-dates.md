# The Moonlight Market's own pages give two November dates

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while re-checking #681 (verification sweep 4, #529)
- **What we found:** The WV Moonlight Market home page lists "5/16 7/18 9/19 11/7." Its apply page says "Dates for the 2026 are: May 16, July 18, Sept. 19, from 4-9pm and Nov 14 from 2-8pm," and in another line mentions spaces open "for 9/19 & 11/7."
- **Evidence:** https://www.wvmoonlightmarket.com/ and https://www.wvmoonlightmarket.com/apply, both read 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** Shoppers and makers cannot tell from the organizer's pages whether the last market of the year is November 7 or November 14.
- **Who keeps it:** WV Moonlight Market.
- **Status:** open
