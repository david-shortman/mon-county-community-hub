# The state's Emergency Assistance page gives no phone number, while a utility page gives one

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while re-checking #1065 (verification sweep 4, #529)
- **What we found:** The state's Emergency Assistance page says to "contact your local Department of Human Services (DoHS) office" and gives no phone number. Mon Power's assistance page lists 304-558-0684 for Emergency Assistance. We found that number on no state page.
- **Evidence:** https://bfa.wv.gov/bfa-programs/emergency-assistance and https://www.firstenergycorp.com/help/billingpayments/assistance_serviceprogram/mon_power.html, both read 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** Someone facing a shutoff gets different first steps depending on which page they find.
- **Who keeps it:** The West Virginia Department of Human Services, and Mon Power for its page.
- **Status:** open
