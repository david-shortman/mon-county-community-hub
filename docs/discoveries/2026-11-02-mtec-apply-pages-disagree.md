# MTEC application pages give mixed signals on year and grades

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while checking teen facts for #592 (sprint wave 10, #529).
- **What we found:** MTEC's Apply Now page still says "APPLY NOW for the 2026-2027 school year," while the recruiting site it links to sets a deadline of November 29, 2026 at 3 p.m. without naming a school year. The recruiting FAQ answers "Can I apply in 9th grade?" with "Yes, we accept 10th graders on a very limited basis." The Monongalia County Schools district site has no page on career and technical education or how students sign up for MTEC.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [MTEC Apply Now](https://mtec.mono.k12.wv.us/o/mtec/page/apply-now), [Go to MTEC home](https://sites.google.com/boe.mono.k12.wv.us/go-to-mtec/home), [Go to MTEC FAQ](https://sites.google.com/boe.mono.k12.wv.us/go-to-mtec/faq).
- **Why it matters:** Families planning for next school year may not be sure which year the window is for or whether a 9th grader can apply.
- **Who keeps it:** MTEC and Monongalia County Schools.
- **Status:** open. Our listing quotes the deadline and grades from the recruiting site (#1488). A call to MTEC, 304-291-9240, could settle the year.
