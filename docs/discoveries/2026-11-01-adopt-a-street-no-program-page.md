# Morgantown's Adopt-A-Street program page is only a sign-up form

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, re-checking the Adopt-A-Street entry (#710, sprint wave 9, #529)
- **What we found:** https://www.morgantownwv.gov/665/Adopt-a-Street-Program redirects to the program's Morgantown 311 (SeeClickFix) form. The rules (4 cleanups a year for a sign, renew each fall by the end of September, bring your own bags, call Public Works 1 to 2 days ahead) are only in the city's June 10, 2025 announcement: https://morgantownwv.gov/CivicSend/ViewMessage/message/260486.
- **Why it matters:** someone who opens the program link sees a form with no rules. If the rules change, there is no current page to show it.
- **Who keeps it:** City of Morgantown, Engineering and Public Works and Development Services
- **Status:** open
