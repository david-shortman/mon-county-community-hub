# The health department's own pages give no clinic hours

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, building the Nearest help box on town pages (#1814)
- **What we found:** The Monongalia County Health Department's home page gives its address (453 Van Voorhis Road) and phone numbers, and its clinical services and immunizations pages say to call 304-598-5119 for an appointment. None of the three pages gives the days or hours the clinic is open. Our older listing takes its hours from the state's page about the department.
- **Evidence:** https://www.monchd.org/, https://www.monchd.org/departments-programs/clinical-services and https://www.monchd.org/services/immunizations, all read 2026-11-14. https://www.monchd.org/contact answered "not found" the same day.
- **Why it matters:** Someone deciding whether to go in person after work cannot tell from the department's own site when it is open.
- **Who keeps it:** Monongalia County Health Department
- **Status:** open
