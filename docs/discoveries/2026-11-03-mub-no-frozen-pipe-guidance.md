# MUB's website has no winter or frozen pipe advice

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, sprint wave 11 winter lane (#529)
- **What we found:** The Morgantown Utility Board's site is being rebuilt. Its home page lists customer links, three news posts and a phone line that answers emergencies 24 hours a day. No page covers frozen pipes, where to find a home's water shutoff valve, or what MUB does when a customer's pipes freeze.
- **Evidence:** https://mub.org/ and https://mub.org/newsroom, read 2026-11-02.
- **Why it matters:** Frozen and burst pipes are a common winter emergency. Residents have to find general advice elsewhere and can't learn which side of the meter is MUB's job.
- **Who keeps it:** Morgantown Utility Board
- **Status:** open
