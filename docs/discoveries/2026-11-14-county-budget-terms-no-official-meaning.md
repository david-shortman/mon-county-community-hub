# No official page we could reach explains "Fund Balance" or the 10% contingency limit in the county budget

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while adding plain meanings for budget words to `/county-budget/` (#1880, sprint wave 17, #529)
- **What we found:** the County Commission's 2026-2027 Levy Estimate lists "Fund Balance" as its first General Fund revenue line and a spending line "Contingencies - Not to Exceed 10% of Budget". West Virginia Code defines "General fund" and "Estimated revenues" (§6-9D-2), describes the levy estimate (§11-8-10) and the coal severance tax revenue fund (§11-13A-6), but we found no official page that says what "Fund Balance" means in a county budget or which rule sets the 10% limit. The State Auditor's local government pages, the likely source, could not be reached from our tools that day.
- **Evidence:** [the Levy Estimate PDF](https://www.monongaliacountyclerk.com/myfiles/documents/budget/FY27_Budget.pdf), read 2026-11-14. [WV Code §11-8-10](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/11-8-10/), read 2026-11-14: the estimate must make "proper allowance for delinquent taxes, exonerations and contingencies", with no limit stated.
- **Why it matters:** "Fund Balance" is $10,000,000 of the $44,011,156 General Fund total. A resident may read it as new income when it may not be. We cannot say either way without a source.
- **Who keeps it:** the WV State Auditor's Office (Local Government Services) and the Monongalia County Clerk.
- **Status:** open. Left off the site. Tracked in #1891.
