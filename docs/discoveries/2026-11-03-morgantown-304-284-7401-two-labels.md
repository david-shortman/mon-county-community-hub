# Two city pages give one phone number two different office names

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while re-reading the building permit sources for #122 (sprint wave 10, #529).
- **What we found:** The City of Morgantown's Code Enforcement page lists 304-284-7401 as the Code Enforcement phone, at 389 Spruce Street. The city's Permitting, Licensing and Registration page gives the same number as the Finance Department's, for questions about B&O fees.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [Code Enforcement](https://www.morgantownwv.gov/167/Code-Enforcement) ("Phone: 304-284-7401"), [Permitting, Licensing and Registration](https://www.morgantownwv.gov/704/Permitting-Licensing-Registration) ("Please contact the Finance Department at 304-284-7401 to gather information on paying your B&O fees").
- **Why it matters:** A resident calling about a permit or about B&O taxes cannot tell from the city's pages which office answers that number. It may be a shared front desk, or one page may be out of date.
- **What we did:** Our permits page keeps 304-284-7401 for Code Enforcement, as the Code Enforcement page says.
- **Who keeps it:** City of Morgantown.
- **Status:** open. A person could ask the city which office answers 304-284-7401.
