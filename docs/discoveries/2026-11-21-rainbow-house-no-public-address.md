# The Rainbow House emergency shelter has no public address we could read

- **Found:** 2026-11-21, while answering review follow-ups on the warming shelter PR (#2024, #219).
- **What we found:** The City of Morgantown's PATHWAYS to Help page lists Project Rainbow's Rainbow House as a 22-bed emergency shelter, open 24/7, with a phone number and a PO Box, but no street address. Project Rainbow's own website builds its pages by script, so its text could not be read automatically, and we found no other official page that gives where the shelter is or how to get in (call first or walk in).
- **Evidence:** https://www.morgantownwv.gov/708/PATHWAYS-to-Help, read 2026-11-15: "Project Rainbow PO Box 1414 Morgantown, WV 26507 (304)-292-7414" and "The Rainbow House is a 22-bed shelter that operates 24/7". https://www.projectrainbowwv.com/, read 2026-11-15: the page loads its text by script.
- **Why it matters:** Someone who needs a bed tonight has to call before they know where to go. A shelter may keep its address private on purpose, which is a fair choice, but then the public record should say how to get in.
- **Who keeps it:** Project Rainbow, and the City of Morgantown for its resource page.
- **Status:** open. The listing on /stay-tonight/ says plainly that no address is given and to call first. A person checks Project Rainbow's own site in #2023, item 4.
