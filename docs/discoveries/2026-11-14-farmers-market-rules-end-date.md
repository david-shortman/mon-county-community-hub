# The farmers market's 2026-2027 rules give two end dates for the Saturday market

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while turning the market's rules into a phone page (#393, sprint wave 17, #529)
- **What we found:** the Morgantown Farmers' Market Growers Association's 2026-2027 Market Rules and Guidelines give two different end dates for the Saturday Summer Market. Section 4.1 says it runs "through the second or third weekend in November". Section 8.2 says "from the first weekend of May through the first weekend of November."
- **Evidence:** [2026-2027 Market Rules and Guidelines (PDF)](https://www.morgantownfarmersmarket.org/uploads/1/4/2/9/142935559/26-27_mfmga_market_rules.pdf), pages 6 and 21, read 2026-11-14.
- **Why it matters:** a vendor or shopper planning for November cannot tell from the rules alone whether the Saturday market is still running.
- **Who keeps it:** the Morgantown Farmers' Market Growers Association.
- **Status:** open. `/farmers-market-vendor-rules/` quotes both and says to ask the Market Manager. The market's own posted dates are the better guide each year.
