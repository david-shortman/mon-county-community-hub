# A county seed swap was listed with no page saying who runs it or how it works

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while checking the growing gaps on /grow/ for #442.
- **What we found:** the Monongalia County Solid Waste Authority's events page listed a Community Seed Swap at the Mon County Center on November 7, 2026 (seen 2026-10-30, #897). We found no page from the swap's organizer that says who runs it, how to take part, or when the next one is. The county's Community Seed Library page names the library but does not say who can use it or how. On 2026-11-14 the events page says "Check back for 2026 updates" and no longer lists the swap.
- **Evidence:** https://www.monongaliacountyswa.com/events-1, read 2026-11-14; https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/departments/mon_county_center/community_seed_library.php, read 2026-11-14.
- **Why it matters:** seed swaps and seed libraries are free ways to start a garden. A gardener who hears of one has no page to learn the next date or how it works.
- **Who keeps it:** the Mon County Center (Monongalia County Commission) for the seed library page, and the swap's organizer.
- **Status:** open. The gap stays in the Help wanted box on /grow/.
