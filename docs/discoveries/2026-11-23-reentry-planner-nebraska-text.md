# The state reentry planner still carries another state's rules

- **Found:** 2026-11-22, during the weekly findability run ([results](../../content/findability/2026-11-22.yaml), question q55 "I just got out of prison. Who helps with a job and an ID?").
- **What we found:** the WV Division of Corrections and Rehabilitation's "Reentry Planner" PDF now ranks near the top of search for help after prison. Parts of it describe another state:
  - The ID section says the cost of a birth certificate "will be covered by NDCS", and a cost line reads "Covered by NDCS, if qualified". NDCS is not the name of West Virginia's agency (WVDCR), which the planner also names in other places.
  - It gives a mailing address in Lincoln, NE, and says "The State of Nebraska has laws regarding landlord and tenant relations".
  - A credit line near the end reads "Created by Nebraska Department of ...".
  - A resident reading it cannot tell which ID and housing rules apply in West Virginia.
- **Evidence:** [Reentry Planner WVDCR (2).pdf](https://dcr.wv.gov/services/offenderservices/SiteAssets/Pages/default/Reentry%20Planner%20WVDCR%20%282%29.pdf), 128 pages, created and last modified 2024-06-10 per the file's own data, downloaded and read 2026-11-22. It was second in the plain search and first in the reworded search for q55. The file is not stored in this repository.
- **Why it matters:** people leaving prison need clear steps for an ID and housing. Rules from another state can send them to the wrong office or lead them to expect a cost to be covered when it may not be.
- **Who keeps it:** the WV Division of Corrections and Rehabilitation (Offender Services).
- **Status:** open.
