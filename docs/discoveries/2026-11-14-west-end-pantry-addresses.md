# Two pantry addresses differ between the United Way food list and other official sources

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while mapping what is nearest to the west end and south end for #1416.
- **What we found:**
  - Starting Points: its own pantry page says pickup is by appointment and lists 537 Blue Horizon Dr. The United Way food list gives 901 Mylan Lane with walk-in hours on Wednesday, 12:30 to 3 p.m. The Census Geocoder could not match 901 Mylan Lane.
  - Clay-Battelle Senior Center, Blacksville: our place record has 5977 Mason-Dixon Highway (from the county's agency contact list). The United Way food list gives 6061.
- **Evidence:** United Way of Monongalia and Preston Counties food list, https://unitedwaympc.org/food-hunger (PDF dated January 26, 2026), read 2026-11-14; https://moncountyfamilysupportcenter.org/, read 2026-11-14; https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/contact_information_for_county_agencies_and_departments/, checked 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** a person who drives from Core or Wadestown to the wrong address, or walks in where pickup is by appointment, may not get food that day.
- **Who keeps it:** United Way of Monongalia and Preston Counties (its list), Starting Points and the Clay-Battelle Senior Center (their own details).
