# West Virginia's LIEAP papers give two hearing deadlines and two emergency deadlines

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, writing the LIEAP "after you apply" page (#1152, part of #1148)
- **What we found:** The Income Maintenance Manual's LIEAP chapter (21.6.9) gives 60 days to ask for a hearing. The general hearing request form DFA-FH-1, printed in the same manual, says 90 days. For Emergency LIEAP papers, the FY 2027 fact sheet says 5 calendar days and the manual says 5 working days.
- **Evidence:** see #1164 for the quotes and links.
- **Why it matters:** Someone who goes by the form could miss the 60 day LIEAP deadline.
- **Who keeps it:** West Virginia Department of Human Services, Bureau for Family Assistance
- **Status:** open (#1164)
