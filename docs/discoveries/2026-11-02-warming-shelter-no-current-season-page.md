# No official page states this winter's warming shelter plan

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while doing the wave 10 warming shelter check for `/tonight/` (#863, #529)
- **What we found:** As of November 2, no official or provider page states the opening date, place, hours or rules for the 2026 to 2027 winter warming shelter in Morgantown. The two warming shelter pages on the Hazel's House of Hope website still describe past seasons. The newest news about this winter's shelter is from August 2026.
- **Evidence:** all read on 2026-11-02.
  - Hazel's House of Hope, "Warming Shelter" (https://www.hazelshousewv.com/organizations/warming-shelter): says the shelter "opened for the season Thursday, Dec. 1" and "will be open until March 31, 2023".
  - Hazel's House of Hope, "Warming Shelter at Hazel's House of Hope" (https://www.hazelshousewv.com/hazels-house-website/warming-shelter): "Operating Dates: December 11, 2024 - March 15, 2025".
  - Catholic Charities West Virginia, Grace Shelter (https://www.ccwva.org/programs/grace-shelter/): describes the year-round Grace Shelter and does not mention a warming shelter.
  - City of Morgantown News Flash (https://www.morgantownwv.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx): newest release is from October 19, 2026, and none is about a warming shelter.
  - Monongalia County home page (https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/): no warming shelter notice.
  - Local news: the newest stories are from August 2026 (Dominion Post, https://www.dominionpost.com/tag/warming-shelter/; WAJR, https://wajr.com/2026/08/24/catholic-charities-west-virginia-makes-request-to-mon-county-commission-for-warming-shelter-funding/).
- **Why it matters:** People searching in early winter find pages with past dates and hours, which can look current. Last winter the shelter opened in mid December, so these weeks are when people look.
- **Who keeps it:** Hazel's House of Hope (its website), and the shelter operator once one is named for this winter.
- **Status:** open. `/tonight/` keeps showing "not announced" until a same-day source confirms details (#863). Related: #944.
