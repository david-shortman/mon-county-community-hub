# The state's Pre-LIEAP mailing notice can be read two ways

- **Found:** 2026-11-03, while rewriting high-stakes pages in plain language (sprint wave 13, #529, #1554)
- **What we found:** The notice says who gets a heating help application in the mail, but one sentence can be read two ways. It could mean households that got LIEAP last season, plus anyone in the three groups. Or it could mean only households that got LIEAP last season and are also in one of the groups. The site keeps the state's wording as written, so it does not pick one meaning.
- **Evidence:** West Virginia Department of Human Services, "West Virginia Department of Human Services Announces LIEAP Application Mailing Beginning November 2," October 30, 2026, read 2026-11-03: https://dohs.wv.gov/article/west-virginia-department-human-services-announces-lieap-application-mailing-beginning. It says: "Mailing criteria include households that received regular LIEAP benefits during the previous season and those who: Have a child under age 6 Are adults aged 60 and older Are individuals with disabilities". Our calendar entry (`content/calendar/lieap-pre-application-2026-27.yaml`) follows that wording.
- **Why it matters:** A household that did not get LIEAP last year, with a child under 6 or someone 60 or older, may not know whether to wait for a form in the mail or apply on its own. Anyone can still apply on WV PATH or at a DoHS office, which the notice also says.
- **Who keeps it:** West Virginia Department of Human Services, Bureau for Family Assistance.
- **Status:** open
