# Grey Line web page and schedule give different airport doors

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while building the first week without a car cards (#1442)
- **What we found:** Mountain Line's Grey Line web page and its Grey Line schedule PDF name different places to get on and off at Pittsburgh International Airport.
- **Evidence:**
  - https://www.busride.org/29-grey-line (read 2026-11-02): "PIT Airport Drop off - Lower Level Door #9 Pick Up - Lower Level Door #9"
  - https://irp.cdn-website.com/869937e9/files/uploaded/29+Greyline-a0cf79c6.pdf, "Effective July, 2025" (read 2026-11-02): "PIT Airport Drop off - Departures Pick Up - Outside Ground Transit Door 2"
- **Why it matters:** A rider waiting for the bus home from the airport needs to know which door to stand at.
- **Who keeps it:** Mountain Line Transit Authority (Grey Line)
- **Status:** open. Tracked for our site in #1460; the door number is off the site until it is confirmed.
