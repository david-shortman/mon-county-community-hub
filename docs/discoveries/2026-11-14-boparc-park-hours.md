# BOPARC park pages do not say when parks are open

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while looking for free places to sit for #1439.
- **What we found:** the BOPARC pages for White Park and Krepps Park list pavilions, trails and seasonal restrooms, and no entry fee, but give no park hours and do not say the parks are open to the public. BOPARC's Know before you go page gives office hours, not park hours. The county's park FAQs do state both (for example, Mason-Dixon Historical Park: "open to the public during regular park hours," 8 a.m. to dusk).
- **Evidence:** https://www.boparc.org/white-park.html, https://www.boparc.org/krepps-park.html and https://www.boparc.org/know-before-you-go.html, read 2026-11-14.
- **Why it matters:** someone looking for a free place to sit, walk or picnic cannot tell from the official page when a city park is open. We list a park only when its source says when it is open, so these parks stay off the free places to sit list.
- **Who keeps it:** BOPARC, the Morgantown Board of Parks and Recreation.
- **Status:** open. Related: [free places to sit research](../research/free-places-to-sit.md).
