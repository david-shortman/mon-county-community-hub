# Blacksville: no official web page for town council meetings

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while looking for where Blacksville posts its meetings (#860, sprint wave 15, #529)
- **What we found:** No official web page says when Blacksville Town Council meets or posts its agendas or minutes. The town does not appear to have a website.
- **Evidence:**
  - The County's directory of local governments (https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/contact_information_for_county_agencies_and_departments/index.php, read 2026-11-14) lists the Town of Blacksville with a mailing address, phone and email, but no web address. It lists a web address for Westover and for Granville.
  - Web searches on 2026-11-14 for a Blacksville town site, council agendas or minutes found none. Guessed addresses (blacksvillewv.gov, blacksvillewv.org, townofblacksville.com, townofblacksvillewv.gov) did not answer, and the state's small-town host has no Blacksville page (https://local.wv.gov/Blacksville, 404).
  - Earlier checks found the same: no agendas found in October 2026 (#13), and none on 2026-11-01 (#860).
- **Why it matters:** Blacksville residents, and anyone with business before the council, cannot find meeting times or decisions online. Our meetings pages list Blacksville with no link.
- **Who keeps it:** Town of Blacksville.
- **Status:** open. The question is on the call list for a person to ask the town office.
