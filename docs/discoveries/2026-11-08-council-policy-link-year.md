# Council's electronic meetings policy is linked with the wrong year

- **Found:** 2026-11-08, while re-reading `/have-your-say/` sources (#122).
- **What:** The City Clerk's page (https://www.morgantownwv.gov/156/City-Clerk) links a document titled "2020 City Council Policy on Conduct of Public Meetings by Electronic Means". The document itself (https://www.morgantownwv.gov/DocumentCenter/View/6103/City-Council-Policy-Conduct-of-Public-Meetings-by-Electronic-Means) is marked 2025-28 and says "Adopted: May 6, 2025".
- **Why it matters:** Someone who sees "2020" may think the rules for commenting by phone or in writing are old or replaced.
- **What we did:** The site gives the adoption date from the document itself. Nothing else to do on our side.
