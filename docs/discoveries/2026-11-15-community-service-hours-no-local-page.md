# No official local page says how court-ordered community service hours work in Mon County

- **Found:** 2026-11-15, while fixing the search miss "community service hours" (sprint wave 20, #529)
- **What we found:** State law says a magistrate or a city judge may order community service in place of a fine or jail time, that the work must be done for a government office, a charity or a nonprofit, and who oversees it. No official Mon County or City page we found says which local places take court-ordered hours, how hours are logged, or who to call. The state courts' Monongalia County page lists the probation office but says nothing about community service. We also found no Monongalia County Schools page that sets a service-hours rule for students.
- **Evidence:** [WV Code §62-4-16](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/62-4-16/), read 2026-11-15: "Any community service ordered pursuant to the provisions of this section shall be performed for government entities or charitable or nonprofit entities." [WV Courts, Monongalia County](https://www.courtswv.gov/public-resources/court-information-county/monongalia_county), read 2026-11-15: lists the probation office at the Justice Center, 75 High Street, with no community service section.
- **Why it matters:** people told to do service hours search for where to do them. Without a local page, the site can only say what state law says and tell them to ask their court or probation officer.
- **Who keeps it:** Monongalia County Magistrate Court, the Sheriff's Office and the County Commission (for magistrate-ordered service), and Morgantown Municipal Court (for city-ordered service).
- **Status:** open. The /volunteer/ page now says what state law says and points people to their court. Nothing about local places or logging hours is on the site.
