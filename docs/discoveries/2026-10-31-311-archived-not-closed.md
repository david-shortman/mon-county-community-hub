# Most Morgantown 311 requests are marked archived, not closed

- **Found:** 2026-10-31, from the first automatic run of the 311 totals job (#1075, #1078)
- **What we found:** Of 931 Morgantown 311 requests opened from October 31, 2025 to October 31, 2026, 757 are "archived" and 10 are "closed." For "Street Maintenance & Potholes," 216 of 226 are archived and 5 are closed.
- **Evidence:** the public SeeClickFix API for Morgantown 311, read by the job on 2026-10-31 (run 41238599453). Credit: Data from SeeClickFix, Morgantown 311.
- **Why it matters:** A resident, or the city, cannot tell from the public data how fast requests are handled, or whether "archived" means fixed. A median "days to close" from 10 requests says little.
- **Who keeps it:** City of Morgantown (Morgantown 311 on SeeClickFix)
- **Status:** open
