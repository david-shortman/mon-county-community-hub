# No hotspot or laptop lending found in Mon County

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while looking for free public Wi-Fi and device lending in Mon County (#1935 follow-on, sprint wave 19, #529)
- **What we found:** The Morgantown Public Library System's Library of Things lists the electronics it lends, and they do not include Wi-Fi hotspots, laptops or tablets. Its Services page says free computers and Wi-Fi are at all its locations, which the site already shows on `/internet/`. A web search found no other library or public office in the county with a current page about lending hotspots or laptops.
- **Evidence:** [MPLS, Library of Things](https://mympls.org/books-music-more/library-of-things/), read 2026-11-14: "Our electronics available: Blu-Ray player CD players Scientific calculators". [MPLS, Services](https://mympls.org/services/), read 2026-11-14: "Public computers and WiFi are available at all our locations."
- **Why it matters:** a resident with no internet at home can use a computer at the library during open hours, but we found no local place that lends a device or a hotspot to take home.
- **Who keeps it:** the Morgantown Public Library System (its lending list). A person could ask the library whether it plans to lend hotspots; that is a contact, so it is David's call.
