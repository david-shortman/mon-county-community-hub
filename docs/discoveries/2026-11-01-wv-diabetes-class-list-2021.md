# The state's list of diabetes classes was last revised in March 2021

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while gathering diabetes and heart health programs for Mon County (#1256)
- **What we found:** The WV Bureau for Public Health's "Diabetes Prevention and Management Programs Offered in West Virginia" describes the program types (National Diabetes Prevention Program, Dining With Diabetes, the Diabetes Self-Management Program and others) and says "Revised 16-MAR-2021". We found no newer statewide list of where classes run. WVU Medicine's Morgantown diabetes education page moved, and its new page shows no text without a script, so we could not confirm what it offers now (#1312).
- **Evidence:** https://dhhr.wv.gov/hpcd/data_reports/Documents/2021%20Diabetes%20Prevention%20and%20Management%20Classes%20Offered%20in%20WV.pdf (read 2026-11-01). https://wvumedicine.org/ruby-memorial-hospital/services/wvu-specialty-clinics/endocrinology-diabetes/diabetes-education-center returned "Page not found" the same day.
- **Why it matters:** Someone told they have prediabetes has no current public list of classes near them. Each hospital or program has to be found one at a time.
- **Who keeps it:** WV Department of Health, Bureau for Public Health, Division of Health Promotion and Chronic Disease; WVU Medicine
- **Status:** open
