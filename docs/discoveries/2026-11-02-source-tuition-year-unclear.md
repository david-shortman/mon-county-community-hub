# The SOURCE's tuition handbook names no school year

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while re-checking school-age care facts for #613 (sprint wave 10, round two, #529).
- **What we found:** The SOURCE's before and after school page has a "2026-27 Tuition" section that links its 2025-26 handbook, and a "Ready to Enroll" section that links a Spring 2026 handbook. Both list the same monthly rates, and neither names a school year. The site's home page also says its programs are "always free, to anyone with any need," while this program charges tuition.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [Before and after school program](https://www.thesourcewv.org/before-after-school-program), [The SOURCE home page](https://www.thesourcewv.org/).
- **Why it matters:** Parents cannot tell what before and after care will cost this school year.
- **Who keeps it:** The SOURCE.
- **Status:** open. Rates left off our site until the year is clear.
