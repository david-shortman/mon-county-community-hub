# The state's Work Opportunity Tax Credit page gives no end date; the IRS page says new hires after 2025 are not covered

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while researching jobs after jail or prison (#215, sprint wave 16, #529)
- **What we found:** WorkForce West Virginia's page describes the Work Opportunity Tax Credit (WOTC) as a current program, with no end date. The IRS page says the credit is for people who begin work on or before December 31, 2025. The state page does not mention that date.
- **Evidence:** [WorkForce WV, Work Opportunity Tax Credit](https://workforcewv.org/job-seeker/specialized-services/work-opportunity-tax-credit/), read 2026-11-14: "Businesses can potentially receive a maximum credit anywhere from $1,200 to $9,600 depending on the qualified targeted group hired." [IRS, Work Opportunity Tax Credit](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/work-opportunity-tax-credit), marked updated July 20, 2026, read 2026-11-14: "The WOTC is available for wages paid to certain individuals who begin work on or before December 31, 2025."
- **Why it matters:** people coming home from prison, and employers who might hire them, may read the state page and expect a credit that the IRS page says does not cover 2026 hires.
- **Who keeps it:** WorkForce West Virginia (its WOTC page) and the IRS.
