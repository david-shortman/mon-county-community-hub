# The state's child care fee scale still uses 2024 poverty levels

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while adding who can get help paying for child care for the needs coverage lane (#1534, sprint wave 11, #529).
- **What we found:** The fee scale the state posts for Child Care Assistance (Appendix A) is titled "FY 2024 Federal Poverty Level" and the PDF is dated October 1, 2024. Its dollar limits match the 2024 federal poverty guidelines (for example $25,820 a year for a family of 3). We found no newer scale on the state's child care policy page. The Child Care Resource Center's page states the rule as a percent (150% to start, 185% to keep help), with no dollar amounts.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [Child Care Policy page](https://bfa.wv.gov/bfa-policy-and-plans/child-care-policy), [Appendix A, Sliding Fee Scale](https://bfa.wv.gov/media/6826/download?inline), [Child Care Resource Center, Families](https://ccrcwv.org/families/).
- **Why it matters:** A family checking the posted dollar limits may see an older, lower number than the one the center uses, if the scale has since been updated, and rule themselves out.
- **What we did:** `/families-and-kids/#child-care-who` shows the posted numbers, says the scale uses 2024 poverty levels, and tells readers to call the center before ruling themselves out.
- **Who keeps it:** WV Department of Human Services, Bureau for Family Assistance.
- **Status:** open. A person could ask the Child Care Resource Center which scale it uses today.
