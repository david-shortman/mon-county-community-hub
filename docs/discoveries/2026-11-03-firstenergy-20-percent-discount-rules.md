# Two FirstEnergy documents describe the 20% winter discount differently

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while adding the 20% winter discount to `/utility-bills/` (#1534, sprint wave 11, #529).
- **What we found:** State law (W. Va. Code §24-2A-1) gives the lower winter rate to gas and electric customers on SSI, WV WORKS (the law's older name is AFDC), or SNAP if 60 or older. Mon Power's 2025 bill notice lists SSI, WV WORKS and SNAP. FirstEnergy's "West Virginia Assistance Programs" PDF lists only "Must receive SNAP and be over the age of 60". Separately, two DoHS manual links (the full Income Maintenance Manual and its LIEAP chapter) returned 404 on 2026-11-02.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [W. Va. Code §24-2A-1](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/24-2A-1/), [Mon Power 2025 notice](https://www.firstenergycorp.com/content/dam/customer/billinserts/2025-10%20WV%20MP%20Low%20Income%20Discount.pdf), [FirstEnergy WV assistance PDF](https://www.firstenergycorp.com/content/dam/customer/get-help/files/asst-programs/West%20Virginia%20Assistance%20Programs.pdf), [DoHS manual chapter 21 link](https://bfa.wv.gov/media/40005/download?inline) (404).
- **Why it matters:** A person on SSI or WV WORKS who reads only the assistance PDF may think they do not qualify.
- **What we did:** the new card follows the law and Mon Power's notice.
- **Who keeps it:** FirstEnergy (Mon Power); WV DoHS for the manual links.
- **Status:** open. `/after-you-apply/lieap/` still cites the chapter 21 link; a person should find its new address.
