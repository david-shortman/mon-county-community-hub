# Several public grant programs for local nonprofits post no current round or dates

- **Found:** 2026-11-15, while adding funders to Grants for local nonprofits (#1982, sprint wave 20, #529)
- **What we found:** Four public grant programs that local nonprofits use do not show a current round on an official page.
  - **City of Morgantown CDBG.** The City's CDBG page lists the FY 2025 application (July 2025 to June 2026, due March 7, 2025) and the final FY 2026 Annual Action Plan. No FY 2026 or FY 2027 application or deadline is posted.
  - **Monongalia County opioid settlement grants.** Local news reports two rounds of county grants to agencies from the county's opioid settlement share. We found no page on the county's own site that describes the program, who can apply or when.
  - **West Virginia First Foundation.** Its Grant Cycles page names three grant types (Initial Opportunity, Momentum Initiative, Community Catalyst) with no dates or rules in the page text.
  - **Volunteer West Virginia.** Its funding page says "There are currently no funding opportunities," while its Start an AmeriCorps Program page says it gives funding each year.
- **Evidence:** [City of Morgantown, CDBG Info](https://www.morgantownwv.gov/707/CDBG-Info), read 2026-11-15. [FY 2025 CDBG application](https://www.morgantownwv.gov/DocumentCenter/View/6021/Morgantown-FY-2025-CDBG-Application-PDF), read 2026-11-15: "The deadline for submission of your application is: 4 p.m. Friday, March 7, 2025." [WV First Foundation, Grant Cycles](https://wvfirst.org/grant-cycles/), read 2026-11-15. [Volunteer WV, Grants](https://volunteer.wv.gov/Grants/Pages/default.aspx), read 2026-11-15.
- **Why it matters:** a small nonprofit cannot plan for these rounds from public pages. It has to call or watch for news.
- **Who keeps it:** the City of Morgantown, the Monongalia County Commission, the West Virginia First Foundation and Volunteer West Virginia.
- **Status:** open. The City CDBG and Volunteer WV programs are on the page with what their pages say. The county opioid grants and the First Foundation are left off. Tracked in #1987.
