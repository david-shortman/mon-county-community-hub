# Two state agencies post different guidance on religious exemptions for school shots

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while re-checking #387 (verification sweep 4, #529)
- **What we found:** The state Department of Health's exemption page says religious exemption requests "will be granted on a school year basis" and tells families to renew for 2026-2027. The state Board of Education's latest posted statement, from December 2, 2025, tells county boards of education not to accept religious exemptions until the state Supreme Court gives further guidance. We found no newer statement from either agency that settles which applies in schools now.
- **Evidence:** https://oeps.wv.gov/immunizations-religious-and-philosophical-exemptions (read 2026-11-01); https://wvde.us/articles/west-virginia-board-education-reinstates-policy-compulsory-vaccination-law (dated December 2, 2025, read 2026-11-01). Earlier entry on the shot charts: [2026-10-31-school-shot-exemption-chart.md](2026-10-31-school-shot-exemption-chart.md).
- **Why it matters:** A family can get an exemption letter from one agency and still not know whether their child's school will accept it.
- **Who keeps it:** The West Virginia Department of Health and the West Virginia Department of Education.
- **Status:** open
