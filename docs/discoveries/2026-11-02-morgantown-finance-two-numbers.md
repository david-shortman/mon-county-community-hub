# Two city pages give different Finance numbers for B&O tax

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while checking small business facts for #638 (sprint wave 10, #529).
- **What we found:** The City of Morgantown's Permitting, Licensing and Registration page says to call the Finance Department at 304-284-7401 about paying B&O tax. The Finance Department's own page lists 304-284-7408 and BandO@morgantownwv.gov. The same permitting page also gives 304-284-7408 for business license questions. No page says whether 7401 is a separate B&O line.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [Permitting, Licensing and Registration](https://www.morgantownwv.gov/747/Permitting-Licensing-Registration) ("Please contact the Finance Department at 304-284-7401 to gather information on paying your B&O fees"), [Finance](https://www.morgantownwv.gov/202/Finance) ("Phone: 304-284-7408").
- **Why it matters:** a new business owner calling about B&O tax may reach the wrong desk. Our own page briefly showed only one number (#1478) before we listed both.
- **Who keeps it:** City of Morgantown Finance Department and the city web team.
- **Status:** open. `/start-a-business/` shows both numbers and the email. A call to City Hall (304-284-7405) could settle which line handles B&O.
