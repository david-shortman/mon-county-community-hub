# The newest county estimate of adult reading and math skills is from surveys done in 2012 to 2017

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while scoring a learning need for the needs register (#1909, sprint wave 18, #529)
- **What we found:** The only county-level measure of adult reading and math skills is the National Center for Education Statistics' PIAAC Skills Map. Its Mon County estimate (16% of adults ages 16 to 74 at the lowest reading level, 26% in math) comes from surveys done from 2012 to 2017. The range is wide: 11% to 20% for reading. No newer county estimate was found.
- **Evidence:** [NCES PIAAC Skills Map](https://nces.ed.gov/surveys/piaac/skillsmap/), read 2026-11-14. The map's county file (`LandingPageEstimates_12122021`) has the Mon County row used here.
- **Why it matters:** the needs register's adult skills need rests on this number for how many people it affects. A count that is 9 to 14 years old cannot show whether things got better or worse, so the need's trend stays unknown.
- **Who keeps it:** the National Center for Education Statistics.
- **Status:** open. The number is on `/needs/` with its data years. Re-check when NCES publishes county estimates from the newer PIAAC survey.
