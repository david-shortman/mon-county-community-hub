# Mountain Line board: three official answers for when it meets

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while adding Mountain Line to `/have-your-say/` (sprint wave 16, ways to act, #529)
- **What we found:** Two official pages give three different meeting patterns for the Mountain Line Transit Authority board. The City's board page says the 3rd Wednesday of each month at noon at the Mountain Line depot in Westover. The County's boards page says the Authority "meets quarterly in January, April, July and October", and in the next sentence that "the board meets on the second Wednesday of each month at 6:00 p.m. at the transit station in Westover." Mountain Line's own comment rules say comment happens "during its monthly board meetings" but give no day or time.
- **Evidence:**
  - City of Morgantown, Transit Authority Board of Directors, https://www.morgantownwv.gov/692/Transit-Authority-Board-of-Directors, read 2026-11-14: "12:00 p.m. 3rd Wednesday of each month MountainLine Transit Depot - Westover"
  - Monongalia County, Authorities, Boards and Commissions, https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/authorities,_boards_and_commissions/index.php, read 2026-11-14 (quotes above)
  - Mountain Line, Board Meeting Public Comment Procedures, https://www.busride.org/board-meeting-public-comment-procedures, read 2026-11-14
- **Why it matters:** A rider who wants to speak at a board meeting cannot tell which day to come. `/have-your-say/` and `/who-decides/` show both pages' answers and say to call Mountain Line first.
- **Who keeps it:** the City of Morgantown (board page), the Monongalia County Commission (boards page), Mountain Line (its Board Meetings page)
- **Status:** open
