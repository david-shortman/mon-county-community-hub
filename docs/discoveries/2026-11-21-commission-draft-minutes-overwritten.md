# County Commission draft minutes are replaced at one address, and approved minutes are not posted online

- **Found:** 2026-11-21, while saving the government PDFs the hub cites to the Internet Archive (#2079)
- **What we found:** The County Clerk posts the County Commission's draft minutes as one file, always at the same address. Each new draft replaces the one before it, so only the newest draft can be read there. The Clerk's page lists one link, "Latest Draft". We found no page from the Clerk or the Commission that posts the minutes once the Commission approves them. The Internet Archive's Wayback Machine had saved only 8 different versions of the file from 2019 to 2026, the newest from February 13, 2026. The Commission meets most weeks, so most drafts from those years were never saved in a public place. That includes the drafts for the meetings of October 28, November 4 and November 10, 2026, which the hub read for its "What was decided" lists.
- **Evidence:**
  - The draft minutes file, read 2026-11-21: https://monongaliacountyclerk.com/myfiles/drafts/draft.pdf. The server gave it a last-modified date of November 21, 2026, so it had been replaced since the hub read the November 10 draft on November 14.
  - The Clerk's minutes page, read 2026-11-21: https://monongaliacountyclerk.com/index.php/21-additional-info/48-commission-meetings-minutes-draft. It says "The County Commission regular session meetings draft of minutes" and links only "Latest Draft". It has no approved minutes and no older drafts.
  - The Commission's page, read 2026-11-21: https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/commission/index.php. It has no link to minutes.
  - Wayback Machine CDX lookup, 2026-11-21: https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=monongaliacountyclerk.com/myfiles/drafts/draft.pdf&collapse=digest. It lists 8 different files, saved 2019-09-18, 2021-01-26, 2023-03-20, 2024-07-03, 2024-10-04, 2024-12-03, 2025-10-12 and 2026-02-13. The hub saved a ninth on 2026-11-21.
  - The hub's November 4 minutes record (`content/minutes/county-commission-2026-11-04.yaml`) notes that the November 10 draft records the November 4 minutes as approved, but "the approved copy is not posted online".
- **Why it matters:** Minutes are the official record of what the Commission voted on. When only the newest draft is online, a resident who looks a week later finds a different meeting, and there is no way to check an older one or to see what changed between the draft and the approved minutes. Anyone tracking a decision over time, such as a budget item or an appointment, has to ask the Clerk's office for a copy.
- **Who keeps it:** Monongalia County Clerk (the minutes page and file); Monongalia County Commission (approves the minutes).
- **Status:** open. Since 2026-11-21 the hub's "Save overwritten PDFs" check (`.github/workflows/save-pdfs.yml`) asks the Internet Archive to save each new version of the file within six hours of seeing it change. All saved versions: https://web.archive.org/web/*/https://monongaliacountyclerk.com/myfiles/drafts/draft.pdf. This keeps drafts from now on. It cannot bring back the drafts that were replaced before, and it does not cover approved minutes, which are not online.
