# No local page says how to file for a protective order after hours

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, building the protective orders page (#1187, part of #529)
- **What we found:** The courts' statewide brochure says magistrates are on call 24 hours a day, but no official Monongalia County page says where to go or whom to call after hours. No court page gives the dollar amount of costs in a domestic violence case; only Legal Aid's 2023 page does. The current petition form says a denial appeal is filed at the Circuit Clerk's office, while the brochure and Legal Aid point to the magistrate's office. The courts' Monongalia page lists no office hours. FRIS and RDVIC's own site give different ZIP codes and emails for RDVIC. The Monongalia video hearing form shows "Approved: TBA".
- **Evidence:** https://www.courtswv.gov/sites/default/pubfilesmnt/2023-06/DV_Brochure_English_2019.pdf, https://www.courtswv.gov/sites/default/pubfilesmnt/2026-04/MDVPETNCombo.pdf, https://legalaidwv.org/legal-information/domestic-violence-protective-orders/, https://www.courtswv.gov/public-resources/court-information-county/monongalia_county, https://fris.org/centers/rdvic/, https://www.rdvic.org/contact, https://www.courtswv.gov/sites/default/pubfilesmnt/2026-03/MDVNORP-Monongalia.pdf, all checked 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** A person in danger at night needs to know where to go, and anyone filing needs to know what it may cost.
- **Who keeps it:** Supreme Court of Appeals of West Virginia; Monongalia County Magistrate Court; RDVIC
- **Status:** open (#1234)
