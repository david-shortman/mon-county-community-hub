# Sources for parents of kids with disabilities are blocked, old or disagree

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, building the page for parents of kids with disabilities (#1183, part of #529)
- **What we found:** The state's Parent Training and Information center site (wvpti-inc.org) and all of ssa.gov, including its PDF pamphlets and office locator, refuse automated reads (403). WVDE and the national Parent Center Hub give different addresses for the parent center. The Children with Special Health Care Needs program's pages are still on the old DHHR site, one is an "under construction" page, and its two toll-free numbers disagree. The CDCSP age limit is "under 19" in the BMS brochure and "under 18" on the I/DD Waiver site. The I/DD Waiver site points to a "Division's website" and a "CDCSP website" without working links. WVDE and BBH both link to ddc.wv.gov/Pages/default.aspx, which is a 404. WVDE's state complaint page says emailed complaints are not accepted, and also gives a staff email for complaints.
- **Evidence:** https://www.wvpti-inc.org/, https://www.ssa.gov/pubs/EN-05-10026.pdf, https://wvde.us/academics/special-education/additional-resources-families, https://www.wvdhhr.org/cshcn/, https://www.wvdhhr.org/cshcn/faq.asp, https://bms.wv.gov/media/40926/download?inline, https://iddwprogram.wv.gov/, https://ddc.wv.gov/Pages/default.aspx, https://wvde.us/academics/special-education/accountability-compliance/idea-dispute-resolution/state-complaint-process, all checked 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** Parents of a child with a disability may not be able to tell which number or rule is current, or which office to call.
- **Who keeps it:** West Virginia Parent Training and Information; U.S. Social Security Administration; WV Department of Education; WV DoHS Bureau for Public Health and Bureau for Medical Services
- **Status:** open (#1225)
