# No page says where to get the Driver Education Card

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while checking driver education for #592 (sprint wave 10, #529).
- **What we found:** The DMV's graduated license page says the 50 hours of practice can be "certified by parent or legal guardian or Driver Education Card," but no DMV or school page says where a teen gets that card, or whether any Mon County high school offers driver education.
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [DMV graduated drivers licensing](https://dmv.wv.gov/graduated-drivers-licensing-gdl).
- **Why it matters:** Parents cannot tell whether driver education is an option here.
- **Who keeps it:** WV DMV and Monongalia County Schools.
- **Status:** open. Needs a call to Mon County Schools, 304-291-9210.
