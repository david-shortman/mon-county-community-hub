# The Fairmont-Morgantown Housing Authority's Administrative Plan and ACOP are not posted online

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while building the after-you-apply page for Section 8 and public housing (#529, #1213)
- **What we found:** The federal rules leave key applicant steps, like how many days you have to ask for an informal review after a denial, to each housing authority's Administrative Plan (Section 8) and Admissions and Continued Occupancy Policy (ACOP, public housing). FMHA's Document Center lists its applications, lease, grievance procedure and other forms, but not these two plans. Its public housing grievance procedure is labeled "Appendix D" with page number 215, which suggests it comes from a longer document that is not posted.
- **Evidence:** https://fmhousing.com/document_center.php, read 2026-11-01 ("The Violence Against Women Act is incorporated as part of the Agency Admissions & Continued Occupancy Policy (ACOP)"); https://fmhousing.com/Public%20Housing%20Grievance%20Procedure.pdf, read 2026-11-01; 24 CFR 982.554(b) ("The Administrative Plan must state the PHA procedures for conducting an informal review").
- **Why it matters:** An applicant who is denied can't look up the deadline to ask for a review before the letter arrives, or check it if the letter is lost.
- **Who keeps it:** Fairmont-Morgantown Housing Authority
- **Status:** open
