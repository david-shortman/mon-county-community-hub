# Morgantown 2033 page still shows the 2023 schedule

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while building the plans and budgets time strip (#1444)
- **What we found:** The city's Morgantown 2033 page still says the city "has started updating" its comprehensive plan and lists "November 7th – City Council – Ordinance 2nd Reading and Public Hearing". The Council agenda for December 19, 2023 lists the plan's second reading on that date instead. The plan PDF names a different date for the same step ("November 8, 2023") and its cover reads "ADOPTED XX 2023". No official page we found states the adoption date in plain text.
- **Evidence:**
  - https://www.morgantownwv.gov/652/Morgantown-2033 (read 2026-11-02)
  - https://www.morgantownwv.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_12192023-2801 (read 2026-11-02): "(Second Reading) ... to adopt the 2023 Comprehensive Plan (Morgantown 2033) (First reading 10/3/2023)"
  - https://www.morgantownwv.gov/DocumentCenter/View/5492/Morgantown-2033-Comprehensive-Plan-FINAL (read 2026-11-02)
- **Why it matters:** Residents looking for when the city's long-range plan took effect, or when the next update is due, find dates that disagree.
- **Who keeps it:** City of Morgantown Development Services (Planning)
- **Status:** open
