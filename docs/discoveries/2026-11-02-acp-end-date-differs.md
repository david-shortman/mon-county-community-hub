# Two pages give different end dates for the Affordable Connectivity Program

- **Found:** 2026-11-02, while checking internet help for remote workers for #665 (sprint wave 10, #529).
- **What we found:** USAC, which ran the program, says "The ACP ended on June 1, 2024, due to a lack of additional funding from Congress." Frontier's affordability page says it "ended in April 2024." The FCC's own ACP page could not be read (it refuses automated reads).
- **Evidence:** read 2026-11-02: [USAC ACP page](https://www.usac.org/about/affordable-connectivity-program/), [Frontier affordability programs](https://frontier.com/discount-programs/affordability-programs).
- **Why it matters:** People who remember the ACP may look for it or trust old advice.
- **Who keeps it:** Frontier (its page); USAC and the FCC.
- **Status:** open. Not on our site yet.
