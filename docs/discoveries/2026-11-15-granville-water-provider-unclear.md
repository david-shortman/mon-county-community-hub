# Granville's own pages do not say clearly who supplies its water

- **Found:** 2026-11-15, while checking every row on /who-serves-me/ against its sources for #2008 (sprint wave 21, #529).
- **What we found:** the Town of Granville's Community and Contact pages list the Morgantown Utility Board (MUB) under "Public Utilities" and say trash pickup is set up through MUB and "billed with water and sewer services." They do not say MUB supplies the town's water. The same site's FAQ (on the Contact and Pay Online pages) says "Our city provides a wide range of services to residents, including ... water and sewer." So one page points to MUB and another says the town provides water and sewer. MUB's own site does not list the towns it serves.
- **Evidence:** https://townofgranvillewv.gov/community/, https://townofgranvillewv.gov/contact/, https://townofgranvillewv.gov/pay-online/ and https://mub.org/, all read 2026-11-15.
- **Why it matters:** someone moving to Granville cannot tell from official pages whether to call MUB or the town to start water service.
- **Who keeps it:** the Town of Granville and the Morgantown Utility Board.
- **Status:** open. We took MUB off the Granville water line on /who-serves-me/ and the Granville town page, and point to the town office instead. A person can check this on #2008.

For contrast, the City of Westover (https://www.westoverwv.org/utilities) and Star City (https://www.starcitywv.com/) both name MUB for water and sewer on their own pages, read 2026-11-15.
