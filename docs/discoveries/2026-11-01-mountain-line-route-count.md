# Mountain Line's pages give two route counts

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, re-checking health, family and benefits listings (#529)
- **What we found:** Mountain Line's home page says it runs 22 routes. Its route list page says 24.
- **Evidence:** https://www.busride.org/ and https://www.busride.org/Maps-Schedules, both read 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** A small thing, but riders and anyone describing the system can't tell which is current.
- **Who keeps it:** Mountain Line Transit Authority
- **Status:** open
