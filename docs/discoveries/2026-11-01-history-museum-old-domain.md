# An old Morgantown History Museum address now goes to an unrelated site

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, while looking for holiday events (#529)
- **What we found:** `morgantownhistorymuseum.org` redirects to unrelated sites (on 2026-11-01, to `yorknailsbanstead.com`, which showed gambling spam). The museum's current site is `themorgantownhistorymuseum.com`, and the City's museum page (`morgantownwv.gov/634`) points there.
- **Evidence:** `https://morgantownhistorymuseum.org/`, read 2026-11-01. We did not link it anywhere.
- **Why it matters:** Anyone who types the old address or follows an old link lands on a spam site instead of the museum.
- **Who keeps it:** the Morgantown History Museum and the City of Morgantown
- **Status:** open
