# County pages give two start years for the dilapidated property agency

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, re-checking board listings (lane B re-check, #529)
- **What we found:** The county's Authorities, Boards and Commissions page says the Abandoned and Dilapidated Property Enforcement Agency was created September 4, 1997. The agency's own county page says it was created "first in 1998" and now runs under County Ordinance 2004-01.
- **Evidence:** https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/authorities,_boards_and_commissions/index.php and https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/departments/abandoned_dilapidated_property_enforcement_agency.php, both read 2026-11-01.
- **Why it matters:** Residents and researchers looking up the agency's history get two answers from the same county site.
- **Who keeps it:** Monongalia County Commission
- **Status:** open
