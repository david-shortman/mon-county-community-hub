# West Virginia WIC's posted processing time policy is dated 1997

- **Found:** 2026-11-01, writing the WIC "after you apply" page (#1158, part of #1148)
- **What we found:** WIC Policy 2.05, Time Frames for Processing Applicants, has an effective date of 08/01/97 and names a computer system (STORC) that newer policies replace with Crossroads. It is still the version posted.
- **Evidence:** https://dhhr.wv.gov/WIC/policyprocedure/Documents/2.05%20Time%20Frames%20for%20Processing%20Applicants.pdf (read 2026-10-31); compare Policy 2.09, effective 4/21/2021, which names Crossroads.
- **Why it matters:** Readers can't tell whether the 10 and 20 day time frames are current, though they match the federal WIC rule's pattern.
- **Who keeps it:** West Virginia WIC (WV Office of Nutrition Services)
- **Status:** open, no issue yet; the WIC page quotes the policy and notes its date in the PR
