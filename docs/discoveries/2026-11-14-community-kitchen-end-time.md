# Community Kitchen lunch: two pages give different end times

- **Found:** 2026-11-14, while building the food by day list (#60, #1938)
- **What we found:** The Community Kitchen's own site says lunch runs to 12:15 p.m. The page about it on Trinity Episcopal Church's site, where lunch is served, says it ends at noon. Both say it starts at 11 a.m., Monday to Friday.
- **Evidence:** https://www.morgantowncommunitykitchen.com/ (read 2026-11-14): "Normal Operating Hours: M-F 11:00 - 12:15". https://trinitymorgantown.org/mck/ (read 2026-11-14): "Lunch is served Monday-Friday from 11:00 a.m. – 12:00 Noon".
- **Why it matters:** Someone arriving after noon cannot tell from the public pages whether lunch is still being served. The site lists the start time only.
- **Who keeps it:** Community Kitchen, Inc. and Trinity Episcopal Church
- **Status:** open
