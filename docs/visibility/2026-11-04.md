# Visibility audit: 2026-11-04

The second weekly visibility run. Method: the weekly visibility prompt, with the rules in [README.md](README.md) and [answer-pages.md](answer-pages.md). Live site checked: https://everythingmorgantown.org/ (the custom domain from #89). Last week: [2026-10-28](2026-10-28.md).

## Summary

- **Biggest gap: still not in search results.** None of the 10 resident searches below showed one of our pages, and a search for "everythingmorgantown.org" itself found only other Morgantown sites. The site is ready; Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools are still not set up, so the sitemap has never been submitted (#89, David).
- **Fixed in this run:** every page carried the hidden "This site is not being kept up right now" notice as plain text in its HTML. People never saw it, but an AI fetch tool reading our stay-tonight page quoted it as the page's first three sentences and answered that the site is not maintained. The words now wait in a `<template>` until the notice really turns on.
- **For David:** `https://www.everythingmorgantown.org` shows a browser security warning (see "Technical checks"). Filed as a `for:david` issue.

**What AI tools would likely cite, and why.** The AI summaries leaned on pages that state a phone number and a rule in plain words, even when old: a WVU resource page for bills and rides, Republic Services for trash day, directory sites for pantries. On SNAP, the summary could not find the state's own application page and fell back to directories. On shelter tonight and flu shots, every source was a news story, and the summary said it could not confirm anything current. Our /snap/, /stay-tonight/ and /flu-shots/ pages answer those directly with sources and dates, so they are the strongest citation candidates once search engines have them.
