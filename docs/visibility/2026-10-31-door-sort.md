# Door sort: the 32 findability questions (#254)

A first test of the door names. For each question in [`content/findability/questions.yaml`](../../content/findability/questions.yaml), we asked: which door would a resident open, going by the name alone? Then we checked which door the site's own topic tags put it in ([`content/doors.yaml`](../../content/doors.yaml), as of 2026-10-31). Where the two differ, the name may be hiding a page.

This is a desk test by an agent, not a test with residents. Resident testing stays with David (#237). Treat it as a list of questions to ask people, not as an answer.

## Counts

- **Clear:** 19 of 32. The name and the tags agree, and no other door competes.
- **Fits two doors:** 6 (q06, q14, q18, q22, q24, q29). Two doors is fine if both link to the page.
- **Weak:** 2 (q11, q17). The name only works if you read the hint.
- **Misfit or disagreement:** 5 (q09, q10, q27, q30, q32).
- **Fits no door by name:** small business (q09, q10).

Your community draws the most questions (meetings, voting, volunteering, data). Family and life changes is no one's first choice here, but this question set has few family, school, work or aging questions, so that says more about the set than the name. A later run should add questions for those areas.

## What the sort suggests

For David to decide (door names are his call, #248). None of these is a change to the site.

1. **Move voting to Your community.** Residents look for voting under community or government, not "Living here". This is a tag change in `content/doors.yaml` (move `voting` from living-here to community), not a rename.
2. **Give small business a home.** Today business entries are tagged `work`, so they sit under "Family and life changes", which a business owner is unlikely to open. Options: a `business` topic under Your community ("start something" is already in its hint), or under Living here. Either is cheaper than a sixth door.
3. **"Get through this month" fits short-term needs well but hides long-term housing.** A housing voucher or affordable housing search (q27) is a months-long wait. Options: keep the name and add "rent and housing" to the visible hint (it is already a topic label), or rename toward the plan doc's "Money and bills" test name, which fits bills and SNAP but fits housing no better.
4. **Keep "Get care" and "Living here".** Both drew their questions cleanly. The test name "New here" would lose trash day, recycling and buses (q19 to q21, q28), which long-time residents ask about too.
5. **Keep "Your community" over "Have a say".** "Have a say" fits meetings and voting (q12, q13, q15, q30 to q32) but would lose volunteering, giving and the "how we're doing" data (q16 to q18, q22 to q24).
6. **Add a transit topic tag** so bus pages (q28, q29) land in Living here by tag, not only by hint.
