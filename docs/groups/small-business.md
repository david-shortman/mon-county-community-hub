## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **One Stop Business Portal** (WV Secretary of State). The state's online place to register a new business. The Secretary of State says "The fastest, easiest way to register a new business is to file online" there. You create a user account and log in. Paper forms are also offered by business type. Filing fees listed: for-profit corporation $100, LLC/PLLC $100, limited partnership $100, limited liability partnership $250, non-profit corporation $25, and others; online filings add a $1.00 processing fee. Veteran-owned businesses and businesses owned by West Virginia resident young entrepreneurs (ages 18 to 29) may have the initial Secretary of State fee waived (that fee only). Source: https://sos.wv.gov/register-new-wv-business, checked October 30, 2026. Portal: https://business4.wv.gov/
- **Business Registration Certificate** (WV State Tax Department, Tax Division). "Any person who is engaging in any business activity in the state of West Virginia must register" with the Tax Division before starting. The fee is $30.00. Some groups do not pay the fee but still need a certificate, including qualifying 501 nonprofits and "Persons engaged in agriculture or farming" (see the full list at the link). The One Stop portal registers you with the Tax Division, the Secretary of State and WorkForce West Virginia; to register with the Tax Division only, use Form WV/BUS-APP. The certificate does not expire, but stops being valid if the legal name, location or ownership changes. It must be posted where you do business. Tax help: (304) 558-3333 or (800) 982-8297. Source: https://tax.wv.gov/Business/BusinessRegistration/Pages/BusinessRegistrationQuestionsAndAnswers.aspx, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Steps to Starting Your Business** (City of Morgantown). The city's own checklist, in this order: (1) check zoning with the Planning Division, 304-284-7431 (parcel and tax map number from the Monongalia County Assessor, 304-291-7222, or the WV Property Viewer); (2) get the state license through the One Stop Business Portal; (3) after you have the State Business License Registration Certificate, apply for a Municipal Business License in the Morgantown Access Portal; a Certificate of Occupancy is required to get the license inside city limits; (4) apply for a Certificate of Occupancy: business license application, a scaled floor plan, and a $65 application fee (cash, check or credit), with Planning and Engineering signatures. Contact Code Enforcement and the Fire Marshal "no less than one week before opening day." Restaurants, bars and nightclubs have stricter Fire Code rules. Forms on the page include a Home Occupation Application Packet, Outdoor Dining Application, and contractor application. City Hall: 389 Spruce Street, Morgantown, WV 26505, 304-284-7405, Monday through Thursday, 7 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. Source: https://www.morgantownwv.gov/247/Steps-to-Starting-Your-Business, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Business licenses and the Morgantown Access Portal** (City of Morgantown). "You must create an account and complete your application through the Morgantown Access Portal." The portal address has changed, so old bookmarks may not work. Finance Department numbers on the page: 304-284-7401 (B&O fees) and 304-284-7408 (to get a Morgantown Municipal License added to the portal). B&O taxes are separate from building permit fees. No license fee amount is listed on this page. Source: https://www.morgantownwv.gov/747/Permitting-Licensing-Registration, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Business and Occupation (B&O) Tax Returns** (City of Morgantown Finance). The city B&O tax is on commercial activity inside city limits. The page offers an Annual return, a "By the Job" return, and a Quarterly return (PDF forms), plus a B&O Tax Worksheet in Excel for your records. The page does not say which return applies to whom, the due dates, or how to pay. Same City Hall contact as above. Source: https://www.morgantownwv.gov/204/Business-Occupation-Tax-Returns, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Vending Downtown** (City of Morgantown). Vending trucks and carts need a City of Morgantown Business License, even if based outside city limits. "A permit is not needed if you are selling on private property with the permission of the landowner." Finance Department for vending questions: 304-284-7423. Source: https://www.morgantownwv.gov/256/Vending-Downtown, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Star City business registration and B&O forms** (Town of Star City). The town has a Business Registration Application (v.2025): print it and return it in person or by mail, or email it to info@starcitywv.com and pay on the town site's Payments tab. "The completed application and payment in full must be received for the license to be processed." A B&O Tax Form (v.2025) is printed and returned to the office. The pages do not list fees, who must file, or due dates. Business Office: 370 Broadway Ave, Star City, WV 26505, 304-599-3550, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday through Friday. A separate Contractor Business Registrations page exists (not opened). Sources: https://www.starcitywv.com/business-registrations/ and https://www.starcitywv.com/bo-tax-forms/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Granville business license and B&O tax** (Town of Granville). "The first step to opening up shop in Granville is getting your business license." B&O tax is "a business' gross receipts tax levied by the Town of Granville for the Privilege of doing business," and rates depend on the type of work or goods. "Any business providing goods or services in the Town of Granville, must also apply for a business or contractors license." Rate questions: finance office, 304-599-5080. Business and contractor license applications are linked. Source: https://townofgranvillewv.gov/business/, checked October 30, 2026 (the page loaded with curl; the WebFetch tool returned it empty).
- **WV Small Business Development Center (WV SBDC)** (state program). Coaches give "confidential, no-cost technical assistance" to start and grow a business: business planning, funding, marketing, financial analysis, government contracting and more. A Business Fundamentals workshop is for people starting a business or in their first year. Sign up as a client at https://wvsbdc.ecenterdirect.com/signup, or call 888-982-7232, or email AskMe@wv.gov. The site does not say which coach or office serves Monongalia County. Source: https://wvsbdc.com/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Small Business Development Center page** (City of Morgantown). The city says "The WVSBDC offers Business Fundamentals classes regularly in Morgantown." Each 3-hour seminar covers how to apply for a business loan, writing a business plan, and choosing a legal structure. Dates are on the WV SBDC training calendar, https://wvsbdc.ecenterdirect.com/events. (The address and phone on this page are City Hall's, not an SBDC office.) Source: https://www.morgantownwv.gov/255/Small-Business-Development-Center, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Morgantown Area Partnership** (business organization). Describes itself as "a public/private alliance for the betterment of economic, business and community development in the area." It "encompasses four entities including the Morgantown Area Chamber of Commerce, the Monongalia County Development Authority, the Morgantown Area Economic Partnership, and the Campus Neighborhoods Revitalization Corporation (Sunnyside Up)." Address: 265 Spruce Street, Suite 100, Morgantown, WV 26505. Phone: 304-292-3311. Sources: https://morgantownpartnership.com/morgantown-area-partnership/who-we-are/ and https://morgantownpartnership.com/contact/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Morgantown Area Chamber of Commerce** (part of the Partnership). A membership group offering networking events, professional development, policy advocacy and business resources. The page does not list fees or who can join. Source: https://morgantownpartnership.com/chamber-of-commerce/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Economic Development Services** (Morgantown Area Partnership). Lists "business retention, expansion and site location services." No loans or small business programs are named on the page. Source: https://morgantownpartnership.com/economic-development/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Main Street Morgantown** (nonprofit). "A 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization" that since 1984 has been "a steward of welcoming, vibrant and prosperous Downtown and Wharf Districts." The page names no grants or startup help. The old downtownmorgantown.com "Starting a Business?" page now redirects here. Source: https://morgantownpartnership.com/main-street-morgantown/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **SBA West Virginia District Office** (U.S. Small Business Administration). "Serving all 55 counties in West Virginia." Helps with "SBA services including funding programs, counseling, federal contracting certifications, and disaster recovery," and connects businesses to partners and lenders. 320 W. Pike St., Suite 330, Clarksburg, WV 26301. 304-623-5631. Source: https://legacy.sba.gov/district/west-virginia, checked October 30, 2026 (the sba.gov address redirects to this legacy page).
- **Business Plan Assistance list** (WV Secretary of State). A list of places to get business plan help (SBA, USDA Rural Development, WV SBDC, WVU Extension and others), with a note that the list does "not imply endorsement." No costs are given. Source: https://sos.wv.gov/business-plan-assistance, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Food permits** (Monongalia County Health Department, Environmental Health). Applications posted for: food establishments, plan review, temporary food events, bake sales and nonprofits, farmers market vendors (with a vendor guide), and mobile food units, plus a "Permit Fees and Fees for Service, Fiscal Year 2026-2027" PDF and online payment. The page does not explain the steps. Environmental Health: (304) 598-5131. Source: https://www.monchd.org/departments-programs/environmental-health, checked October 30, 2026.
