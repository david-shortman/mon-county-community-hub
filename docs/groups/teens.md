## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **Child labor rules for ages 14 to 17** (WV Division of Labor, Wage and Hour). A 14 or 15 year old needs an age certificate before starting a job. It needs "promise of a job, parental consent, verification the minor is attending school, and proof of age." The employer, parent and school registrar fill out Sections A, B and C; the Division of Labor fills out Section D and issues it. Homeschool parents fill out the school section. Hours for 14 and 15 year olds: when school is in session, no work before 7:00am or after 7:00pm, no more than 3 hours on a school day and 18 hours in a school week, and no work during school hours. When school is out, no work before 7:00am or after 9:00pm, up to 8 hours a day and 40 hours a week. A 14 year old may not be a lifeguard or swim instructor; a 15 year old may be a lifeguard if certified. 16 and 17 year olds may work the same hours as adults; a certificate is generally not required, but an employer may ask for one as proof of age. No one under 18 may work in a bar or serve or sell alcohol. Contact: wageandhour@wv.gov, 1-304-558-7890. Sources: https://labor.wv.gov/wage-hour/child-labor and https://labor.wv.gov/wage-hour/child-labor/child-labor-faq, checked October 30, 2026. Forms "Age Certificate (14-15 year old)" and "Age Certificate (16-17 year old)" are linked from the child labor page (relative paths /media/280/download and /media/281/download); I did not open the PDFs.
- **Graduated Driver Licensing** (WV Division of Motor Vehicles). Level 1 instruction permit: at least 15 and under 18, knowledge test ($7.50 per attempt), parent or guardian signs if under 18. A licensed driver 21 or older in the front seat, driving only 5:00 am to 10:00 pm, up to two non-family passengers besides the supervising adult, no phone use. Level 2 intermediate license: at least 16, held Level 1 for six months with no infractions, road skills test ($7.50), a 50 hour certification form (10 hours at night) signed by a parent or a Driver Education Card. Passenger and night limits apply. Level 3 full license: at least 17, one year at Level 2 with no infractions, $5.00 per year until age 21. Source: https://dmv.wv.gov/graduated-drivers-licensing-gdl, checked October 30, 2026 (no last-updated date on the page).
- **Career Connections, WIOA youth services** (Region VI Workforce Development Board, run under contract by Ross Innovative Employment Solutions, Ross IES). For out-of-school youth ages 16 to 24 in the region who are at risk. Services can include tutoring and alternative high school options, paid and unpaid work experience (including summer or school-year jobs, internships, job shadowing, pre-apprenticeships), skills training toward a credential, mentoring, and follow-up. An appointment with a career specialist decides eligibility. The career specialist for Marion, Monongalia, Harrison, Doddridge, Taylor and Preston counties is reachable at 304-844-1697. Sources: https://regionviwv.org/youth/youth-servies-career-connections/ and https://regionviwv.org/youth/youth-work-experience/, checked October 30, 2026. (The site menu also calls it "Youth Ready." The pages name a staff person; list the role and phone, not the name.)
- **WIOA Youth Program, statewide** (WorkForce West Virginia). Ages 14 to 24 at enrollment, with at least one barrier to school or work; low income applies to in-school youth. Free. Delivered through WV American Job Centers; 1-800-252-JOBS. Source: https://workforcewv.org/job-seeker/workforce-development/youth-program/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Region VI Workforce Development Board** office: 17 Middletown Rd., Whitehall, WV 26554, 304-368-9530. Source: https://workforcewv.org/businesses/workforce-development/workforce-development-board/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Junior Volunteer (JV) Program** (The Shack Neighborhood House). Summer program where teens help with roles like counselor's aide, kitchen assistant, swim instructor assistant, maintenance and concessions, plus community service (nursing home visits, park and trail cleanups, community garden, food security). One-time $75.00 fee for the summer. Incentives need at least 25 service hours. Apply online at the-shack.org/civicrm/jv-application/; spots are first come, and you are on the list only after the fee and paperwork are in. The page does not state ages or dates. Phone (304) 599-5466, Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM; 537 Blue Horizon Drive, Morgantown, WV 26501. Source: https://the-shack.org/teen-programs/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Teen programs at the library** (Morgantown Public Library System). Teens can borrow books, manga and video games, get homework help, and come to tween and teen programs. Programs listed for teens include Teen Anime Club, Teen Hangout, Dungeons and Dragons (Adventurer's Guild and Teen D&D), Trading Card Game Group, NASA's AstroCamp, and the all-ages Summer Reading Program. Free online help: TutorWV (1 to 1 tutoring and test prep, live 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.), Learning Express Library, and Job and Career Accelerator (resumes, interview practice; call 304-291-7425 for login). No days or times on the page. Source: https://mympls.org/programs/teens/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Volunteering at the library** (Morgantown Public Library System). Volunteers are taken "on an as-needed basis"; watch social media and the newsletter for calls. No minimum age stated. Source: https://mympls.org/about/volunteer/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Youth career tools** listed by Region VI: My Next Move, O*NET, CareerOneStop videos, Pathways to the Future (students and young adults with disabilities), College for West Virginia (scholarships and financial aid planning). Source: https://regionviwv.org/youth/youth-resources/, checked October 30, 2026.
