## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **WIC** (Monongalia County Health Department). Free nutrition and breastfeeding support, health education, healthy food packages, nutrition counseling, health screenings and referrals, and lead testing. For pregnant, postpartum and breastfeeding mothers, and children up to their fifth birthday, who financially qualify. The page says "Mothers on Medicaid and foster parents automatically qualify." Phone (304) 598-5181, toll free 1-800-675-5181. Serves Monongalia, Preston, Marion, Harrison, Doddridge and Taylor counties. Source: https://www.monchd.org/departments-programs/wic, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WIC Morgantown office location** (Monongalia County Health Department). 1311 Pineview Drive, White Birch Tower II, Suite 400, Morgantown, WV 26505. Phone 304-598-5181 or 800-675-5181. Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Evening hours 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. the first Tuesday of the month. Source: https://www.monchd.org/departments-programs/pages/mchd-wic-locations, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WIC eligibility and how to apply** (Monongalia County Health Department). Pregnant women; women breastfeeding an infant up to the first birthday; postpartum women up to six months after delivery or end of pregnancy; infants up to the first birthday; children from age one until the fifth birthday. Must also have a nutrition risk, assessed at the clinic. Household income no more than 185% of the federal poverty income guidelines; families on Medicaid, WV Works or SNAP, and foster families, automatically qualify; if pregnant, add one person to household size. Income table "Effective May 15, 2026" is an image (not read). Apply: call (304) 598-5181 for an appointment, or apply online through the linked state WIC site. Source: https://www.monchd.org/departments-programs/pages/wic-eligibility-guidelines, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WIC breastfeeding support and free classes** (Monongalia County Health Department). Trained WIC counselors, phone consultations, the Pacify app for clients, a 24/7 app through the Appalachian Breastfeeding Network open to the public, breast pumps for active WIC participants based on need and availability (WIC does not fill insurance pump prescriptions). Free breastfeeding classes are open to the public, including people not on WIC. Monongalia classes: first and third Wednesday, 4 p.m.; second Wednesday, 10 a.m. (page says call ahead). Phone (304) 598-5181. Source: https://www.monchd.org/services/breastfeeding, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WIC hospital liaison** (Monongalia County Health Department). WIC staff visit new mothers in the hospital to enroll them in WIC. Started at WVU Medicine Golisano Children's in 2023; added Vandalia Health Mon Medical Center in mid-April 2026. Source: https://www.monchd.org/newsroom/mchd-wic-s-hospital-liaison-program-continues-to-grow (news post dated June 24, 2026), checked October 30, 2026.
- **WIC postpartum mood screening** (Monongalia County Health Department). WIC staff screen pregnant and postpartum mothers through the most recent child's first birthday for perinatal mood and anxiety disorders, and screen fathers too. Staff do not diagnose; they refer at-risk families to mental health professionals. Source: https://www.monchd.org/newsroom/mchd-wic-staffers-now-screening-clients-for-postpartum-mood-disorders (news post dated August 24, 2026), checked October 30, 2026.
- **Child Care Resource Center (CCRC)** (Catholic Charities West Virginia; the Child Care Resource and Referral agency for this county). Serves families and programs in Hancock, Brooke, Ohio, Marshall, Tyler, Wetzel, Monongalia and Marion Counties. Morgantown office: 20 Scott Avenue, Suite 302, Morgantown, WV 26508. Phone 304-292-7357. Hours: M-F 8:30am-4:30pm (page's wording). Child care assistance intake is walk-in only ("We do not schedule appointments"), first come, first served; plan for at least 45 minutes with a case manager; pick or enroll your child's provider first if you can; 13 days to turn in verifications. Source: https://ccrcwv.org/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Child care assistance (subsidy) rules** (CCRC families page). Parent must live with the child and be working, in school, or in WV Works, and within the income limit: "must first meet the income guidelines based at 150% of the poverty level"; after services start, income may rise to "185% of the national poverty level." If both parents live in the home, or the parent is married, both must be in a qualifying activity. Up to 90 days of care after a job loss or finishing school while looking for work. Care is paid only for approved times. Certificate good for one year. Parent fee is a sliding scale by monthly gross income, per day, per child. Report changes within 5 days. CCRC can give a list of licensed centers and registered in-home providers on request. Source: https://ccrcwv.org/families/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Child Care Assistance program** (WV DoHS Bureau for Family Assistance). Subsidies for certified family-based or licensed center-based care for working parents or, in some cases, parents in school, for "families who meet income requirements." Says the assistance "covers the majority of the cost of direct care." CCR&R agencies manage the subsidy program and link parents with child care options. Source: https://bfa.wv.gov/bfa-programs/child-care-assistance and https://bfa.wv.gov/child-care-resource-and-referral, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Help Me Grow** (WV state program, Office of Maternal, Child and Family Health). A "FREE referral service" for children birth through eight years. Parents can call to reach a care coordinator who talks with them about how their child is doing, mails a developmental screening (ASQ-3), and connects them to resources. Email dhomcfhhmg@wv.gov. No phone number on the current page. Source: https://dhhr.wv.gov/helpmegrow/ and https://dhhr.wv.gov/helpmegrow/families/Pages/default.aspx, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Car seat fitting stations** (WV DMV Governor's Highway Safety Program list). Two in Mon County: Monongalia County Sheriff's Department, 116 Walnut Street, Morgantown, WV 26505, (304) 291-7260; WVU Medicine Children's Injury Prevention and Safety Program, 1 Medical Center Drive, Morgantown, WV 26506, (304) 554-8519. Page says call the station to schedule an appointment and not to contact stations through social media. (The page also names staff contacts; leave names off the site.) Source: https://dmv.wv.gov/cps-seat-fitting-stations, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Lactation Clinic** (WVU Medicine Children's). For expectant and nursing mothers; staffed by nurses and certified lactation consultants; help with weight gain, latching, low milk supply, pain, and pumps and supplies. No location, clinic phone or cost on the page; the page's general number is 1-855-WVU-CARE. Page also lists "Breastfeeding classes" with no details. Source: https://wvumedicine.org/our-care/institutes/childrens/our-care/center-of-excellence/birthing-center/clinics/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Baby Pantry** (Scott's Run Settlement House). "Baby items such as diapers, wipes and formula," "available for parents who need baby items," by pickup appointment. Call (304) 435-5098 or use the Contact Us form. Locations: 750 Fairchance Road, Morgantown, WV 26508 and 41 Lady Bug Drive, Osage, WV 26543. Source: https://www.srsh.org/baby-pantry, checked October 30, 2026. (Hours conflict, see below.)
- **Starting Points baby pantry, playgroup, Happy School, respite** (Starting Points Family Support Center). Baby pantry Wednesdays, 9 a.m. to 12 p.m., at The Shack Neighborhood House, items "based upon availability." Playgroup twice a week for children under three, parent must attend. Happy School Pre-K style class for ages 3 to 5, with an attached parent group. Respite Fridays, 10:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. Annual community baby shower. Phone 304-599-5466. Source: https://moncountyfamilysupportcenter.org/, checked October 30, 2026. (Already on site; parent group and respite are new.)
- **Library programs for babies and toddlers** (Morgantown Public Library System). Main library: Preschool Storytime occasional Tuesdays and Wednesdays, 10 a.m.; Evening Preschool Storytime occasional Tuesdays, 6:30 p.m.; blocks and Play-Doh play occasional Tuesdays, 10 a.m.; Read Baby Read (0 to 24 months) occasional Thursdays, 10:30 a.m. Cheat Area Public Library: Wednesday Morning Storytime every Wednesday, 10:30 a.m.; Toddler Playgroup every Friday, 10:30 a.m. Clay-Battelle Public Library: Pre-K Storytime every Friday, 10:30 a.m. Clinton District Public Library: Toddler Storytime every Friday, 11 a.m. Page says to double-check the calendar. Read Baby Read is for "infants between the ages of birth to 24 months and their caregivers to help promote socialization and early literacy." Also a 1,000 Books Before Kindergarten program. Sources: https://mympls.org/programs/kids/preschool-storytime/, https://mympls.org/programs/kids/read-baby-read/, https://mympls.org/programs/kids/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WV Healthy Start/HAPI Project** (WVU School of Medicine, Ob/Gyn). Home or virtual visits, check-ins and phone support from social workers and nurses; education on pregnancy, postpartum, child development; a fatherhood program; screenings for postpartum depression, substance use, smoking, oral health and domestic violence; group classes for moms, dads and families. Eligibility as written: "Prenatal"; "Active Right From The Start (RFTS) Client"; "Medicaid, Maternity Services Recipient, or CHIP Insurance"; resident of a served county (Monongalia is listed). Phone 1-866-738-HAPI (listed as the project director's contact, not labeled intake). Source: https://medicine.hsc.wvu.edu/obgyn/west-virginia-healthy-starthapi-project/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Circle of Parents (virtual) and Path Forward Group** (WVU Center for Excellence in Disabilities). Circle of Parents is a virtual support group, Tuesdays at 7:00 p.m. Path Forward is an in-person grief group in Morgantown (959 Hartman Run Rd.) for parents and caregivers who lost a child or adult with disabilities, 4th Wednesday at 5:00 p.m. Source: https://cedwvu.org/families/frs/family-support-groups/, checked October 30, 2026. (Not a general baby/toddler group; useful as a "Find your people" link for parents of kids with disabilities.)
