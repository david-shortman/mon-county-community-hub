## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **WV Contractor License Search** (WV Contractor Licensing Board). Free online search of licensed contractors by license number, classification, company name, city or county. Results show license number, company, address, county, phone, classification and expiration. The page says it "is updated in real time with the Board's licensure database." For anyone hiring a contractor. Source: https://wvclboard.wv.gov/verify/, checked October 30, 2026. The Division of Labor's Database Searches page links here: https://labor.wv.gov/database-search, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Contractor complaint: Request for Investigation** (WV Contractor Licensing Board). Takes complaints against unlicensed contractors, or licensed contractors working outside their classification, on residential projects that total $5,000 or more (materials and labor) and commercial projects of $25,000 or more. The Board says it "cannot be of assistance to you regarding building code violations, contractual disagreements and misappropriation of funds or workmanship issues." Anonymous complaints are not accepted. Include contractor name, address, phone, project location, and copies of canceled checks, contracts or invoices. Board office: 1900 Kanawha Boulevard East, State Capitol Complex, Building 3, Room 200, Charleston, WV 25305. Phone (304) 558-7890. Hours: Monday through Friday, 8:00am to 5:00pm (as written on the page). Source: https://wvclboard.wv.gov/complaint/index.asp and https://wvclboard.wv.gov/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Plumber and HVAC certification searches** (WV Division of Labor). Separate searches for "Current Certified Plumbers" and "Current Certified HVAC Technicians," linked from the Database Searches page (the links go to wvlabor.com; not opened). Source: https://labor.wv.gov/database-search, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Building code and permits outside city limits** (Monongalia County Planning Office). The county page says that within county jurisdiction "there is no local enforcement of building code at this time," and points to the town sites for Morgantown, Star City, Granville and Westover (Blacksville has no website). In zoned areas of the county (West Run), use and siting rules apply; check the County GIS map. Also lists: WV Division of Highways encroachment permit for new driveways onto state roads, the Assessor's Real Property Improvement form, septic and wells through the Health Department, and stormwater permits through MUB (inside the MS4 district) or WV DEP. It also states the $5,000 residential and $25,000 commercial contractor license thresholds. Office: 243 High Street, Room 026, Morgantown, WV 26505. Main office number 304-284-7302. Sources: https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/moncpc/about/building_enforcement.php and https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/moncpc/about/planning_office.php, checked October 30, 2026. FAQ (floodplain permit, land use permit, "Do I need a building permit?"): https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/moncpc/frequently_asked_questions.php, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Building permits inside Morgantown** (City of Morgantown, Permitting, Licensing and Registration). All building permit applications go through the online Morgantown Access Portal; the city does not accept paper applications. A separate application is needed for each scope of work (for example electrical, plumbing). Has a City Zoning Viewer to check whether an address is inside city limits, a fee schedule (Ordinance 2026-25), user guides and video tutorials. Source: https://www.morgantownwv.gov/704/Permitting-Licensing-Registration (the old /172/Building-Permits link redirects here), checked October 30, 2026.
- **Code Enforcement** (City of Morgantown, Development Services). Issues building and demolition permits, does property maintenance inspections and routine rental housing inspections. Complaints go through SeeClickFix (https://seeclickfix.com/morgantown). Address 389 Spruce Street, Morgantown WV 26505. Phone 304-284-7401. Chief Building Official phone 304-284-7536. Office hours (site footer): Monday through Thursday 7 am to 5:30 pm. Source: https://www.morgantownwv.gov/167/Code-Enforcement, checked October 30, 2026.
- **MO.T.O. tool lending library** (City of Morgantown). Lends tools for home repairs, yard work and community projects. Membership is free for City of Morgantown residents 18 and older, with proof of residency and a valid photo ID. Three memberships: Everyday (tools up to $250 per checkout, no credit card), Project (all tools, credit card on file), Local Organization (groups and nonprofits, includes tool trailer for events). Loans are 7 days with one 7-day renewal if no one else has reserved. Late fee $1 per day per tool; full replacement cost after 30 days overdue; $5 cleaning fee may apply. Register in person or online through MyTurn. City page contact: 304-241-3116, 200 M-Tec Dr., Morgantown, WV 26501. Sources: https://morgantownwv.gov/720/Morgantown-Tool-Outreach-MOTO and https://moto.myturn.com/library/, checked October 30, 2026. (Pickup location and hours conflict; see "Could not confirm.")
- **Septic and wells** (Monongalia County Health Department, Environmental Health). For homes without public sewer or water. New, modified or failing septic systems start with a certified installer, who does a perc test and submits the application; a sanitarian visits, issues a construction permit, and does a final inspection before the system is covered. New, modified or abandoned wells start with a certified well driller; the Health Department issues a construction permit. The Health Department "can also test well water for the presence of bacteriological contamination"; chemical testing must be done by a certified water quality laboratory (state list linked). Also does home loan (sale) evaluations of septic and well. Phone (304) 598-5131. Forms on page: sewage system application, water well application, water well sample request, request for water analysis. Source: https://www.monchd.org/services/septic-and-wells, checked October 30, 2026. Parent page: https://www.monchd.org/departments-programs/environmental-health, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Wastewater Revolving Loan Fund** (administered by the Monongalia County Commission, listed on the Health Department page). "Provides low-interest loans to citizens based on income to repair or replace failing on-site sewage systems." Application PDF linked on the page. The income table did not come through in our copy, so no limits are stated here. Source: https://www.monchd.org/services/septic-and-wells, checked October 30, 2026. [high stakes]
- **Weatherization Program** (North Central West Virginia Community Action Association, NCWVCAA). Lowers heating and cooling costs for low-income households: insulation of attics, sidewalls, water tanks, water lines and ductwork, air sealing, heating system repair or replacement, refrigerator checks. Priority to households with children, older residents and people with disabilities. Approved applicants go on a waitlist and are served in order. Homes can be deferred for moisture, big roof leaks, clutter or sewer leaks. The page does not list income limits. Apply with the downloadable application or at a county office. Lists Monongalia among counties served. Monongalia County office: 40 Commerce Dr. Suite 200, Westover, WV 26501, phone 304-363-4367. Weatherization staff phone (304) 457-3420. Main line 304-363-2170. Sources: https://ncwvcaacorp.net/programs/weatherization/ and https://ncwvcaacorp.net/counties/monongalia-county-office-2/, checked October 30, 2026. [high stakes]
- **Homestead exemption** (Monongalia County Assessor). For people 65 or older on or before June 30 following the July 1 assessment date, or permanently and totally disabled. New applicants file between July 1 and December 1; those eligible apply once. File online (link on page) or at the office. Office: 243 High Street, Room 335, Morgantown, WV 26505, phone 304-291-7222, open Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 4:30 pm. Real Estate Office phone 304-291-7279. Source: https://www.assessor.org/2205/Real-Estate, checked October 30, 2026. (Already on our calendar.)
- **Assessor "Important Information for Monongalia Taxpayers, TY 2026"** (Monongalia County Assessor, PDF). Lists key dates: July 1 assessment date; Sept. 1 first half taxes due for 2.5% discount; Dec. 1 homestead applications due; Jan. 1 appeals to Assessor on value must be completed; Feb. 1 Board of Equalization and Review meets to hear appeals of value, "Appointments are required"; Mar. 1 second half taxes due for 2.5% discount; Apr. 30 last day to pay taxes without penalty. Explains Market Value x 60% = Assessed Value, and that a value increase over 10% brings a written notice from the Assessor. Also says anyone who builds or improves so value rises more than $1,000 must notify the Assessor on Form 12:75 within 60 days (a city permit counts as notice). Source: https://assessor.org/DocumentCenter/View/16109/Important-Information-for-Monongalia-Taxpayers-TY-2026-PDF, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Appealing an assessment** (Monongalia County Commission, Board of Review and Equalization page). First, an informal review with the Assessor. If no agreement, appeal to the County Commission sitting as Board of Equalization and Review (February) or the Board of Assessment Appeals (October). For October, notify the County Commission in writing not later than February 20. If you do not request either before February 20, "you waive your right to ask for a correction of your assessment in the current year." The Board hears the upcoming year's values only. The taxpayer must prove the value is wrong by clear and convincing evidence, such as an appraisal by a licensed appraiser within the past three years or values of comparable nearby properties. To appeal, contact the County Commission office at 304-291-7257; the Assessor's office can help complete the application. Source: https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/commission/board_of_assessment_appeals/index.php, checked October 30, 2026. State law: https://code.wvlegislature.gov/11-3-24/, checked October 30, 2026. [high stakes] (Conflicts below.)
- **Paying property tax** (Monongalia County Sheriff's Tax Office). Collects real estate and personal property taxes, mails tax statements in July, and sells delinquent real estate taxes in November. Online search and online payment links on the page. Address 243 High St. Rm 300, Morgantown, WV 26505. Phone (304) 291-7244. Hours: Monday through Friday, 8:30am to 4:30pm. Email taxoffice@monongaliasheriff.gov. Source: https://monongaliasheriff.gov/tax/index.php, checked October 30, 2026. (Note: the issue's old link https://monongaliasheriff.gov/tax.html now returns a 404 page.) [high stakes]
- **Mon Valley Habitat for Humanity**. Serves Marion, Monongalia and Preston counties. Homeownership program (pre-applications open for a Fairmont Housing Project). Its Owner-Occupied Rehabilitation Program is only for homes in Fairmont Census Tracts 203 or 211 (Marion County), for owners with household income between 30% and 80% of Area Median Income. So it does not cover Monongalia County homes today. Phone 304-292-0914, Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm; mailing address PO Box 504, Morgantown, WV 26507. Sources: https://monvalleyhfh.org and https://monvalleyhfh.org/owner-occupied-rehab/, checked October 30, 2026.
