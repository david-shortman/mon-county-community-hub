## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **Monongalia Arts Center (MAC)** (nonprofit). Describes itself as a local museum, theater, arts education and cultural center. Lists adult and youth classes (art, dance, improv, digital photography, watercolors), half-day summer camps for ages 6 to 18, galleries, a Community Maker Space, artist studios and memberships. Address: 107 High Street, Morgantown, West Virginia 26505. Phone: 304-292-3325. Email: info@monartscenter.com. Hours: Tuesdays 6 p.m. to 9 p.m., Wednesday through Friday 1 p.m. to 7 p.m., Saturdays 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., also during classes and events and by appointment. Caution: footer reads "Copyright © 2022" and the classes page (/classes-and-events) returned 503, so no current class dates were confirmed. Source: https://www.monartscenter.com/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **MAC Community Maker Space** (MAC). Shared tools and supplies for members. Daily membership $10, monthly $60. Guest passes $5 a day (monthly members get one free guest pass twice a month). "Scholarships are available" for anyone for whom cost is a barrier. The page says the space is in beta and policies and prices may change. Mediums: linoleum relief printing, black and white darkroom photography, painting (oil, acrylic, watercolor), drawing, collage. Included: paints, brushes, inks, drawing tools, collage library, darkroom. Paper and canvas cost extra. Same hours as the MAC; closed during figure drawing sessions, and access may be limited until 4 p.m. during summer camps. Source: https://www.monartscenter.com/community-maker-space, checked October 30, 2026.
- **MAC Artist Studios** (MAC). Rented studios for visual artists, first come, first served. Email info@monartscenter.com to schedule a tour, then complete the online application. No price listed. Source: https://www.monartscenter.com/artist-studios, checked October 30, 2026.
- **MAC exhibits and calls for entries** (MAC). Artists apply to show through an exhibit application form. MAC is "accepting applications for solo and group exhibits in 2027." Fee options listed: commission only (gallery keeps 30% of sales), or a flat fee with no commission ($150 Benedum Gallery or $100 Davis Gallery; or $250 Benedum or $200 Davis with 100 printed invitations). Sources: https://www.monartscenter.com/exhibits and https://www.monartscenter.com/gallery-faqs, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Morgantown Public Library: Makerspace Spotlight** (Morgantown Public Library System). "We showcase a piece of equipment from our Makerspace cart at the Morgantown Public Library, giving people of all ages the opportunity to explore new tools and technologies." Dates are on the library calendar. Source: https://mympls.org/programs/adults/makerspace-spotlight/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Morgantown Public Library: Adult Craft Night** (library). "Morgantown and Arnettsville each host monthly craft nights, featuring projects you get to make and take home with you." Source: https://mympls.org/programs/adults/adult-craft-night/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Morgantown Public Library: Chronicles of Yarnia** (library). "This twice-monthly fiber meetup at the Morgantown Public Library is for knitters and crocheters of all experience levels." Source: https://mympls.org/programs/adults/chronicles-of-yarnia/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Morgantown Public Library: ArtistWorks** (library). Listed on the adult programs page as "Beginner to advanced lessons for art, voice and musical instruments," online with a library card. Source: https://mympls.org/programs/adults/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Morgantown Public Library: Library of Things** (library). Borrow items with a library card, 21 day checkout, 1 item per card, no renewals or holds. Music items listed: children's guitar, amplifier. Source: https://mympls.org/books-music-more/library-of-things/, checked October 30, 2026. (Mostly a borrowing collection; may fit lane 6 better.)
- **Local Author Fair** (library). "Saturday, November 21, 12-3 p.m. At the Morgantown Public Library 373 Spruce Street, Morgantown, WV." Local authors, artists and creators sell directly. Year not printed on the event page; the library home page says "our annual Local Author Fair is 12-3 p.m. on Saturday, November 21," and November 21, 2026 is a Saturday. Library phone on the site: 304-291-7425. Sources: https://mympls.org/programs/all-ages/local-author-fair/ and https://mympls.org/, checked October 30, 2026. (Calendar candidate.)
- **Morgantown Tool Outreach (MOTO)** (City of Morgantown). Free tool lending "for home repairs, property upkeep, and community improvement projects." For City of Morgantown residents aged 18 and older, and local organizations. Needs proof of residency and photo ID. Address: 200 M-Tec Dr., Morgantown, WV 26501. Phone: 304-241-3116. Hours not listed. Inventory at moto.myturn.com. Source: https://morgantownwv.gov/720/Morgantown-Tool-Outreach-MOTO, checked October 30, 2026. (Home repair focus, not arts; likely lane 6 or a home page. Listed because makers will ask.)
- **West Virginia Folklife Apprenticeship Program** (West Virginia Folklife, WV Humanities Council). Yearlong one-to-one apprenticeships in traditional music, dance, craft, foodways, storytelling and more. Mentor and apprentice apply together. Mentor stipend $3,000, apprentice $800, pair may request $400 more for expenses. 2026-27 cycle: application available July 27, 2026, closes September 18, 2026, panel October 2026, notices November 2026. The page also shows a banner "The application window has closed." Contact: Jennie Williams, 304-346-8500, williams@wvhumanities.org. Statewide. Source: https://wvfolklife.org/folklife-apprenticeship-program/ and https://wvfolklife.org/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Frank and Jane Gabor West Virginia Folklife Center** (Fairmont State University, Fairmont, outside Mon County). Free public events, including a "Traditional Acoustic Jam, 6:30-9:30 p.m., the last Friday of each month." Hours Monday to Thursday 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Upcoming on the page (no year printed): Nov 10 Thanksgiving Debate, Dec 4 West Virginia Storytelling Guild Holiday Concert. "All events are free and open to the public." Address: Squibb Wilson Blvd., Fairmont, WV 26554. Phone: (304) 367-4403. Source: https://www.fairmontstate.edu/folklife/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Augusta Heritage Center** (Augusta Arts and Culture, Elkins, outside Mon County, statewide draw). Workshops, retreats, concerts, dances and jams. Summer workshops are now in downtown Elkins (the Wilt Building), "no longer held at Davis & Elkins College." 2026 summer tuition was $595 (late $625); scholarships for tuition and a Volunteer Collective work exchange. Upcoming: Dulcimer Retreat Nov 5 to 8, 2026 (Blackwater Falls State Park); Augusta Irish Retreat Nov 12 to 15, 2026 (Elkins); monthly Augusta Community Sing, last Monday. No 2027 summer dates posted yet. Sources: https://augustaartsandculture.org/ and https://augustaartsandculture.org/augusta-heritage-center-summer-workshop-series-faq/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WV Department of Arts, Culture and History grants** (state). For individual artists: Creative Aging for Lifelong Learning (CALL), up to $7,500, and Living Traditions: Folk Arts Project Support, up to $5,000, both "Not Currently Open." For organizations, open on a rolling basis: Arts in Education Mini Grant (up to $1,000), Community Arts Mini Grant (up to $1,000), Cultural Facilities and Capital Resources Fast Track (up to $10,000). Not high stakes (not money for residents in need). Source: https://wvculture.org/arts/grants/, checked October 30, 2026.
