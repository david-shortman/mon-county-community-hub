## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **West Virginia Contractor Licensing Board** (WV Division of Labor). Issues state contractor licenses. Its "Apply for License" guide lists 7 steps: contact the board with your scope of work to learn which classifications and exams apply, pass the exam (scheduled through Prov, Inc.), register for a tax number, register with the Secretary of State when required, carry workers' compensation and unemployment coverage, complete a wage bond status check, then apply online or by mailed PDF. The person taking the exam must be an officer, member, owner, or full-time employee. Phone (304) 558-7890, Monday through Friday 8:00am to 5:00pm. Address: 1900 Kanawha Boulevard East, State Capitol Complex, Building 3, Room 200, Charleston, WV 25305. The site also has a Contractor License Search at /verify/. Source: https://www.wvclboard.wv.gov and https://www.wvclboard.wv.gov/account/guide.asp, checked October 30, 2026.
- **When a state contractor license is required** (WV Code). The current code defines a contractor as someone who works for pay (not as an employee) to build, alter, repair, improve, move or demolish a structure "where the cost of the undertaking is $5,000 or more for residential work or $25,000 or more for commercial work." Source: https://code.wvlegislature.gov/30-42-3/, checked October 30, 2026. Exemptions include work an owner or occupant does on their own structure, selling or installing finished products that do not become a permanent part of the structure, and emergency repair of equipment. Licensees must post the license at the job and put the license number in ads and contracts. Source: https://code.wvlegislature.gov/30-42-6/, checked October 30, 2026. Note: the board's own pages do not state the dollar threshold, and many third-party sites still say $2,500 (the older figure). Use the code pages.
- **Plumber certification** (WV Division of Labor). No one may do or offer plumbing work in WV without a plumber license. Classes: Master, Journeyman, Plumber in Training. Master and Journeyman applicants certify experience and take a written exam. Fees: Master and Journeyman initial and renewal $75, Plumber in Training $25, duplicate $10, late fee $25. Phone 1-304-558-7890. Source: https://labor.wv.gov/licensing/plumber-certification and https://labor.wv.gov/licensing/plumber-certification/plumber-certification-fees, checked October 30, 2026.
- **HVAC technician certification** (WV Division of Labor). No one may do or offer HVAC work in WV without a Division of Labor certification. Classes: HVAC Technician (at least 2,000 hours of HVAC work, training and experience), HVAC Residential Technician, and Technician in Training (works only under direct supervision). Phone 1-304-558-7890. Source: https://labor.wv.gov/licensing/hvac-technician-certification, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WV Division of Labor licensing hub**. Also lists crane operator certification, elevator safety, fees, and a license database search. Links contractor licensing to the board site above. Source: https://labor.wv.gov, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Electrician licensing** (WV State Fire Marshal, Regulatory and Licensing Division). Licenses electricians, electrical inspectors, home inspectors, fire protection workers and others. Applications and renewals go through the online portal at sfm-permit-public.wvoasis.gov. Exam dates for 2026 and 2027 are posted on the page. Phone 304-558-2191. Email sfmlicensing@wv.gov. Address: 1600 Pennsylvania Avenue, Charleston, WV 25302 (the page says the office moved here). Hours 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday through Friday. The page does not cover plumbing or HVAC. Source: https://firemarshal.wv.gov/regulatory-and-licensing-division, checked October 30, 2026.
- **City of Morgantown: starting a business** (City of Morgantown). Steps: confirm zoning with Planning (304-284-7431), get a state business registration through the WV One Stop Business Portal, then apply for a Municipal Business License through the Morgantown Access Portal, then a Certificate of Occupancy if you have a location in city limits ($65 application fee). Contractors use a separate "Contractor Application for Municipal Business License." Address 389 Spruce Street, Morgantown, WV 26505. Phone 304-284-7405. Hours Monday through Thursday, 7:00 am to 5:30 pm. Source: https://www.morgantownwv.gov/247/Steps-to-Starting-Your-Business, checked October 30, 2026.
- **City of Morgantown: building permits** (City of Morgantown). All building permit applications go through the online Morgantown Access Portal. Paper applications are not accepted. Each scope of work needs its own application. If a contractor's Morgantown Municipal License does not show in the portal, call Finance at 304-284-7408. B&O taxes are separate from permit fees (Finance, 304-284-7401). Fee schedule: Ordinance 2026-25. Phone 304-284-7405. Source: https://morgantownwv.gov/433/Building-Permits and https://morgantownwv.gov/704/Permitting-Licensing-Registration, checked October 30, 2026. Fee schedule: https://www.morgantownwv.gov/DocumentCenter/View/6820/Ordinance_2026-25---Plan-Review-and-Permitting-Fee-Schedule
- **Jumpstart Savings Program** (West Virginia State Treasurer's Office). A state career investment account for skilled trade workers. Can be used to save for "tools, supplies, equipment, licensing and certification, and other business costs." For community and technical college students and graduates, registered apprentices and journeymen, ACE and high school CTE students, and others. The Treasurer deposits $100 if the beneficiary is a WV resident under 18 or enrolls in a qualified program within 180 days of opening. Enroll at access.wvjumpstart.gov. Phone 681.319.4638. Source: https://wvjumpstart.gov/what-is-jumpstart/overview, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Apprenticeships** (WorkForce West Virginia). General apprenticeship page. Links to apprenticeship.gov Job Finder and the WV Building and Construction Trades Council. Phone 1-800-252-JOBS. Source: https://workforcewv.org/job-seeker/find-a-job/apprenticeships/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **West Virginia Apprenticeship Office** (U.S. Department of Labor, apprenticeship.gov state office list). Listed for West Virginia. Contact details sit behind a "View contact info" profile link that we did not open. Source: https://www.apprenticeship.gov/about-us/state-office, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WV Building and Construction Trades Council** (WVBCT). A statewide council for craft unions. Says it represents 33 locals that offer apprenticeships across the state, run jointly by unions and union contractors. Has a county map and per-trade pages. Source: https://www.wvtrades.org/ and https://www.wvtrades.org/local-unions/, checked October 30, 2026. This is a council page, not a local's or an international's own locator, so per the issue rule we do not list union locals from it on the site yet (see Could not confirm).
