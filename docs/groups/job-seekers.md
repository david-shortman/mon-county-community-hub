## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **City of Morgantown job openings** (City of Morgantown). The city's home page has a "Job Opportunities: See current openings" link to its Paylocity job board. Openings listed today included Executive Assistant, Police Communications Technician, Downtown Cleanliness Ambassador, Laborer (Street), Airport Financial Manager, and Airport Operations and Maintenance Specialist. Source: https://www.morgantownwv.gov/ and https://recruiting.paylocity.com/recruiting/jobs/All/effdc15d-c347-442b-ab66-bbbd3ac9cd65/City-of-Morgantown, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Monongalia County Commission job postings** (Mon County Commission). A "Job Postings" page lists openings with job description, post date and closing date, and links a "Monongalia County Commission - Employment Application" PDF. No openings showed in the table when we checked. County Parks and Recreation points to the same page. Contact on the page: 243 High St, RM 202, Morgantown, WV 26505, 304-291-7257, info@monongaliacounty.gov. Source: https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/government/job_postings.php, checked October 30, 2026.
- **State of West Virginia jobs** (WV Division of Personnel). "Search Available Public Jobs" goes to https://www.governmentjobs.com/careers/wv. "After finding a position for which you'd like to apply, click the 'Apply' link in the upper righthand corner and follow the prompts." Questions: DOP Staffing and Recruitment, 304-414-1852. Main line (304) 558-3950. A Job Interest Card sends email alerts for new openings; there is also an online application tutorial and a job fair calendar. Source: https://personnel.wv.gov/empopp, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WVU Medicine careers** (WVU Medicine). "Search all jobs" opens the WVU Medicine job board at https://wvumedicine.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/WVUH. Categories include nursing, respiratory therapy, home health and hospice, pharmacy, IT, radiology and others. No recruiter phone on the page. Source: https://wvumedicine.org/careers/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Unemployment benefits** (WorkForce West Virginia). File online at https://uc.workforcewv.org/Consumer/Default.aspx ("the quickest way to apply"), by phone at 1-800-252-JOBS (5627), WV Relay 711, or at a local office with computers and staff. Bring: Social Security number, addresses, phone, email, government photo ID, direct deposit bank information, and employer name, address, phone, first and last day worked, and reason for leaving. File a weekly certification for each week (weeks run Sunday to Saturday; file Sunday, "you may file through Friday by 5pm"). The first eligible week is an unpaid waiting week but must be claimed. "Four (4) work search activities each week" are required, with proof kept for the benefit year plus six months. Maximum 26 weeks. Appeals go to the nearest local office by the deadline on the notice. Sources: https://workforcewv.org/job-seeker/unemployment/ and https://workforcewv.org/unemployment-insurance-benefits/unemployment-resources/unemployment-basics/, checked October 30, 2026. Work search chart: https://workforcewv.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Work-Search-Activities-Chart.pdf.
- **Region VI Workforce Development Board** (local WIOA board). Runs WIOA programs for 13 north central counties; "100% funded with Federal WIOA funding." Its Morgantown American Job Center is at 40 Commerce Drive, Morgantown, WV 26505, "Serving Monongalia & Preston Counties." Office phone listed: (681) 404-6377; job openings or unemployment: (800) 252-5627; TTY 7-1-1. No hours listed. Sources: https://regionviwv.org/ and https://regionviwv.org/american-job-centers/locations-and-contacts/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WIOA training scholarships (Individual Training Accounts)** (Region VI WDB). For people a career specialist finds "unlikely or unable to obtain or retain employment that leads to self-sufficiency or higher wages" through career services alone. "Free tuition assistance up to $12,000 and/or transportation supportive services up to $25/week." "ITAs are not entitlements and are contingent upon availability of funding." Training must be with a Region VI Eligible Training Provider. Start by calling the career specialist for Monongalia and Preston counties: 304-844-1666. Source: https://regionviwv.org/training-education/wioa-training-scholarships/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Job seeker help: resumes, interviews, career counseling** (Region VI WDB, through the American Job Centers). Lists "Resume assistance," "Job interview preparation and planning," "Individualized career counseling," job search help, skill assessments, referrals to training and tuition help, unemployment help, and support services. For "all job seekers including Veterans and individuals with disabilities." Source: https://regionviwv.org/job-seekers/job-seeker-assistance/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Library job help and computers** (Morgantown Public Library System). Free public computers and WiFi at all locations. Print, copy and fax: black and white copies 10 cents, color 25 cents, faxes $1.00 a page (no more than $5.00). Tech Help with your own device. Under "Career and Skill Development": Job and Career Accelerator ("Create professional resumes, cover letters, and practice interview skills"), Learning Express Library (practice tests in math, reading, writing, science), TutorWV ("No Cost 1-to-1 tutoring, test prep and job search assistance," live 10 a.m. to 10 p.m.), and Mango Languages. Call 304-291-7425 for login details. Sources: https://mympls.org/services/ and https://mympls.org/programs/adults/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Pierpont Career and Corporate Training** (Pierpont Community and Technical College). Accelerated, open enrollment, non-degree career trainings: CompTIA IT certifications, Industrial Mechanical Technician, Electrical and Automation Technician, Certified Clinical Medical Assistant, EKG/ECG Technician, EMT-Basic, Phlebotomy, Surgical Technology, and TEAS test prep. Delivered online, in person or hybrid. Funding options named on the page: FAFSA, WV Future Workforce Grant, and Region VI WIOA scholarships. Contact: cct@pierpont.edu; financial aid financialaid@pierpont.edu or 304-367-4907. Main campus 500 Galliher Dr., Fairmont, WV 26554. Source: https://www.pierpont.edu/career-corporate-training/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WV Invests Grant** (WV Community and Technical College System, via College for West Virginia). Last-dollar grant that covers "tuition, mandatory fees and academic program fees" for certificate or associate programs in high-demand fields at participating WV public colleges. Must be a U.S. citizen living in WV at least one year. Not for people with a bachelor's degree or higher (except some EMS programs). File the FAFSA and the WV Invests application every year; priority deadline April 15, awards rolling. Two hours of community service per term. Must live in WV two years after finishing or dropping below half time, or the grant becomes a loan with interest. Questions: wvinvests@wvctcs.edu, (304) 558-4618, toll free (877) 987-7664. Sources: https://www.cfwv.com/financial-aid/wv-invests-grant/, https://www.cfwv.com/financial-aid/wv-invests-grant/faqs/, https://www.cfwv.com/financial-aid/wv-invests-grant/apply-now/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WV Future Workforce Grant (shown as HEAPS Workforce on the state page)** (WV Higher Education Policy Commission). For students with financial need in a certificate, industry credential or skill program in a demand occupation in WV. "Up to $2,000 toward tuition, required testing and required supplies." Complete the FAFSA and the application; rolling from July 1, first come, first served. Contact: workforce@wvhepc.edu, (304) 558-4618. Source: https://www.cfwv.com/financial-aid/workforce/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WVU Career Center** (WVU). Handshake job search, one on one appointments, resume and cover letter templates, career fairs, a virtual career closet. Evansdale Crossing, 3rd Floor, 62 Morrill Way, Morgantown, WV 26505; 304-293-2221; careercenter@mail.wvu.edu; 8:15 a.m. to 4:45 p.m., Monday to Friday. The page does not say whether alumni or the public can use it. Source: https://careercenter.wvu.edu/, checked October 30, 2026.
