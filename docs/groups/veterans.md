## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **Clarksburg, WV Benefit Office** (WV Department of Veterans Assistance, WVDVA). The state benefits office whose page lists "Counties Served: Harrison, Lewis, Marion, Monongalia, Preston, Taylor & Doddridge." Staffed by state veterans service officers (VSOs). Physical address: 153 W Main Street, Suite C, Clarksburg, WV 26301. Phone 304-627-2175. Fax 304-627-2177. No hours on the page. (Staff names and emails are on the page; we should not list individuals.) Source: https://veterans.wv.gov/offices/clarksburg-wv-benefit-office, checked October 30, 2026. Office list (15 offices; no Morgantown office): https://veterans.wv.gov/benefits-office-locations, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WVDVA main office** (WV Department of Veterans Assistance). 1900 Kanawha Boulevard East, Bldg. 5, Room 205, Charleston, WV 25305. Phone 304-558-3661. Source: https://veterans.wv.gov/benefits-office-locations, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Help with a VA disability claim** (WVDVA). The disability compensation page says: "For assistance with your disability claim to the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), find the West Virginia Department of Veterans Assistance Benefit Office closest to you." It also explains disability pay, clothing allowance, Specially Adapted Housing and Special Home Adaptation grants (FY 2026 maximums $126,526 and $25,350), Veteran Readiness and Employment, and Special Monthly Compensation. Source: https://veterans.wv.gov/benefits-assistance/benefits/disability-compensation, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Accredited representatives are free if they are VSO reps** (U.S. VA). The VA FAQ says: "The services that an accredited VSO representative provides on your VA benefit claim are always free." Attorneys and claims agents may charge only after VA decides the initial claim, and only with a signed fee agreement. Source: https://www.va.gov/resources/va-accredited-representative-faqs/, checked October 30, 2026. (The VA "find a rep" tool at https://www.va.gov/get-help-from-accredited-representative/find-rep/ is a script app; it did not load for us, but the URL is live.)
- **Huntington VA Regional Benefit Office** (U.S. VA). 640 4th Avenue, Huntington, WV 25701. Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.; "we'd prefer you have an appointment." Handles disability compensation, education, pensions, home loans, burial benefits and more. VA benefits hotline 800-827-1000 (TTY 711), 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. ET, Monday to Friday. The page's own main phone was blank. Source: https://www.va.gov/huntington-va-regional-benefit-office/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Monongalia County VA Clinic** (U.S. VA). Re-checked: 40 Commerce Drive, Suite 101, Westover, WV 26501-3952; Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Lists audiology, lab, physical/occupational therapy, primary care, radiology. Transportation: "We work with Disabled American Veterans and county Veterans Affairs directors to provide transportation"; "Individual van schedules vary." Phone numbers rendered blank in our fetch (our record already has 304-292-7535 from an earlier read). Source: https://www.va.gov/clarksburg-health-care/locations/monongalia-county-va-clinic/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Morgantown Vet Center** (U.S. VA). Re-checked: 34 Commerce Drive, Suite 101, lower level of the WesMon V building; Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Free, confidential counseling for veterans, service members and families; "Anyone in crisis will be seen within the same day." Also helps with Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E), benefits and health care applications, and referrals. Source: https://www.va.gov/morgantown-vet-center/, checked October 30, 2026. (See "Could not confirm" for a phone number conflict on this page.)
- **Operation Welcome Home** (nonprofit). Its own site says it is "a Veterans Support Facility at Mylan Park in Morgantown, WV" serving "the North Central West Virginia population of Service Members, Veterans, and families." Services listed: job placement, resume services, employment search, linkage to existing services, recreational opportunities, agriculture training. Also "a common meeting place at Mylan Park in Morgantown for veterans to congregate." Serves "all veterans and family members regardless of service era, branch, rank, ability, or socioeconomic status." Contact: online form and info@welcomehomewv.com. No street address, phone or hours on the site. Footer shows Copyright 2026 and a 2025 Candid seal; newest news post is March 16, 2023. Sources: https://welcomehomewv.com/, https://welcomehomewv.com/contact/, https://welcomehomewv.com/news/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WorkForce West Virginia veteran services** (state agency). The veterans page says "WorkForce West Virginia is proud to give veterans priority of service," with career counseling, resume and interview help, job placement, workshops, and computers. It points to Local Office Locations and 1-800-252-JOBS. It does not name LVER or DVOP staff or a Morgantown veterans contact. Source: https://workforcewv.org/job-seekers/veterans/, checked October 30, 2026 (opened through a fetch tool; direct requests were blocked by Cloudflare). Our existing record `workforce-wv-morgantown-office` covers the Westover office.
- **American Legion posts in Mon County** (American Legion Department of West Virginia official locator, https://www.wvlegion.org/locator/). Within about 4 miles of Morgantown: **Monongalia Post No. 2**, Old Cheat Road, Morgantown, WV 26505 (no phone listed); **Mountaineer Post No. 174**, 3472 Earl Core Road, Morgantown, WV 26508, phone 1-304-291-4676. Checked October 30, 2026.
- **VFW posts in Morgantown** (VFW national post locator, https://www.vfw.org/find-a-post, ZIP 26505). **Post 548 Gen. Daniel Morgan Post**: mailing PO Box 56, Morgantown, WV 26507-0056; phone (304) 292-3927; meets at 494 Spruce St, Morgantown, 8 p.m. 1st and 3rd Thursday. **Post 9916 John L. Frazier Post**: 340 Holland Avenue, Morgantown, WV 26501-4207; phone (304) 296-5981; meets 7 p.m. 1st Thursday. Checked October 30, 2026. (The locator also lists post officers by name; leave those off.)
- **State VSO directory** (WVDVA). Lists American Legion Department of WV (888-534-4667), VFW Department of WV ((304) 310-0407), Disabled American Veterans (640 4th Avenue Room 138, Huntington, WV 25701, 304-399-9350), Vietnam Veterans of America WV, and others. No Mon County chapters listed. Source: https://veterans.wv.gov/basic-block/veteran-service-organizations, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Burial benefits** (WVDVA). VA pays up to $2,000 toward burial for a service-connected death; for non-service-connected deaths up to $796 if hospitalized, $300 if not, plus a $796 plot-interment allowance outside a national cemetery. Free government headstone or marker. The state Jack Bennett Fund gives up to $380 toward installing a grave marker, based on need. Source: https://veterans.wv.gov/benefits-assistance/benefits/burial-benefits, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Donel C. Kinnard Memorial State Veterans Cemetery** (WVDVA). 130 Academy Drive, Dunbar, WV 25064. Phone 304-746-0026. No cost for eligible veterans; spouse or other eligible family member fee listed as "$780." Source: https://veterans.wv.gov/facilities/donel-c-kinnard-memorial-state-veteran-cemetery, checked October 30, 2026.
- **West Virginia National Cemetery** (U.S. VA National Cemetery Administration). 42 Veterans Memorial Lane, Grafton, WV 26354. Phone 304-265-2044. Office Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.; grounds open daily dawn to dusk. "This cemetery has space available to accommodate casketed and cremated remains." Source: https://www.cem.va.gov/cems/nchp/westvirginia.asp, checked October 30, 2026. (Closest burial option to Morgantown; the state cemetery is near Charleston.)
