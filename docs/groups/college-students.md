## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **WVU Ride Free on Mountain Line** (Mountain Line Transit Authority). WVU students, faculty and staff ride any local Mountain Line bus free by swiping a valid WVU ID when boarding, because the service "has been prepaid." The Grey Line to Pittsburgh is the exception. Customer service: 304-291-RIDE (7433), "7am to 8pm every day." Late-night service runs "until 3:00 a.m. on Thursday, Friday and Saturday while classes are in session" during fall and spring, on the West Run and Campus P.M. routes. Source: https://www.busride.org/wvu-ride-free, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WVU Campus Services routes** (Mountain Line Transit Authority). Lists the routes students use most. Campus PM "Travels Towers to Downtown via Stewart Street: 6PM to 3:40AM Thursday - Saturday." West Run routes "have expanded late night and weekend service and travel between Campus and Downtown." Source: https://www.busride.org/wvu-campus-services, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Mountain Line fares for riders without a WVU ID** (Mountain Line Transit Authority). Individual fare "$0.75 - $1.00," 15 Ride Pass $10.00, 30 Day Pass $38.50 (Token Transit only), Monster Pass $125.00, children 5 and under free. Token Transit app single ride $0.86. Also lists "High School Ride Free: Available to all Monongalia County High School Students." No mention of Pierpont or other colleges. Source: https://www.busride.org/Fares-Programs, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Grey Line to Pittsburgh** (Mountain Line Transit Authority). Morgantown to Pittsburgh is $15 one way; stops include Fairmont, Clarksburg, Waynesburg, Washington, Pittsburgh Airport and the Pittsburgh Greyhound Station. Reserve online or call 304-296-3869. For holiday send-off and return trips, it advises booking a few weeks ahead. Source: https://www.busride.org/29-grey-line, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Rental registration and inspection** (City of Morgantown Code Enforcement). All rental property in city limits must be registered with the City and is "inspected once every 3 years." Before renting, you can call to learn whether a unit is registered, has a letter of compliance, its occupancy type and its occupant limit: 304-284-7401 (the page also lists 304-284-7528 to verify registration). "Occupants as well as property owners may report a concern online" (SeeClickFix link). 389 Spruce Street, Morgantown, WV 26505. Monday to Thursday, 7 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. Source: https://morgantownwv.gov/435/Rental-Information, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Code Enforcement directory listing** (City of Morgantown). Email rentals@morgantownwv.gov. Same address, phone 304-284-7401 and hours as above. Also lists 304-284-7405 under "Contact Us." Source: https://morgantownwv.gov/Directory.aspx?DID=9, checked October 30, 2026.
- **GoVoteWV online tools** (WV Secretary of State). Register or update registration: https://ovr.sos.wv.gov/Register/Landing. Check your registration: https://apps.sos.wv.gov/Elections/Voter/AmIRegisteredToVote. Find your polling place: https://apps.sos.wv.gov/Elections/Voter/FindMyPollingPlace. County clerk directory. Source: https://sos.wv.gov/govotewv, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Voter FAQ** (WV Secretary of State). "You must have the address where you live on your voter registration" (no PO Box). "To vote in the next election, you must register at least 21 days before the date of the election." Register online, by mail, or in person at the County Clerk, the Secretary of State, the DMV and other offices. First-time voters may be asked for ID showing their current address (photo ID, utility bill, bank statement and others). The page says nothing specific about college students. Source: https://sos.wv.gov/elections/voter-information/elections-frequently-asked-questions-faqs, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Voter Information** (City of Morgantown). "Monongalia County Clerk maintains the registration of all voters in the City of Morgantown." County Clerk phone: 304-291-7230. City Clerk (city elections, finding your precinct): 304-284-7434. "The next Morgantown City Council Election takes place in 2027." Source: https://morgantownwv.gov/590/Voter-Information, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Monongalia County Clerk** (WV Secretary of State officials directory). 243 High St., Rm 123 Courthouse, Morgantown, WV 26505. Email clerk@monongaliacountyclerk.com. (This page labels 304.291.7230 as "Fax"; the City page above calls it the County Clerk's phone. See "Could not confirm".) Source: https://apps.sos.wv.gov/elections/Officials/Home/Details/4c5dfc8e-d056-4b01-8439-66a457d3a47a, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Pierpont Community and Technical College** locations. Its About page lists Fairmont (main campus, 500 Galliher Dr.), Clarksburg, a Culinary Academy in White Hall, and the aerospace center in Bridgeport. No Morgantown location is named. Source: https://pierpont.edu/about/, checked October 30, 2026.
