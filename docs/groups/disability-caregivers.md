# People with disabilities and caregivers

Part of #261. Issue: #268. Researched October 30, 2026.

## What people in this group are trying to do

- "I have a disability. Who can help me live on my own and find out what I can get?"
- "I need a ramp, grab bars or a roll-in shower so I can stay in my home."
- "I want to try a wheelchair, walker, screen reader or other device before I buy one."
- "I want a job, or I want to keep the job I have, and my disability makes it hard."
- "How do I get around town on the bus with a wheelchair? Is there a cheaper fare?"
- "My child has a disability. Who do I call at the school district about special education?"
- "My kid or I want to play sports, swim or do activities with other people with disabilities."
- "I think my rights were violated, or someone with a disability is being mistreated. Who can I call?"
- "I take care of my mom with dementia. I need a break, and I want to talk to other caregivers."
- "Someone needs to leave a nursing home and come back home. Who helps with that?"

## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **Community Living Services Program (CLSP)** (LiveAbility). State-funded independent living services. The page lists home modifications "including adding a ramp for wheelchair access to the home," roll-in showers and grab bars, vehicle modifications (van conversion, lifts, hand controls), communication devices and training, help getting durable medical equipment such as a shower chair or walker, and skills training. The page does not state eligibility, cost or how to apply; it gives the Morgantown office, 304-296-6091 or (844) 212-3464, and info@liveabilitywv.org. Source: https://liveabilitywv.org/programs/clsp/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Transition services** (LiveAbility). Help for people with significant disabilities moving from a nursing home or other institution back to the community, help for people at risk of entering one, and help for youth with an IEP leaving high school. Listed help includes returning home, assistive technology, home or vehicle changes, medical equipment, and a housing search. Source: https://liveabilitywv.org/core-services/transition-services/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **LiveAbility Morgantown office** (LiveAbility). Already on our site. Re-confirmed today: 601-603 East Brockway, Suite A, Morgantown, WV 26501; "M-F: 8am – 4:00pm"; 304-296-6091 or (844) 212-3464; fax 304-292-5217; info@liveabilitywv.org. Core services: advocacy, IL skills training, info and referral, peer support, transition services. The logo reads "LiveAbility Northern WV Independence Network." Source: https://liveabilitywv.org/locations/ and https://liveabilitywv.org/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WVATS device demonstrations, 30-day loans and free reuse** (WV Assistive Technology System, WVU Center for Excellence in Disabilities). Try a device before you buy it (demonstrations at the Morgantown office or by a specialist who comes to your area), borrow one for 30 days, or get a donated device for free that you keep. "Our services are available to all West Virginia residents and no documentation of a disability is required." The page calls them "free statewide services" and says the program is federally funded. Phone 800-841-8436, wvats@hsc.wvu.edu. No street address or hours on that page. Source: https://wvats.cedwvu.org/wvats-services/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WVU Center for Excellence in Disabilities (CED)** (WVU). Works to improve opportunities, health and quality of life for people with disabilities and their families. Areas: access to health care, employment, health and wellness, transitions. CED North: 959 Hartman Run Road, Morgantown, WV 26505; 304-293-4692; (888) 829-9426; TTY (800) 518-1448; Monday to Friday 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.; cedcontact@hsc.wvu.edu. Source: https://cedwvu.org/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **DRS Morgantown Branch Office** (WV Division of Rehabilitation Services). Already on our site. Re-confirmed today: Sabraton Plaza, 1415 Earl Core Road, Morgantown, WV 26505, 304-285-3155; statewide toll free 1-800-642-8207. No hours on the page. Source: https://wvdrs.org/contact-us/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **SteppingStones** (nonprofit). "Recreation Programs for Children & Adults with Disabilities": year-round sports, recreation and educational programs for children and adults with intellectual and physical disabilities, "from ages birth and up." Facilities listed: gym, teaching kitchen, computer lab, art room, sensory room, heated indoor pool (year-round) and heated outdoor pool (spring through fall). Runs Miracle League Baseball (named on the home page only). The "Playground For All," a fully inclusive playground, is open across the street in Mylan Park. 400 Mylan Park Ln, Morgantown, WV 26501; 304-983-7837; fax 304-983-8037. No hours, cost or email on the pages; registration page exists at https://www.steppingstoneswv.org/programs/registration/ (not opened). Sources: https://www.steppingstoneswv.org/, https://www.steppingstoneswv.org/programs/, https://www.steppingstoneswv.org/contact/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Mountain Line accessible buses and half fare** (Mountain Line Transit Authority). "All of our buses are equipped with a wheel chair lift or ramp." Larger buses kneel; drivers help with lifts and securing wheelchairs; service animals welcome; oxygen allowed. "The fare for persons with disabilities, seniors, and Medicare cardholders is half the regular fare." You can ask a bus to leave the route to pick you up or drop you off (a "Call and Ride Application" PDF is linked). ADA Compliance Officer: 420 DuPont Road, Morgantown, WV 26501, ADAOfficer@busride.org, (304) 296-3680; TDD (304) 291-7467. Half fare application is mailed to 420 DuPont Road. Sources: https://www.busride.org/accessibility, https://www.busride.org/persons-with-disabilities, https://www.busride.org/accessible-services, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Disability Rights of West Virginia (DRWV)** (nonprofit, statewide). "the federally mandated protection and advocacy system for people with disabilities in West Virginia." Information and referral, abuse or neglect investigations (only with probable cause), advocacy, self-advocacy training, legal representation. Serves people with mental health, developmental, intellectual and physical disabilities, and traumatic brain injury. It does not take divorce or custody, personal injury, wills, property, Social Security denials, or criminal cases. Not everyone who asks gets direct advocacy; intake gives information and referrals to anyone. "Our services are confidential and free of charge." 304-346-0847, 1-800-950-5250, contact@drofwv.org, 5088 Washington St. W, Suite 300, Charleston, WV 25313, Monday to Friday 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Sources: https://www.drofwv.org/, https://www.drofwv.org/our-services, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Monongalia County Schools Special Education Department (Exceptional Students)** (Monongalia County Schools). Led by the Director of Student Services and Exceptional Student Education. 304-291-9210, ext. 1512; 1715 Earl L Core Rd., Morgantown, WV 26505. Links to a Parents/Guardians section and a Special Education Handbook. The landing page does not explain how to ask for an evaluation. Linked from the district home page. Source: https://sites.google.com/boe.mono.k12.wv.us/exceptionalstudents/home, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Alzheimer's Association WV caregiver support groups** (Alzheimer's Association, West Virginia Chapter). Groups "for unpaid family members or friends caring for someone living with Alzheimer's disease" and other dementias; "not appropriate for professionals or paid caregivers." "All services and resources are free." Call the 24/7 Helpline at 800.272.3900 "to confirm dates, times and locations or to register for any group." The page links a PDF list of current groups (not opened); no Morgantown group is shown on the page itself. Source: https://www.alz.org/wv/support, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Special Olympics West Virginia** (statewide nonprofit). Athletes must be identified by a medical agency or professional as having an intellectual disability; competing starts at age 8; Young Athletes for ages 2 to 7; "There is no aging out." Fill out an Athlete Registration Form, then contact a County Director (a PDF list is linked, not opened). The 2027 State Basketball and Cheerleading tournament is listed for March 13-14 in Morgantown. Sources: https://sowv.org/become-an-athlete/, https://www.sowv.org/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **West Virginia Bureau of Senior Services** (state). State directory listing: "lead entity for programs serving older West Virginians." 304-558-3317, toll free 877-987-3646, 1 Players Club Dr Suite 301, Charleston, WV 25311. Its own site (wvseniorservices.gov / seniorservices.wv.gov) could not be opened from this session. Source: https://www.wv.gov/agencies/senior-services-bureau, checked October 30, 2026.
