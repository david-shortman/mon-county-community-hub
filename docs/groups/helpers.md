## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **United Way of Monongalia and Preston Counties** (nonprofit). Works in four areas: Healthy Community, Youth Opportunity, Financial Security, Community Resiliency. Runs the Imagination Library, Helpful Harvest, WV211 work, and a holiday food and toy distribution. Has Donate and Volunteer pages. 278C Spruce Street, Morgantown, WV 26505. 304-296-7525. Its home page says it held its Day of Caring "for the 34th year" on May 13th. Source: https://unitedwaympc.org, checked October 30, 2026.
- **United Way volunteer page** (United Way of Monongalia and Preston Counties). Community members find openings through GivePulse (https://givepul.se/zv0pit). WVU staff, faculty and students use WVU iServe. Examples of tasks: "sorting food donations, mentoring youth, assisting at an event, or lending your professional skills." Open to individuals, businesses, students and groups. A waiver form is linked. Source: https://www.unitedwaympc.org/volunteer-opportunities, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Christian Help** (nonprofit). Says it "gives dignity and support in unexpected and emergency situations at no cost," with free help for money, food, work clothes and guidance. Phone 304-296-0221. Source: https://motownchristianhelp.com, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Christian Help donations** (Christian Help). Takes product donations that are "clean and functional." It is not a recycling center. "Donations are accepted every Wednesday from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m." at 219 Walnut Street, Morgantown, WV 26505. Money can be given online or by check made out to "Christian Help." Source: https://motownchristianhelp.com/donors/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Christian Help volunteering** (Christian Help). Tasks include donation distribution, food orders, reviewing resumes, data entry, mock interviews, life skills class partners, and events like the Holiday Gift Giveaway. "No matter your experience or ability, we have a place for you." Sign up at https://motownchristianhelp.com/apply-here/. Source: https://motownchristianhelp.com/volunteer/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Morgantown Area Meals on Wheels** (nonprofit). Prepares and delivers meals to clients in their homes. Volunteers deliver meals ("Help us deliver nourishing meals to clients"). 3375 University Ave, Morgantown, WV 26505. 304-599-1954. morgantownareamow@gmail.com. Kitchen and office hours on the page: 5:30 AM to 12:00 Noon and 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Has a Donate link. Driver rules are not on the home page (see Could not confirm). Source: https://morgantownwvmow.org, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Literacy Volunteers of Monongalia and Preston Counties** (nonprofit). Free adult reading, writing and English lessons. Its contact section asks "Ready to learn, volunteer, partner, or donate?" Email director@lvmpc.org, call or text 724-491-2007. Online donations through PayPal; the page says donations support learner materials, tutoring resources, classes and community programs. Source: https://lvmpc.org/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Volunteer West Virginia** (state agency, the state's commission for national and community service). Home page has a "Find a Volunteer Opportunity" search by focus area, zip code, distance and virtual. Links to Join AmeriCorps, the AmeriCorps position search (https://my.americorps.gov/mp/listing/publicRequestSearch.do), and AmeriCorps Seniors (https://americorpsseniors.wv.gov). Source: https://volunteer.wv.gov, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Monongalia County Emergency Management** (county, on the MECCA 911 site). 911 Responders Dr, Morgantown, WV 26501. Administration 304-599-6332 (Monday to Friday, 7:00am to 3:00pm). Links to the "2026 Monongalia County Emergency Operations Plan" PDF and to ReadyWV. The page does not mention CERT, volunteers or alert sign-up. Source: https://www.mecca911.org/emergency_management/index.php, checked October 30, 2026. The county site links to it under "Mecca 911 & Department of Homeland Security": https://www.monongaliacounty.gov, checked October 30, 2026.
- **CERT in West Virginia** (ReadyWV, state). CERT "educates volunteers about disaster preparedness" and teaches fire safety, light search and rescue, team organization and disaster medical operations. The page has a Monongalia County CERT entry with a contact phone (304-225-0264) and a wv.gov email, and no website. Source: https://ready.wv.gov/cert-programs-west-virginia, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Start a nonprofit: Articles of Incorporation, Form CD-1** (WV Secretary of State, Business and Licensing Division). Rev. 7/2026. "FILING FEE: $100 (profit) ... $25 (non-profit)." Fee waived for veteran-owned and young entrepreneur corporations. Tel: (304)558-8000. File one original (two for a stamped copy back). Source: https://sos.wv.gov/sites/default/files/2026-07/cd-1.pdf, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Charity registration and lookup** (WV Secretary of State, Charities Division). Groups that ask the public for money for charity in West Virginia must register unless exempt. Fee "$15 for single organizations collecting under $1 million," "$50 for organizations collecting over $1 million." Valid one year, renewed each year. Anyone can look up a charity with "Search Charities & Disbursements": https://erls.wvsos.gov/OnlineCharitiesSearch/Search. Sources: https://sos.wv.gov/charities-frequently-asked-questions and https://sos.wv.gov/business/charities/charitable-organizations, checked October 30, 2026. The second page also notes that for a 501(c)(3) application, "your articles must contain certain provisions," and links the One Stop Business Portal (business4.wv.gov).
