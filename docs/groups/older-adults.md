# Older adults and retirees

Part of #261. Issue: #267. Researched October 30, 2026.

## What people in this group are trying to do

- "I don't drive anymore. How do I get to the doctor, the store, or the senior center?"
- "Is the bus cheaper for me now that I'm older?"
- "I can't cook much anymore. Can someone bring meals to my house?"
- "Where can I eat lunch with other people my age?"
- "Mom needs help at home with bathing and dressing. What programs are there, and what do they cost?"
- "Dad has dementia. Is there anyone who can sit with him so I can get a break?"
- "I have a problem with my parent's nursing home. Who can I talk to?"
- "I just retired. Where can I take classes, exercise, or meet people?"
- "I want to volunteer. Who needs help?"
- "Who do I call if I don't know where to start?"

## Already on our site

- `/disability-and-aging`: LiveAbility (independent living), DRS (job help), WV SHIP (Medicare help). Links to the Medicaid waiver page, `/turning-65`, `/staying-well`, `/veterans`. Nothing on meals, rides, senior centers, in-home help outside Medicaid, or staying active.
- `/turning-65`: Medicare sign-up window, free preventive visits, WV SHIP, Medicare Savings Programs, Extra Help.
- `/medicaid-waivers`: the four Medicaid waivers, including the Aged and Disabled Waiver (care at home instead of a nursing home), and the local DoHS office.
- `/staying-well`, `/flu-shots`: checkups and shots, useful for older adults but not aimed at them.
- `/dental-and-prescriptions`: dental and medicine cost help.
- `/help/food/` (and `/help/food/monthly`, `/help/food/today`): food help in general. No home-delivered or senior meals.
- `/money`: includes free tax help (Christian Help).
- `/work-and-learning`: adult classes (MTEC, literacy), not aimed at retirees.
- `/veterans`: VA clinic and Vet Center.
- Records: `content/orgs/wv-bureau-of-senior-services.yaml` (its `website` points to wvship.org, not the Bureau's own site; worth fixing later), `content/resources/wv-ship-medicare-counseling.yaml`.

## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **Congregate meals ("Eat with Friends")** (Senior Monongalians, Inc.). Lunch for people age 60 and older at two sites: the Mountaineer Mall site, Monday through Friday, 11:30 to 12:30, and the "Core site," Tuesdays only, 11:30 to 12:30. No required fee; a voluntary contribution is suggested. Phone 304-296-9812. Address 5000 Greenbag Road, Suite A5, Morgantown, WV 26501. Source: https://www.seniormons.org/blank, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Home-delivered meals** (Senior Monongalians, Inc.). For eligible adults age 60 and older who cannot get to a meal site, after a short assessment. Home page says "hot, nutritious meals five days a week" for homebound seniors. Cost not stated. Phone 304-296-9812. Sources: https://www.seniormons.org/blank and https://www.seniormons.org, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Rides to the senior center** (Senior Monongalians, Inc.). For Monongalia County residents age 60 and older, from home to the senior center. Reserve a day ahead; limited seats, first come first served. Call 304-296-9812 to sign up. Cost not stated. Source: https://www.seniormons.org/blank-3, checked October 30, 2026.
- **In-home help programs** (Senior Monongalians, Inc.). Nurse's office phone for all programs: 304-296-2503. Source: https://www.seniormons.org/blank-1, checked October 30, 2026. The page lists:
  - Medicaid Aged and Disabled Waiver: must be medically and financially eligible; up to 155 hours of in-home aide help per month; start with your doctor and the local DoHS (page says "DHHR") office.
  - Lighthouse: for people age 60 and older whose income or assets are too high for Medicaid; a Senior Monongalians nurse decides eligibility; up to 60 hours per month; sliding-scale hourly co-pay based on household income.
  - FAIR (Alzheimer's respite): needs a doctor's diagnosis of Alzheimer's or dementia and an in-home nurse visit; companion care, activities, toileting and light meal prep (no bathing or grooming); up to 16 hours per week; sliding-scale co-pay.
  - VA in-home services: page says Senior Monongalians is VA-approved; start with your VA caseworker.
  - Private pay care: personal care and respite at $25.00 per hour when workers are available.
- **Senior center activities** (Senior Monongalians, Inc.). In-center guest speakers, themed lunches, free AARP tax prep help, holiday parties, health screenings, medication reviews, flu shot clinics, summer farmers' market vouchers, billiards, cornhole, fitness room, library. No dates or eligibility stated on the page. Source: https://www.seniormons.org/blank-4, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Volunteering at Senior Monongalians** (Senior Monongalians, Inc.). Steps: application and interview, sign the volunteer policy, training. Bring a photo ID and, if applicable, a food handler's card. Stop by the front desk at the Mountaineer Mall office on Greenbag Road. Source: https://www.seniormons.org/blank-2, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Senior Monongalians, Inc. (about)**. Nonprofit "dedicated to supporting older adults in Monongalia County," founded July 28, 1969. Office hours Monday through Friday, 8:00 am to 4:00 pm. Address 5000 Greenbag Road, Morgantown, WV 26501. Email info@seniormons.org. (Phone on this page conflicts; see Could not confirm.) Source: https://www.seniormons.org/blank-5, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Morgantown Area Meals on Wheels (MAMOW)**. Prepares and delivers meals to people in their homes in the Morgantown area. Delivery within a 10-mile radius of three centers: Goshen Baptist Church, Rock Forge Presbyterian Church, and Star City Kitchen; no delivery in Pennsylvania. Weekdays, drivers come between 10 AM and noon on the days you choose. Clients pay at most $6.00 per meal (general and diabetic) or $7.00 (special diets such as gluten free, low salt or vegan); the fee is set by income and expenses at an interview. Every client needs a contact person. To sign up, call the kitchen at 304-599-1954 (leave name, address and phone if no answer) or email morgantownareamow@gmail.com. Doctors, hospitals, agencies and families can refer. Address 3375 University Ave, Morgantown, WV 26505. Sources: https://www.morgantownwvmow.org and https://www.morgantownwvmow.org/get-meals, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Mountain Line bus fares for older riders** (Mountain Line Transit Authority). Regular cash fare is 75 cents; no change given. "If you are sixty two or older you can sign up for our Gold Card program": 50 percent off cash fares on local trips. Customer service 304-291-RIDE. Source: https://busride.org/how-to-pay, checked October 30, 2026. The accessibility page says "The fare for persons with disabilities, seniors, and Medicare cardholders is half the regular fare." ADA Compliance Officer (304) 296-3680, TDD (304) 291-7467, ADAOfficer@busride.org. Source: https://www.busride.org/accessibility, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Mountain Line route deviations** (Mountain Line Transit Authority). Any rider can ask the bus to leave the route up to three-quarters of a mile to pick up or drop off, if the bus can reach and turn around safely. Submit a Deviation Request Form first; a supervisor checks the site and you are notified in writing. Then call 304-291-RIDE, as little as 15 minutes ahead. Cost: fare plus $0.50 (cash $1.25; 30-day pass holders $0.50). Source: https://www.busride.org/deviations, checked October 30, 2026.
- **BOPARC wellness classes** (Morgantown BOPARC). Drop-in classes at the Wiles Hill Community Center, "tailored for adults 55 and over but are open to adults of all ages." Yoga for Seniors, Fridays 9 to 10 a.m., $5.35 per class. Ballet Basics, Fridays 10 to 11 a.m., $5.35. Tai Chi, Mondays (beginner 9 to 10 a.m. and 3:45 to 4:45 p.m., intermediate 10 to 11 a.m.), listed as "$5:35" (likely $5.35). Line Dancing, Thursdays 10:30 a.m. to noon, free with membership. Source: https://www.boparc.org/wellness-classes.html, checked October 30, 2026. Wiles Hill Community Center: 287 Eureka Drive, Morgantown (Wiles Hill Neighborhood Park). Source: https://www.boparc.org/wiles-hill-community-center.html (via https://www.boparc.org), checked October 30, 2026.
- **WV Medicaid Personal Care Services** (WV Bureau for Medical Services). In-home help for West Virginia Medicaid members with eating, bathing, grooming, mobility and toileting. Need active WV Medicaid and needs in at least three daily living areas, set by a medical assessment. To start: sign the PC-MNER form; a doctor, PA or RN completes and sends it to Acentra Health-PC (toll free 1-866-385-8920); an Acentra nurse schedules a home assessment. Source: https://pcs.wv.gov/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WV Bureau of Senior Services** (state). Cabinet-level state agency, "lead entity for programs serving older West Virginians." Website listed as wvseniorservices.gov. Phone 304-558-3317, toll free 877-987-3646. Source: https://www.wv.gov/agencies/senior-services-bureau, checked October 30, 2026. (The Bureau's own site could not be opened from this session; see below.)
