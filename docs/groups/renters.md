## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **Rental registration, inspections and occupancy check** (City of Morgantown Code Enforcement). Under the Housing Code adopted in 1979, all rental property inside Morgantown city limits must be registered with the city. Rentals are inspected once every 3 years under the I.C.C. property maintenance code, and owners must fix violations within 20 days of the on-site inspection or face citations, condemnation or both. People thinking of renting can call 304-284-7401 to find out if a unit is registered, has a letter of compliance, its type of occupancy, and how many people may live there. To verify a property is registered, the page says to call 304-284-7528. Contact block: rentals@morgantownwv.gov, 389 Spruce Street, Morgantown, WV 26505, phone 304-284-7401, hours Monday to Thursday 7 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. For people renting or thinking of renting inside Morgantown city limits. Source: https://www.morgantownwv.gov/435/Rental-Information, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Report a rental concern online** (City of Morgantown, through SeeClickFix). The Rental Information page says "Occupants as well as property owners may report a concern online" and links to a SeeClickFix report category (https://seeclickfix.com/web_portal/rZqRi1ZwsfR971ma1ddYkrPm/report/category/42710) and the Morgantown SeeClickFix page (https://seeclickfix.com/morgantown). I did not open the SeeClickFix form itself. Source: https://www.morgantownwv.gov/435/Rental-Information, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Rental Housing Registration** (Town of Star City). The town has a rental registration application to print, fill out and return to the town office. Questions go to the Code Enforcement Officer during normal business hours at 304-599-3550. The Code Enforcement page also says anyone who lists a room, apartment or house in Star City on sites like Airbnb must register with the town as a business, at (304) 599-3550. Sources: https://www.starcitywv.com/rental-registration/ and https://www.starcitywv.com/code-enforcement/, checked October 30, 2026. Application PDF: https://www.starcitywv.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Rental-Housing-Registration.pdf (not opened).
- **Renters Rights West Virginia** (Legal Aid of West Virginia). A statewide hub with videos, articles and fill-in forms on being evicted, bad housing conditions, mobile home park eviction, moving into a rental, moving out, getting property back after eviction, and assistance animals. It also has a "housing issues questionnaire" to help people find where to start. For West Virginia renters. Source: https://legalaidwv.org/renters-rights-west-virginia/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Bad Housing Conditions** (Legal Aid of WV). Videos on "safe and habitable" housing and on bed bugs, a fill-in "Letter to Landlord Requesting Repairs," and an article. Source: https://legalaidwv.org/renters-rights-west-virginia/bad-housing-conditions/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **What To Do When Housing Conditions Are Bad** (Legal Aid of WV article, last updated 12/19/2023). Says that under WV Code 37-6-30 a landlord must deliver and keep a home fit and habitable. Lists steps: document the problem with dated photos, notify the landlord (keep copies; certified mail if no timely reply), then contact a health department inspector, code enforcement officer or utility if the landlord does not act. Warns that an inspector could say a home is too unsafe to stay in, so have another place to stay first. Says the law sets no fixed repair timeframe, and that holding back rent "often is not your best choice." Says renters generally must be current on rent for the landlord's repair duty to apply. Source: https://legalaidwv.org/legal-information/what-to-do-when-housing-conditions-are-bad-a-tenants-guide-to-safe-housing/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Moving Into Rental Housing** (Legal Aid of WV). Videos on moving in, understanding a lease and security deposits, plus a "Checklist for Moving into a Rental Unit." Source: https://legalaidwv.org/renters-rights-west-virginia/moving-into-rental-housing/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Renting a home or apartment: Understanding your lease** (Legal Aid of WV article, posted 08/08/2024). Questions to ask before signing (affordability, length, pets and guests, who handles utilities and maintenance, other fees), common lease types (1 year, month-to-month), documents landlords usually ask for, getting changes in writing, and inspecting and documenting damage before signing. Source: https://legalaidwv.org/legal-information/renting-a-home-or-apartment-understanding-your-lease/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Moving out of Rental Housing** (Legal Aid of WV). Videos on moving out and deposits, a fill-in "Letter to Request Return of a Security Deposit," and a move-out checklist. Source: https://legalaidwv.org/renters-rights-west-virginia/moving-out-of-rental-housing/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **How to get your Security Deposit back** (Legal Aid of WV article, posted 07/12/2021). Explains what counts as a deposit, what a landlord may deduct, "normal wear and tear" examples, and deadlines. It says renters with federal rental assistance (HUD, Section 8, housing authority, tax credit housing) have a 30-day federal deadline, and others have the shortest of: 60 days from the end of the tenancy, 45 days from when a new tenant moves in, or any shorter deadline in the lease. Source: https://legalaidwv.org/legal-information/how-to-get-your-security-deposit-back-2/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WV Code 37-6A (security deposits)** (West Virginia Legislature). Read on code.wvlegislature.gov. To describe only, never to advise:
  - 37-6A-1 defines "security deposit" as any refundable deposit given to a landlord to secure the rental agreement or as security for damages. It does not include rent, prepaid rent, or a pet fee or application fee that both sides agree in writing is nonrefundable. The "notice period" is within 60 days of the end of the tenancy or within 45 days of a new tenant moving in, whichever is shorter. Source: https://code.wvlegislature.gov/37-6A-1/, checked October 30, 2026.
  - 37-6A-2 says that at the end of the tenancy and within the notice period, the landlord must return the deposit minus deductions, with a written itemization. It lists the allowed deductions (rent due and late charges in the rental agreement, damages less reasonable wear and tear, unpaid utilities billed to and paid by the landlord, reasonable removal and storage costs, and other charges in the rental agreement). If damages exceed the deposit and need a contractor, the landlord must give written notice within the notice period and then has 15 more days to itemize. The owner at the end of the tenancy is responsible for the deposit. The renter must give the landlord an accurate address. Source: https://code.wvlegislature.gov/37-6A-2/, checked October 30, 2026.
  - 37-6A-3 says the landlord must keep itemized deduction records for one year and let the renter see or get a copy within 72 hours of a written request. Source: https://code.wvlegislature.gov/37-6A-3/, checked October 30, 2026.
  - 37-6A-4 says a lease cannot make a renter give up rights under this article. Source: https://code.wvlegislature.gov/37-6A-4/, checked October 30, 2026.
  - 37-6A-5 says if a landlord's failure to follow the article is willful or not in good faith, the renter is entitled to a judgment for the unreturned deposit plus damages equal to one and a half times the amount wrongfully withheld (credited against any rent owed), and cases go to magistrate or circuit court in the county where the rental is. Source: https://code.wvlegislature.gov/37-6A-5/, checked October 30, 2026.
  - The sections I read do not set a maximum deposit amount. (Legal Aid's lease article says a deposit is "usually equal to one month's rent," which describes custom, not a limit.)
- **WV Code 37-6-30 (fit and habitable)** (West Virginia Legislature). Says a landlord must deliver and keep a rental fit and habitable, meet health, safety, fire and housing codes, make repairs needed to keep it habitable (unless caused mainly by the renter's lack of care), keep electrical, plumbing, heating and other supplied systems working, and supply running water, reasonable hot water, and reasonable heat between October 1 and the last day of April where the landlord controls them. It also says the landlord is not required to make repairs when the renter is behind on rent. Source: https://code.wvlegislature.gov/37-6-30/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Off-Campus Housing** (WVU Campus and Community Life). Helps WVU students find housing and roommates, and gives information on apartment shopping, moving in and out, and renters' rights and responsibilities. Hosts housing fairs in the fall and spring with landlords and utilities. Has sublet and re-let information. Contact: OCH@mail.wvu.edu, 304-293-5611. For WVU students. Source: https://campuslife.wvu.edu/living-off-campus/off-campus-housing, checked October 30, 2026. Its FAQ urges a free lease review with Student Legal Services before signing, and says renter's insurance plans "can cost anywhere from $15-$30 a month." Source: https://campuslife.wvu.edu/living-off-campus/off-campus-housing/faq, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WVU Student Legal Services** (WVU). Helps with landlord-tenant disputes and other matters. For students currently enrolled at WVU who have paid the student activity fee. No extra fees, but students pay service of process and court filing fees if a case goes to court. Cannot help with cases against other students, student groups, WVU faculty or staff, or WVU. Apply through the Appointment tab; staff call or email to set a time. Bring leases, letters and emails. Accommodations: 304-293-4897. Email: SLS@mail.wvu.edu. Source: https://studentlegalservices.wvu.edu/slsfaq, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Morgantown Tool Outreach (MOTO)** (City of Morgantown). A tool lending program "for home repairs, property upkeep, and community improvement projects," for City of Morgantown residents and local organizations. Membership is free for city residents 18 and older; proof of residency and photo ID are needed at signup. Register in person or online through MyTurn and sign a liability waiver. Loans are 7 days with one 7-day renewal if no one else has reserved the tool. Late fee $1 per day per tool, full replacement cost after 30 days overdue, $5 cleaning fee may apply. Contact on the page: 304-241-3116, 200 M-Tec Dr. Morgantown, WV 26501. Source: https://morgantownwv.gov/720/Morgantown-Tool-Outreach-MOTO, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Section 8 and public housing** (Fairmont-Morgantown Housing Authority). Already in our record `fmha-section-8-and-public-housing`. Re-opened today: office at 103 12th Street, Fairmont; phone 304-363-0860; fax 304-366-0469. Source: https://fmhousing.com/visitors/faq_-_section_8.php, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Environmental Health** (Monongalia County Health Department). Regulates food, water, land, public spaces and businesses. Its "Housing and Institutions" program lists hotels and motels and manufactured home community rules (64 CSR 40) and a general sanitation regulation (64 CSR 18). Phone: (304) 598-5131. The page does not describe a rental housing complaint process. Source: https://www.monchd.org/departments-programs/environmental-health, checked October 30, 2026.
