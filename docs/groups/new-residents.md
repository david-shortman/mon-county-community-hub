## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **Morgantown Regional Office** (WV Division of Motor Vehicles). DMV office for licenses and vehicles. Page says "1525 Deckers Creek Blvd, Morgantown, WV 26505"; phone 304-558-3900, and 1-800-642-9066 (in-state only); "8:30 am - 5:00 pm Monday through Friday"; "walk-ins are always welcome" and "appointment based scheduling, including same-day appointments." The page does not list which services this office does. Source: https://dmv.wv.gov/node/118, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Moving to West Virginia** (WV DMV). New-resident rules. A valid, non-expired out-of-state license can be transferred; you must pass a vision screening, complete an alcohol awareness course, and surrender the old license. Expired license means all tests. Vehicles must be titled and registered "within thirty (30) days of establishing residency." Fees listed: title $15.00, lien $10.00, registration $51.50. The 6% titling tax does not apply if you already hold the title out of state (applied for within 30 days; not for leased vehicles). Insurance must be from a company licensed in WV. Source: https://dmv.wv.gov/moving-wv, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Online Voter Registration** (WV Secretary of State). Register or update online. Needs your name as on a WV driver's license or ID, date of birth, WV license or ID number, and last four of your Social Security number. Without a WV license or ID number you can fill the form online, print, sign, and mail or deliver it to your County Clerk, or use a blank form. The page says the deadline for the November 3, 2026 General Election has passed. Source: https://ovr.sos.wv.gov/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **GoVoteWV** (WV Secretary of State). Links to "Register to Vote or Update Your Registration" and "Check Your Voter Registration Record." Lists October 13, 2026 (Voter Registration Deadline) and November 3, 2026 (Election Day). Source: https://sos.wv.gov/govotewv, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Voter registration** (Monongalia County Clerk). Residents can register at the Clerk's office, "243 High St., Courthouse Room 123, Morgantown, WV 26505-5491", phone (304) 291-7230, "Monday - Friday: 8:30am to 4:30pm." Links to state online registration. Source: https://monongaliacountyclerk.com/index.php/voter-registration, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Voter Information** (City of Morgantown). Says the County Clerk keeps registration for city and county voters, and city residents use the same registration for city elections. "Voter registration closes on the twenty-first day before the election or on the first day thereafter which is not a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday." Polling places are by precinct, not ward; precinct is on your voter registration card; help from the City Clerk at 304-284-7434. Lists city precinct polling places. Source: https://morgantownwv.gov/590/Voter-Information, checked October 30, 2026 (WebFetch got 403, read with curl).
- **Ward Representative Interactive Map** (City of Morgantown). City maps page that links to an ArcGIS app titled "Morgantown City Council Lookup 2025" and the city GeoHub. Source: https://morgantownwv.gov/399/Ward-Representative-Interactive-Map, checked October 30, 2026. (App itself not tested; see Could not confirm.)
- **Neighborhood Associations** (City of Morgantown). City page lists nine associations with meeting times: Evansdale, First Ward, Greenmont, Jerome Park, South Hills, South Park, Suncrest, Wiles Hill-Highland Park, Woodburn. A way for new city residents to meet neighbors. Source: https://morgantownwv.gov/319/Neighborhood-Associations, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Monongalia County Assessor's Office**. "243 High Street, Room 335, Morgantown, WV 26505", 304-291-7222, Monday through Friday 8:30 am to 4:30 pm. Site menu links a GIS Parcel Viewer (ArcGIS) where you can look up an address. Source: https://www.assessor.org/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WV Property Assessment viewer** (WV Property Tax Division and WV GIS Technical Center). Statewide property search and parcel viewer. Page does not say it shows town limits. Source: https://www.mapwv.gov/assessment/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Get a Library Card** (Morgantown Public Library System). Free full card for West Virginia residents with proof of identity and proof of West Virginia residence (examples: driver's license or government ID, utility bill, car registration, lease, a current postmarked letter). Out-of-state residents $20 a year, in person. Digital-only card for Monongalia County residents by emailed application. Fine-free. Source: https://mympls.org/books-music-more/get-a-library-card/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Adult Programs** (Morgantown Public Library System). Free social programs (book discussion group, craft night, D&D for adults, Senior Lunch and Learn, LEGO Night, and others), plus Mango Languages (70+ languages) and TutorWV free tutoring. Source: https://mympls.org/programs/adults/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Literacy Volunteers of Monongalia and Preston Counties**. Free, confidential reading, writing, speaking and listening help for adults, for native English speakers and English language learners. "Hablamos Español." Call or text 724-491-2007, email director@lvmpc.org. Says 3 Zoom classes a week with a certified English teacher. Source: https://lvmpc.org/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Refugee Resettlement and Immigration Services** (Catholic Charities West Virginia). Refugee resettlement (housing placement, airport pickup, cultural orientation, ESOL and school enrollment, job prep) for groups federal law makes eligible (refugees, asylees, certain SIV holders, trafficking victims, Cuban/Haitian entrants, certain humanitarian parolees). Says it is "the only Refugee Resettlement agency in West Virginia." Also low-cost immigration legal help for WV residents (family petitions, green cards, DACA renewals, TPS, citizenship). Program phone (304) 343-1036, email immigration@ccwva.org. Page names no Morgantown office and says nothing about the program being paused or open. Source: https://www.ccwva.org/programs/west-virginia-immigration-refugee-resettlement-services/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Locations** (Catholic Charities West Virginia). Lists Monongalia County services by phone, including Refugee Resettlement and Immigration Services at 304.343.1036. No Morgantown street address on the page. Source: https://www.ccwva.org/locations/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Ascend WV**. Relocation incentive for remote workers who move to a Featured Community; "Morgantown Area" is one of six listed. $12,000 cash paid over two years, free outdoor recreation, gear rentals and a welcome trip, free coworking. Page says "Applications are now open at five Ascend communities!" (conflicts with six listed). Source: https://ascendwv.com/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Find a Doctor** (WVU Medicine). Search tool with "Accepting New Patients" and "Offers Online Scheduling" filters and a "Show Available Primary Care Appointments" option. No phone on the page. Source: https://wvumedicine.org/find-a-doctor/, checked October 30, 2026.
