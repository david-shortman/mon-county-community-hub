## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **Morgantown Board of Parks and Recreation Commissioners, BOPARC** (city parks agency). Runs city parks, trails, pools, an ice arena and community centers. Phone 304-296-8356, 705 East Brockway Ave, Morgantown, WV 26501. Customer service hours: Mon and Wed at Marilla Customer Service Office, 9:30 to 11:30 a.m. and 1:00 to 3:00 p.m.; Tue and Thu at Wiles Hill Community Center, 287 Eureka Drive, 8:00 to 11:30 a.m. and 1:00 to 3:00 p.m.; Fri at Marilla, 1:00 to 3:00 p.m. Parks listed: Krepps Park, Marilla Park, White Park, Dorsey's Knob Park, Wiles Hill Neighborhood Park, Dog Parks, Flegal Reservoir, Jack Roberts Park, Pocket Parks. Source: https://boparc.org/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **BOPARC trails** (BOPARC). Lists MRTC Rail-Trail (BOPARC helps maintain about 50 miles for MRTC), Krepps Park Trail, White Park Trails (hiking and mountain biking), Marilla Park Trails, Dorsey's Knob Trails, Whitemoore Park Trail (footpath from Woodburn to the Deckers Creek part of the rail-trail). Rules: shared use, bikes yield to people on foot, dogs on leash at all times (city ordinance), stay on official trails. Source: https://www.boparc.org/trails.html, checked October 30, 2026.
- **White Park** (BOPARC). 1001 Mississippi Street. "Five miles of wooded trails" used by hikers and bikers. Home of the ice arena. Source: https://www.boparc.org/white-park.html, checked October 30, 2026.
- **BOPARC Sports Complex: Ice Arena and Indoor Turf** (BOPARC). Public skating October through February; public sessions began October 16. Wed noon to 2:30 p.m. and 7:15 to 9:15 p.m.; Fri noon to 2:00 p.m. and 7 to 9 p.m.; Sat 1 to 3 p.m. and 7 to 9 p.m.; Sun 1 to 3 p.m. Admission: 3 and under free, youth $6, adults $7, seniors 55+ $6, skate rental $3; Wed and Fri noon special $7 with skates. Everyone 4 and up pays to enter, skating or not. Programs: Learn to Skate (with Morgantown Figure Skating Club), curling (Morgantown Curling Club), pick-up hockey, Totsicles (5 and under). Phone 304-296-8356 ext. 1. Source: https://www.boparc.org/boparc-sports-complex-ice-arena-indoor-turf.html, checked October 30, 2026.
- **BOPARC pools: Krepps Pool and Marilla Pool Complex** (BOPARC). Outdoor city pools. Page says "Marilla & Krepps Pools are CLOSED for the 2026 Season!" 2027 prices and dates are blank. Splash pad season ended October 12, 2026. Krepps: 1235 Parkview Drive. Marilla: 705 East Brockway Avenue. Phone 304-296-8356. Source: https://www.boparc.org/pools.html, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Mylan Park** (Mylan Park Foundation, Inc.). Sports, recreation and events park. 500 Mylan Park Lane, Morgantown, WV 26501, (304) 973-9733, info@mylanpark.org. Has an aquatic center, track, fields, sports courts, fitness center, an ice pavilion and a KOA campground. Source: https://mylanpark.org/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Peak Health Aquatic Center and Track Complex** (Mylan Park). Indoor pools (Huntington Community Pool, Competition Pool), splash pad, fitness center. 1847 Fitness Way, Morgantown, WV 26501, (304) 933-2782. Hours: Mon to Fri 6a to 8:30p, Sat 8a to 6p, Sun 11a to 6p. Track: dawn to dusk daily. Closed New Year's Day, Easter, Thanksgiving, Christmas. Day passes sold at the desk; price not listed online. Sources: https://mylanpark.org/facility-hours/aquatics/ and https://mylanpark.org/facility-hours/ and https://mylanpark.org/visit-passes/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Hope Gas Ice Pavilion public skating** (Mylan Park). 156 Mylan Park Lane, (304) 446-2539. Sat 2:30 to 4:30 p.m.; Sun 2:30 to 4:30 and 4:45 to 6:30 p.m. $10.50 with skate rental, $8.50 with own skates, 3% card fee. Extra Friday night and "Schools Out" sessions listed October through February. Sources: https://mylanpark.org/public-skating/ and https://mylanpark.org/facility-hours/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Monongalia County Parks and Recreation** (Monongalia County). Main office 270 Mylan Park Lane, Suite #150, Morgantown, WV 26501, 304-291-7013, parks@monongaliacounty.gov. Runs Camp Muffly Park, Chestnut Ridge Park and Campground, Mason-Dixon Historical Park. Online booking for pavilions, cabins, tent sites. Sources: https://monongaliacounty.gov/departments/parks_and_recreation and https://app.amilia.com/store/en/monongalia-county-parks--recreation, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Chestnut Ridge Park and Campground** (Mon County Parks). 346 Chestnut Ridge Camp Road, Bruceton Mills, WV 26525, (304) 594-1773. More than 30 primitive tent sites, 14 RV sites, seven cabins. Hiking into the WVU Forest. Winter snow tubing with a cable lift. Two ponds, fishing with no license required, catch and release only; pond beach for swimming. Daytime activities free, dawn to dusk. Page warns not to use Darnell Hollow Road to get there. Source: https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/departments/parks_and_recreation/chestnut_ridge_park_and_campground/index.php, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Mason-Dixon Historical Park** (Mon County Parks). 79 Buckeye Road, Core, WV 26541, (304) 879-4101. More than six miles of trails (including Fairy Door Trail), pavilions, a rustic cabin. Source: https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/departments/parks_and_recreation/mason_dixon_historical_park/index.php, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Camp Muffly Park** (Mon County Parks). 1474 4H Camp Road, Morgantown, WV 26508, (304) 284-8484. Open seven days a week, 8:00 a.m. until dark. Pool access, pavilions, historic log cabins. Local 4-H camps use it two weeks each summer. Source: https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/departments/parks_and_recreation/camp_muffly_park/index.php, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Coopers Rock State Forest** (WV State Parks). 61 County Line Dr., Morgantown, WV 26525, 304-594-1561, coopersrocksf@wv.gov. Hours "9 A.M. to 5 P.M." Overlooks, historic sites, climbing and bouldering, cross-country skiing, cabins and camping (campground April to November, weather permitting). Fishing at Messinger Lake with a WV license; hunting in season in set areas with a license. Free trail map PDF. Sources: https://wvstateparks.com/park/coopers-rock-state-forest/ and https://wvstateparks.com/parks/coopers-rock-state-forest/activities-at-coopers-rock-state-forest/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WV hunting and fishing licenses** (WV Division of Natural Resources). Buy online at WVhunt.com: new buyers pick "Enroll today!", past buyers use "Look up your Account". General number 304-558-6200, Mon to Fri 8:30am to 5:00pm. Source: https://wvdnr.gov/buy-a-license, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WV Free Fishing Days** (WV DNR). June 13 to 14, 2026: residents and nonresidents fish public waters with no license. Source: https://wvdnr.gov/free-fishing-days/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Country Roads Cyclists** (bike club). North central WV club since 1979. Group rides every Saturday and Sunday from March 1 to October 31, about 80 rides a season, plus a 100-mile Century ride each September. Membership fee not listed on the home page (form PDF). Source: https://www.crcyclists.org, checked October 30, 2026. Linked from the MRTC page.
- **Girls on the Run of North Central West Virginia** (nonprofit). Running and life-skills program for girls in grades 3 to 5 and 6 to 8. Serves Monongalia and 13 other counties. Says no girl is turned away for money reasons. info@gotrncwv.org, 681-320-3867. Source: https://www.gotrncwv.org, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Mon River Trails Conservancy links for groups** (MRTC). The trail info page lists local groups: Country Road Cyclists, Morgantown RoadRunners (Facebook group), Morgantown Area Paddlers (Facebook), Girls on the Run, Hike it Baby Morgantown (Facebook), Mountaineer Audubon, Friends of Deckers Creek. Source: https://montrails.org/rail-trail-info/, checked October 30, 2026.

## Build on list from the issue

- **Mon River Trails Conservancy / mon-river-rail-trails record**: confirmed. https://montrails.org/rail-trail-info/ still says 48 miles open, 8 miles paved in Morgantown and Star City, not plowed in winter. Matches our record. Maps at https://montrails.org/rail-trail-maps/.
- **BOPARC**: confirmed. https://boparc.org/ (parks, trails, pools, ice arena, programs).
- **Coopers Rock State Forest**: confirmed. https://wvstateparks.com/park/coopers-rock-state-forest/.
- **Morgantown Area Mountain Bike Alliance (MAMBA)**: could not confirm. No current site of its own found. The IMBA chapter page (https://www.imba.com/join/chapter/mamba) returned 403. See "Could not confirm".
- **City of Morgantown Bicycle Board group rides**: could not confirm. Nothing on morgantownwv.gov home page links to it. Newest leads are a March 2024 WV Public Broadcasting story and undated WBOY items. See "Could not confirm".

- **Chestnut Ridge Park**: confirmed (county park). https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/departments/parks_and_recreation/chestnut_ridge_park_and_campground/index.php.
