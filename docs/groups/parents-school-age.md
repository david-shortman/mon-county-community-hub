# Parents of school-age kids

Part of #261. Issue: #272. Researched October 30, 2026.

## What people in this group are trying to do

- "Which school does my kid go to at this address?"
- "How do I sign my kid up for school, or for kindergarten next year?"
- "When does the bus come, and how do I get alerts about delays?"
- "When are the days off, early dismissals and breaks this year?"
- "Do I have to fill out a form for free school lunch?"
- "Who watches my kid before and after school, and what does it cost?"
- "Is there free after-school help with homework at my kid's middle or high school?"
- "What can my kid do on days with no school?"
- "Where can my kid go to camp next summer?"
- "Where can my kid get free tutoring?"
- "What can my tween or teen do at the library or the parks?"

## Already on our site

- `/families-and-kids`: Pre-K and Head Start (Mon County Schools), Birth to Three, Starting Points playgroups, library story times. Stops at preschool. Nothing for kids in K to 12.
- `/family`: young kids, jobs and training, reading and English lessons, veterans. No school-age content.
- `/help/food/` and `/money`: food help, including the summer meals work tracked in #418.
- `/calendar/`: deadlines and dates. Does not yet carry the school year calendar.
- `/work-and-learning`: adult education only (MTEC, Literacy Volunteers, WorkForce WV).
- Records already in `content/`: `orgs/monongalia-county-schools.yaml` (health forms page only), `resources/mcs-prek-and-head-start.yaml`, `orgs/morgantown-public-library-system.yaml`, `resources/mympls-story-times.yaml`, `resources/starting-points-family-programs.yaml`.

## What exists locally (confirmed)

School basics (Monongalia County Schools, phone 304-291-9210, 1751 Earl L. Core Road, Morgantown, WV 26505):

- **Enroll Your Child page** (Monongalia County Schools). Says to contact your child's school before completing enrollment forms. Has a New Student Information Form for grades 1 to 12, a returning student form, a Pre-K enrollment link, and a kindergarten registration flyer. Source: https://www.boe.mono.k12.wv.us/page/enroll-your-child, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Kindergarten registration, 2026-27** (Monongalia County Schools). For children who are 5 on or before June 30, 2026. Online registration at tinyurl.com/MCS-Kstudentinfo, "no later than April 15, 2026." No internet or need help: 304-291-9210 ext. 1770. The 2027-28 dates are not posted yet. Source: https://aptg.co/6rjRtl (resolves to Kindergarten-Registration-Flyer-for-26.27 PDF), linked from the Enroll page, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Attendance Area Map** (Monongalia County Schools). An online map where you can search an address to see which schools serve it. The page says the map "should not be used to decide where to reside" because accuracy is not guaranteed. Questions about attendance: 304-291-9210. Source: https://www.boe.mono.k12.wv.us/o/mcs/page/attendance-area-map, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Transportation Department** (Monongalia County Schools). 2026-27 bus schedules for elementary, middle and high school, morning and afternoon. Families are asked to fill out an online bus enrollment form to get delay, shutdown and road closure alerts. Phone: 304-983-8215. Source: https://sites.google.com/boe.mono.k12.wv.us/mcstransportationdepartment/home, checked October 30, 2026.
- **2026-2027 At-A-Glance School Calendar** (Monongalia County Schools, updated 7/7/2026, WVDE approved 6/24/26). First day K-12 August 18, Pre-K August 19. Last day May 28 (marked with an asterisk: may change). No school: Sept 7, Oct 12*, Nov 3, Nov 11, Nov 23 to 27*, Dec 21 to Jan 1*, Jan 4*, Jan 18, Feb 15*, Mar 26 to Apr 2*, May 7*, May 31. 2-hour early dismissals: Sept 4, Sept 25, Oct 9, Nov 20, Dec 18, Jan 15, Feb 12, Feb 26, Mar 12, Apr 30. Asterisked days can become make-up school days under WV Code 18-5-45. Source: https://www.boe.mono.k12.wv.us/article/2813739 (links to https://aptg.co/n93BY_, the At-a-Glance-Calendar-2026-27 PDF), checked October 30, 2026.
- **Free school meals for all students** (Monongalia County Schools Child Nutrition). The "Child Nutrition Information for Parents 2026-2027" letter says all students get free breakfast and lunch through the Community Eligibility Provision, and no meal application is needed. Special diet requests go through the school nurse with a medical statement. Child Nutrition: 304-291-9210. Source: https://www.boe.mono.k12.wv.us/o/mcs/page/menus (letter at https://5il.co/3n3lg), checked October 30, 2026.
- **Before and after school care at elementary schools** (Monongalia County Schools page). Lists partner providers at nine elementary schools: Kidematics, (304) 906-3612 (Cheat Lake, Suncrest); The Shack Neighborhood House, (304) 599-5466 (Eastwood, Mylan Park, Ridgedale); The Source Foundation, (304) 276-8427 (Skyview); Visiting Homemaker Service, Morning and Afternoon Adventures, (304) 599-7743 (Brookhaven, Cheat Lake, Mountainview, North). Note: the table lists Cheat Lake under two providers. Source: https://www.boe.mono.k12.wv.us/page/before-after-school-care, checked October 30, 2026 (page blocked plain fetches with a JavaScript check; content read through the district's own site content service at https://thrillshare-cmsv2.services.thrillshare.com/api/v4/o/22300/cms/pages/before-after-school-care).
- **Free after-school programs at middle and high schools** (Monongalia County Schools). School-based, led by school staff, with tutoring, enrichment and clubs. Not held Fridays, bad weather days, early dismissal days or days off. Clay-Battelle Middle High "CB Time" Tues and Thurs 2:35 to 4:35 pm; Mountaineer Middle "Mountie Time" Mon to Thurs 2:35 to 4:05 pm; South Middle "Stallion Time" Wed and Thurs 2:35 to 4:05 pm; Suncrest Middle "Suncrest Time" Tues and Thurs 2:55 to 4:25 pm; Westwood Middle "Prime Time" Mon to Thurs 2:30 to 4:30 pm; University High "UTime" Tues to Thurs 2:30 to 5:00 pm. The page does not say whether there is a cost. Source: same page as above, checked October 30, 2026.

Before and after school care providers (own sites):

- **Before and After School care** (The Shack Neighborhood House). Grades K to 5 at Eastwood, Mylan Park and Ridgedale. Drop-off from 7:00 a.m., pick-up by 5:30 p.m. (5:00 p.m. on early release days). Follows the county school calendar. For 2026-27, online applications for Eastwood are closed and families must ask to join the waitlist; applications need shot records, a health assessment and the front page of the 2025 federal tax return. Address 537 Blue Horizon Drive, Morgantown, WV 26501. Phone (304) 599-5466, office hours Mon to Fri 9AM to 5PM. Source: https://the-shack.org/child-care/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Outside of School Environment (OSE) Days** (The Shack). Care on school days off, 7:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. at 537 Blue Horizon Drive, with STEM, reading and physical activities. $25 to $50 per child per day on an income sliding scale; pre-registration required. Source: https://the-shack.org/child-care/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Festival of Fun Summer Camp** (The Shack). For kids entering kindergarten through 12th grade, Mon to Fri 7:30 to 5:00, with breakfast, lunch and snack. Sliding-scale prices, $75 non-refundable deposit per child, scholarships mentioned. "Festival of Fun 2026 is now closed for enrollment." 2027 dates not posted. Source: https://the-shack.org/child-care/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Morning and Afternoon Adventures** (Visiting Homemaker Service, Inc.). Before and after school care for kindergarten to 5th grade, ages 5 to 12, at Brookhaven, Cheat Lake, Mountainview and North elementary schools. Daily fees by household income: $9 ($0 to $50,000), $10 ($50,001 to $75,000), $11 ($75,000+), plus $3 if a child attends both morning and afternoon. Rolling enrollment, no waitlist, one-time registration fee. Register online or at 382 Broadway Ave., Morgantown, WV 26505. Office (304) 599-7743. Source: https://www.visitinghomemakerservicewv.org/afternoon-adventures, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Before and After School Program** (The SOURCE Foundation). Before and after care at Skyview Elementary, 668 River Rd, Westover. Drop-off 7 AM, pick-up by 5:30 PM, with snack, homework help and STEM. "2026-27 Registration is Now OPEN." Monthly tuition, CCRC payments accepted. An "Extra Care Package" for early dismissal and teacher workdays is a one-time $200 fee. Office 540 Fairmont Rd., Morgantown, WV 26501, (304) 276-8427, Mon to Fri 8 AM to 5:30 PM. Source: http://www.thesourcewv.org/before-after-school-program and http://www.thesourcewv.org/contact-us, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Before and after school program** (Kidematics). Runs at Cheat Lake and Suncrest elementary schools; physical activity and social skills lessons. Phone 304.906.3612. Does not run on snow days, 2-hour delay mornings or early dismissal afternoons. Source: https://kidematics.com/beforeandafter.html, checked October 30, 2026. (Prices on the page are undated; see "Could not confirm".)
