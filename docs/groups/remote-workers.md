## What exists locally (confirmed)

- **Free Wi-Fi and public computers** (Morgantown Public Library System). The Services page says "Public computers and WiFi are available at all our locations. Computer use is free." The FAQ says "We offer free wi-fi and computers for public use at Morgantown, Arnettsville, Cheat, Clay-Battelle, and Clinton. Computers can be used for up to 60 minutes." For anyone. Source: https://mympls.org/services/ and https://mympls.org/about/faqs/, checked October 30, 2026. (The two pages word the locations differently: "all our locations" vs. a list of five that leaves out the Aull Center. Say what each says, or use the FAQ list.)
- **Quiet study tables** (Morgantown Public Library System). "Across our locations, we offer tables for quiet studying." No study rooms are listed. Source: https://mympls.org/services/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Meeting Room A and Meeting Room B** (Morgantown Public Library, main library). FAQ: "The Morgantown Public Library has two rooms available for public use: Meeting Room A and Meeting Room B." Services page: "Rooms are available for public meetings, community organizations and non-profit groups at no charge. Please reserve your room by calling 304-291-7425." For groups and public meetings, not stated as for one person's private work. Source: https://mympls.org/services/ and https://mympls.org/about/faqs/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Morgantown Public Library, main branch** (Morgantown Public Library System). 373 Spruce Street, Morgantown, WV 26505. Phone 304-291-7425. Hours: Monday to Thursday 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., Friday and Saturday 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., Sunday closed. Source: https://mympls.org/about/locations/morgantown/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Print, copy and fax** (Morgantown Public Library System). "Black and white copies are .10 each, .25 for color. Faxes are sent for $1.00 a page (charges will not exceed $5.00 total)." Notary at Morgantown; the FAQ says to call ahead. Source: https://mympls.org/services/ and https://mympls.org/about/faqs/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Drop-In Digital Navigator Hours** (Morgantown Public Library). Wednesday, November 4, 2026, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., MPL first floor. Help with devices, email and accounts, internet basics, online safety and scams, online forms, files and printing. Page does not state a cost or whether it repeats. Source: https://events.mympls.org/event/17866389, checked October 30, 2026. (One dated event; good for the calendar, not a standing fact.)
- **Tech Help** (Morgantown Public Library System). Staff help you use the library's digital materials on your own device; bring your card number and passwords. Source: https://mympls.org/services/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Library programs for adults** (Morgantown Public Library System). The library says it offers "programming for all ages at our locations across the county almost every week," with a calendar. A low-cost way to meet people. Source: https://mympls.org/about/faqs/ and https://mympls.org/calendar/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Ascend WV** (a remote worker relocation program; its site links to WVU privacy and WVU OEDC pages). The home page says "Applications are now open at five Ascend communities," and lists the Morgantown Area as one. The FAQ says "Rolling applications are open for all featured communities." The terms say selected people "will receive $12,000.00," paid "monthly over 24 (twenty-four) months" or as a lump sum after a qualifying home purchase. For people who live full-time outside West Virginia, are 18 or older, are U.S. citizens or green card holders, and work remotely full-time for a business based outside West Virginia (or are self-employed outside the state). It is not for people who already live here. The FAQ says participants get a "free coworking space membership"; it does not say non-participants can use the space. Source: https://ascendwv.com/, https://ascendwv.com/faq-2026/, https://ascendwv.com/terms-conditions-eligibility-requirements/, https://ascendwv.com/featured-communities/morgantown-area/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **WV Department of Economic Development, Office of Broadband** (state government). State speed test and survey, broadband maps (a Broadband Dashboard marked updated 07-24-26, a BEAD location story map), and county availability and program summaries dated September 2026. BEAD: the page says federal approval came November 21, 2025, and "Eight subgrantees are slated to provide high-speed internet service to more than 73,000 locations." Contact: 304-558-2234, WVBroadbandCouncil@wv.gov, Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Broadband Council meets online the second Thursday of each month at 10 a.m. (next listed: November 12 and December 10, 2026). Source: https://broadband.wv.gov/, checked October 30, 2026. Speed test: https://broadband.wv.gov/survey/.
- **Broadband service complaint form** (WV Attorney General, linked from the Office of Broadband and the county broadband page). A fillable PDF to complain about internet service. Source: https://broadband.wv.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/WVAG-Broadband-Complaint-Form-Fillable.pdf (link read on https://broadband.wv.gov/), checked October 30, 2026.
- **Lifeline** (FCC program run by USAC). "A federal program that lowers the monthly cost of phone or internet service." Up to $9.25 a month off. You may qualify through Medicaid, SNAP, SSI, Federal Public Housing Assistance, Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), and other listed programs, or with income at or below 135% of the 2026 Federal Poverty Guidelines (the page has a table by household size). Help line (800) 234-9473, 7 days a week, 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. ET. Source: https://www.lifelinesupport.org/ and https://www.lifelinesupport.org/how-to-qualify/, checked October 30, 2026.
- **Monongalia County Commission broadband page** (county government). Exists, but the main text is a press release about a broadband strategic plan delivered February 19, 2022 (the plan recommends a middle mile fiber backbone and targets western Mon County). It links the AG complaint form and the full report. No current project list or map of where new service is coming. Source: https://www.monongaliacounty.gov/government/commission_broadband_index.php, checked October 30, 2026.
