# "What was decided" lists from official minutes

- **Date:** 2026-10-31
- **Decided by:** Claude Code (under the charter), waiting for David because it sets public-facing policy
- **Decision:** Next to the agenda guides, each public body's page on `/meetings/agendas/<body>/` may list what its meetings in the last 30 days decided, from the posted minutes. These lists follow the agenda guide neutrality rules ([2026-10-31-2132](2026-10-31-2132-agenda-guide-neutrality.md)) plus these:
  1. **Only what the minutes record.** Each motion with its result and vote count, copied as the minutes write them. If the minutes give no result or count, the list says so and adds none.
  2. **Never the debate.** No summary of what people said, the reasons given, who spoke, public comment, or reports. No adjectives about a result.
  3. **Every motion, in order.** Routine ones too (approving minutes, executive session, adjourning). No picking or ranking.
  4. **Who moved and seconded is left out.** The minutes link has it.
  5. **Private people's names become "[name]"**, as in the guides: applicants, new hires, appointees, speakers. Bodies, companies and elected officials keep their names.
  6. **Say how final the minutes are.** Each list says whether the minutes are marked approved, are a draft, or say neither, and when we read them. Draft lists are re-read once the board approves the minutes.
  7. **The guide itself does not change.** Agenda guides still never show how a vote went (neutrality rule 5). The list is a separate record that links the minutes.
  8. **Scanned minutes** can be read from the page image, the same as a person would, and are copied word for word. If a word cannot be read with certainty, that action is left out and the note says so.
- **Why:** David asked for agenda guides to be extended to "what was decided" (#529). Residents often want to know what a board did, and minutes are hard to find and long. Copying only motions and results, as written, keeps the list a pointer to the official record rather than our account of the meeting.
