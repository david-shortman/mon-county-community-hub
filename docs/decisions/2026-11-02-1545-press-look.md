# The Press look becomes the site's design

- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Decided by:** David
- **Decision:** The site uses the "Press" look from the identity wave (#396) for everyone. The other two previews (Field guide and Ledger) and the design lab page are removed. The look's notes are in [`docs/design/looks/press.md`](../design/looks/press.md), and the rules are in [`docs/design/identity.md`](../design/identity.md) (version 3).
- **Why:** David found the old look clear but plain and generic. Three lanes built three directions as previews. He picked Press, asked for a spacing pass and a shorter phone header, and then asked to switch the site to it.
- **What changed for residents:** new colors (sandstone and hemlock inks, all text at 7:1 or more), ruled lines and square corners instead of rounded boxes, quilt marks on cards, a serif for headings on everyday pages, one spacing scale, and a shorter header on phones.
- **Limits kept:** urgent pages (the home page, Help now, food help, utility bills, after a storm and every crisis page) keep the Atkinson headings and never load the serif, so they stay under the 50 KB budget. The crisis strip, Quick exit and tab bar keep their layout. This replaces the October 30 "no drawn motif" rule with three plain quilt marks (see identity.md, "Quilt marks").
- **Cost:** $0. Newsreader is under the SIL Open Font License.
