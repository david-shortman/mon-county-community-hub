# Meet people where they are: through trusted helpers, and directly

- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Decided by:** David (asked for the ideas to shape issues, epics, and philosophy docs, then asked to stay open to direct and new kinds of reach); takes effect when he merges this PR
- **Decision:** The hub has two front doors. It makes trusted helpers' work easier (librarians, pantry and shelter staff, health workers, school coordinators, 211), and it lets anyone find help on their own, at any hour, with no name and no judgment. We stay open to new ways of sharing information that are not happening today. Principles and thirteen ideas live in [`docs/playbook/reaching-people.md`](../playbook/reaching-people.md), tracked in epic #155. A new belief in the charter points to it.
- **Why:** Helpers are trusted because they check information first, and outdated listings cost them time. But many people will not or cannot reach a helper: stigma and self-reliance hold them back, and need often comes after hours. Self-serve tools get used (77% against 14% for in-person help in one trial), as long as a person stays on offer.
- **What waits for David:** any contact with outside groups, printing and spending, a text line, and the no-account channel for reports and questions (#163, #99, #48, #23, #24).
