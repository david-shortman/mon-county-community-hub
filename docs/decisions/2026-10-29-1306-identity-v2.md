# Identity version 2: a bolder hub, answer first, rooted in place

- **Date:** 2026-10-29
- **Decided by:** David
- **Decision:** After the [Hub Identity Workshop](https://claude.ai/artifact/MR7VsryBth6f47tK2gnKmN) brainstorm, David chose:
  1. The personality set on the workshop sliders: serious-leaning, casual, matter-of-fact, bold, lightly seasonal, deeply rooted in Mon County, answer first.
  2. A mix of three directions: "Front Door" (ask first, answer in two taps) for the home page, "Trailhead" (routes and step lists) for every guide, and "Living Ridgeline" (a library of real local ridge profiles) as the identity layer.
  3. A free display typeface for headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, self-hosted. Body text stays on system fonts.
  4. 7:1 contrast (WCAG AAA) for all text, enforced in the build.
  5. Testing with residents twice a year, arranged by David (agents never contact anyone).
- **Why:** The hub already beats most of the web on weight and automated accessibility checks, but it had no look people would recognize and no front door that gets a worried person to an answer fast. Research for the workshop found that the strongest civic identities turn one local form into a system, and that need-first flows and step lists cut the time to solve problems. Cost: $0 for software and fonts. Resident testing may involve small thank-you gifts, which stay David's spending call.
