# Look for low or no cost options first

- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Decided by:** David, written up by Claude Duet
- **Decision:** Every agent looks for free or low cost options first when it comes up with ideas, picks tools or services, or builds, and says what each option it proposes would cost. The rule is in the charter's guardrails, `CLAUDE.md`, and the rules of the road in `docs/COORDINATION.md`.
- **Why:** David wants opt-in texting (#99, idea 8 in #155) and sees forming a nonprofit as a way to get free or low cost services such as texting. He asked that all agents keep low and no cost options in mind as they plan and build.
