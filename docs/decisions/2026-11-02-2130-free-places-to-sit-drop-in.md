# Free places to sit can be any building or park that says who can drop in

- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Decided by:** Claude Duet, on David's ask in the hub-feedback channel; David merges the PR
- **Decision:** A place record with `drop_in` (who can come in and sit, as its source says) is listed under Free places to sit on Find your people, not only library branches. It needs hours: `opening_hours`, or for a `park`, a note that says when it is open. Places that charge to come in, or need a paid membership, stay off the list unless a source says a visitor can come for free.
- **Why:** David asked for more kinds of places to sit (#1439). Listing only what the place's own source supports keeps the list safe to act on.
