# Each guide gets its own link preview picture

- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Decided by:** David (the direction), Claude (the layout)
- **Decision:** Link previews change from page to page. Every guide gets a share card with its own title, summary and door, and the logo kept at the top, drawn at build time. The Share this "Download image" button gives that same card. Crisis pages and pages about domestic or sexual violence keep the plain site picture.
- **Why:** Every link showed the same picture, and "Download image" always gave the same file. David asked whether previews in messaging apps could differ for every page, said the image could vary by category, the logo should stay, and "especially the title and text being varied." Details: "Share cards" in [`docs/design/identity.md`](../design/identity.md).
