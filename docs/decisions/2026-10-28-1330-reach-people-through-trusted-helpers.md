# Reach residents through the helpers they already trust

- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Decided by:** David (asked for the ideas to shape issues, epics, and philosophy docs); takes effect when he merges this PR
- **Decision:** The hub reaches residents mainly through trusted helpers (librarians, pantry and shelter staff, health workers, school coordinators, 211) and through warm handoffs to existing groups. Principles and eight ideas live in [`docs/playbook/reaching-people.md`](../playbook/reaching-people.md), tracked in epic #155. A new belief in the charter points to it.
- **Why:** Research found helpers are trusted because they check information first, and outdated listings cost them time; people often prefer to help themselves but need an easy person to turn to; QR codes alone barely get used without a person handing them over.
- **What waits for David:** any contact with outside groups, printing and spending, a text line, and the no-account report channel (#163, #99, #48, #23, #24).
