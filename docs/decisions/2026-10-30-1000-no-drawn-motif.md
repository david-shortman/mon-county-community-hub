# No drawn motif: the menu border is a plain line

- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Decided by:** David
- **Decision:** Remove the drawn ridgeline motif. The header and phone tab bar get a plain 1px line instead. No drawn shapes on the menu, pages, sections or titles. This replaces the skyline decision from earlier the same day ([`2026-10-30-0800-skyline-ridgeline.md`](2026-10-30-0800-skyline-ridgeline.md)).
- **Why:** Three tries at a drawn local motif (the original wavy hills, a named landmark per door, a tiny skyline of local shapes) each read as noise once seen on the page. David asked to remove the glyphs and find quieter ways to root the site in Morgantown. Hill green, brick edges, the heading typeface and local words stay. Cost: $0.
