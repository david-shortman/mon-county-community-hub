# The site is organized into Help now and five doors

- **Date:** 2026-10-30 (approved by David on 2026-10-29, built through 2026-10-30)
- **Decided by:** David
- **Decision:** The site is organized around one urgent lane (Help now, `/stay-tonight/`) and five doors named for what is going on in a person's life: Get through this month (`/money/`), Get care (`/care/`), Living here (`/living-here/`), Family and life changes (`/family/`), and Your community (no page yet). There are four levels: Help now, door, topic and answer page. The menu shows only Help now, the doors, Dates and All topics. On phones it is four tabs: Home, Help now, Dates and Topics. Every page names its door and topic in its own frontmatter, and the build makes the Topics page, breadcrumbs, door lists and search filters from those tags. Each door page is built from a data file (`content/doors/<slug>.yaml`) on one shared layout: pick what fits, then steps grouped Today, This week and When it opens. Adding, renaming or removing a door is David's call. The names are a first guess, tested in #254.
- **Why:** before this, every new page added a line to the menu, the footer and the home page list, and the menu already had eleven items. People search for the problem in front of them, so topic and answer pages keep problem titles, while the doors give people who browse a short, steady set of choices. Bills, rent and food are the top needs people bring to 211, and people rarely have just one, so each door gives one plan for all of them. Cost: $0. Built in #249, #250, #235, #251, #252 and #175, under epic #248.
