# The ridgeline becomes a straight line with tiny local silhouettes

- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Decided by:** David
- **Decision:** The wavy ridgeline under the menu becomes one straight line with very small, plain silhouettes of places here sitting on it: trees, a knob, hillside houses, brick downtown blocks, Wharf warehouses, the glass factory stack and a rail-trail bridge. It stays only on the menu border (header on wide screens, tab bar on phones). No landmarks or symbols on pages, sections, titles or cards, and no courthouse or other government building.
- **Why:** Issue #241 asked for a ridge profile per door. Two tries did not work: per-door wavy lines drawn from real elevation data read as scribbles at 16px tall, and a named landmark drawing beside each door heading was distracting. David asked instead for tiny, simple shapes of notable places, connected along a straight border, to give every page a little character without pulling attention. Cost: $0.
