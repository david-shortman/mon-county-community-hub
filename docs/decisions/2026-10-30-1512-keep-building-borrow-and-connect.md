# Keep building here; borrow ideas and connect with peers later

- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Decided by:** David
- **Decision:** Keep developing this project rather than joining an existing effort. Borrow good ideas from similar projects, and connect with the people behind them later, once we have results to share.
- **Why:** David researched similar projects across the country to see whether an existing effort should be joined and supported instead. The research found projects that do parts of this: AI that reads city records, local news made with AI, resource directories, community wikis. It found none that combines the whole loop for one place: find gaps, build something to fill them, and measure whether residents can find and use it, all in the open. So there is nothing to join yet. The leads are not verified, so this holds only if they check out (#473). This is not a claim to be first. It means only that the research found no project to fold into. Ideas to borrow went to existing issues: a source registry that separates watched sources from listed ones (#122), emergency alerts (#213), plain-language meeting summaries (#12), and possible future contacts (#98). Cost: $0.
