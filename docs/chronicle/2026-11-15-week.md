# Week three: a quiet start, then a two-day sprint

*November 9 to November 15, 2026. Written by the Chronicler, an AI session, from the public record.*

Week three had two halves. From November 9 to 13 the pace was slow: 1 to 7 pull requests merged a day. On November 14 David saw his usage meter at 50% with a day left before the reset, and a sprint ran on November 14 and 15. About 122 pull requests merged in those two days. The site now has an answer page for 74 of 76 test questions. Search engines still do not show it, so the main score did not move. The list of things waiting on David grew a lot.

- Total merged pull requests went from [908 on Nov 8](snapshots/2026-11-08/snapshot.json) to [1,052 on Nov 15](snapshots/2026-11-15/snapshot.json). That is 144 in the week, and 122 of them landed on Nov 14 and 15. The per-day counts are from the change in the running total, so they can differ by one from the lists inside each snapshot.
- Open issues ended the week at 276, up from 258.
- Issues labeled `needs-verification` rose from 100 to 119. These are facts left off the site until a current official page confirms them. Many end in a phone call. The [week 3 retro](../process/retros/2026-11-16.md) says nobody has been named to make those calls ([#165](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/165)).
- The `for:david` label count went from 124 to 173. Of the rise, 34 `for:david` pull requests were opened on Nov 14 and 13 on Nov 15, per the retro.
- The calendar grew from 92 to 95 entries.

## What shipped

- **New explainer pages that follow one case through its steps.** [Probate](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1944), [public records requests](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1933) and a [noise complaint](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1913).
- **Phone pages for money and fees.** The [county budget](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1873), the [transfer station](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1887), [Westover business license fees](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1879) and [farmers market vendor rules](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1912).
- **Your state lawmakers.** A page to [find your districts and each lawmaker's official page](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1942).
- **Recycling.** [Appliances, scrap metal and yard waste](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1779), then [batteries, motor oil, Freon appliances and the hazardous waste day](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1989).
- **Learning and internet.** [Free classes to read, learn English or use a computer](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1977), [steps to a high school equivalency diploma](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1961), and an [internet service page with the state's county numbers](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1976) and a [way to look up an address and challenge the map](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/2015).
- **Printable town sheets** with a date and a QR code ([#1792](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1792)), later set to [11 point body text](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1877).
- **Meeting pages.** Agenda guides for the [Land Reuse Agency](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1789), the [Board of Zoning Appeals](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1797), the [MPO Policy Board](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1802), the [Utility Board](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1804) and the [County Commission](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1816), plus "What was decided" pages for the [Nov 4](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1798) and [Nov 10](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1810) Commission meetings. The Nov 10 page uses draft minutes.
- **Facts that re-check themselves.** More pages read key facts from records, for example [selling food](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1954), [the contractor license page](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1865) and [parks](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1885).
- **Search and plain words.** [Bulk trash and DMV hours now find their pages](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1778), [city council and county commission find Who decides](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1846), and plainer "Who it's for" lines on [22 cards](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1790).
- **Pointing to the source.** A [source order review](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1911) trimmed or re-cited 21 listings that rested on news or a directory alone.
- **Upkeep.** Fixed broken links for the [Utility Board](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1803) and the [state highway office](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1795), and moved the [Nov 17 Utility Board date](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1815) to match its agenda.
- **A budget change.** Scheduled builds now run [four times a day](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1805).

The week also added 110 build log files, 35 discovery files and no new decision files. Examples of discoveries: [the County calendar does not show the Commission's Nov 24 move](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1947), [no NA page names Mon County](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1854) and [Blacksville has no official page for council meetings](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1845).

## How well can residents find answers?

- The [weekly run on Nov 15](../scoreboard.md) scored the original 32 questions at **76 out of 100**, the same as the baseline. All 76 questions scored 74, per the retro.
- **74 of 76 questions have an answer page on our site**, up from 63 of 65 a week ago, with 11 questions added to the test.
- Our pages still show up in none of the test searches, so by the method they cannot raise the score. Search engine setup waits on David ([#89](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/89)), now about 19 days old.
- On the urgent-needs benchmark, our pages meet the full bar on **6 of 9** needs. Eviction help slipped when Legal Aid closed online applications. The fix waits for David ([#1824](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/1824)).
- Use, action and recognition are not measured yet. No resident has been recorded as using the site.
