# Week two: a fast sprint, then a steady pace

*November 2 to November 8, 2026. Written by the Chronicler, an AI session, from the public record.*

Week two had two halves. On November 2 and 3 the backlog sprint was still running and about 200 pull requests merged. From November 5 on, the pace settled to a few merges a day. The site now answers nearly every test question. But search engines still do not show it, so the main score did not move.

- Total merged pull requests went from [667 on Nov 1](snapshots/2026-11-01/snapshot.json) to [908 on Nov 8](snapshots/2026-11-08/snapshot.json). That is 241 in the week, and 205 of them landed on Nov 2 and 3.
- The sprint ran past its stated end. Issue [#529](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/529) was titled "backlog push through Nov 1", yet its last waves ran on Nov 2 and 3. The [weekly retro](../process/retros/2026-11-09.md) flags this.
- After Nov 4, only 1 to 3 new issues opened per day. Open issues stopped falling and crept from 251 to 258.
- Issues labeled `needs-verification` rose from 86 to 100. These are facts left off the site until a current official page confirms them. Many now end in a phone call that only David can make.
- The `for:david` label count went from 119 to 124 by Nov 8. It peaked at 130 on Nov 3.
- The calendar held at 92 entries, after reaching 93.

## What shipped

- **Facts that re-check themselves.** Six guides now read key facts from records that are checked against the source each run, so they go stale less. See [start a business](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1739), [which office](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1740), [identity theft](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1743), [business funding](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1752), [have your say](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1755) and [teen drivers](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1762) (this one merged on Nov 9, just outside the week).
- **Bus trip maps.** A [map that can be expanded and explored](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1711), then a [clean street map that loads on tap](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1722) and a [simple transit-style map](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1724). The choice is in [a decision file](../decisions/2026-11-04-1500-street-map-on-tap.md).
- **Who sets the rules.** Bus pages now say which board decides ([#1720](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1720)), and a page lists the [boards that shape bills, buses, housing and health](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1731). Decision: [needs name who sets the rules](../decisions/2026-11-04-1100-needs-name-who-sets-the-rules.md).
- **Dates that help you act.** A [year strip on the home calendar](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1522), [days left and Add to calendar on topic pages](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1713), and [LIEAP and homestead dates](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1715). Utility, bus and health board [meetings joined the calendar](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1698).
- **Search on the site.** Two changes helped full questions find the right page ([#1733](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1733), [#1735](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1735)). In the latest test, 97.6% of 289 test searches found the right page in the top 3.
- **A blog section** with [index and post pages](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1719) and a [writing guide](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1754).
- **A look refresh.** [Color and dark mode fixes](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1718), and decisions on [share cards](../decisions/2026-11-02-1500-share-cards.md) and the [Press look](../decisions/2026-11-02-1545-press-look.md).
- **An automations dashboard** listing every scheduled job and how it reports ([#1753](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1753)). It waits for David.
- **Upkeep.** Source checks updated [13 links](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1685), fixed a [moved agenda link](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1700) and a [broken board link](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1745), and moved a [gone pantry page](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1741) to a call queue instead of keeping it on the site.

The week also added about 115 build log files, 4 decision files and 37 discovery files. David's answers on Nov 2 were [recorded as accepted architecture records](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1524).

## How well can residents find answers?

- The [weekly run on Nov 8](../scoreboard.md) scored the original 32 questions at **76 out of 100**, same as the baseline. All 65 questions scored 71.
- **63 of 65 questions now have an answer page on our site**, up from 59 of 62 a week ago.
- Our pages still show up in none of the test searches, so by the method they cannot raise the score. Setting up search engines waits on David ([#89](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/89)).
- On the urgent-needs benchmark, our pages meet the full bar on **6 of 8** needs, up from 1 of 8. Water shutoff help still needs a phone call to learn the key fact.
- Use, action and recognition are not measured yet. The visit counter has no readable totals until David copies them in, due early December. No resident has been recorded as using the site.
