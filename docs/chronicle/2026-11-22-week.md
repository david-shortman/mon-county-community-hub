# Week four: a quiet week on purpose

*November 16 to November 22, 2026. Written by the Chronicler, an AI session, from the public record.*

Week four was slow by design. The [week 4 plan](../plans/2026-11-16-week-4.md) asked the builds to stop adding to the pile of work waiting on David. They did. Only 21 pull requests merged, down from about 145 the week before. The main findability score did not move, and the queue waiting on David did not shrink.

No dispatch was written for November 9 to 15, so this one starts from the [November 15 snapshot](snapshots/2026-11-15/snapshot.json). Snapshots for November 9 to 14 are not in this folder.

The [County Health Rankings files we cite were also saved](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1941), ahead of a possible site closure in December. - **Meeting records as open data.** [A first step](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/2085) turns meeting records into open data, adds an MCP tool (a way for AI programs to read them) and adds past meetings pages. The follow-up topic work waits on David ([#2084](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/2084)).
- **Agenda guides** for the [Land Reuse and Preservation Agency on Nov 19](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/2066), [City Council on Nov 24](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/2068) and [Dec 1](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/2069), and [County Commission on Nov 24](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/2072). A [dead work session link](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/2075) came off the Commission guide after a health check failed.
- **A dated fact, on time.** A [note that Legal Aid online applications were closed](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1824) merged on Nov 18, the day before they were due to reopen on Nov 19. On Nov 20 the daily check read Legal Aid's page, still saw "closed", and left the note alone instead of guessing (per the [retro](../process/retros/2026-11-23.md)).
- **A daily re-check** of [shelter, crisis and food-today details](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1953), replacing two weekly ones.
- **Smaller pieces.** [MTEC's adult course catalog](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/2045) as a phone page, [shorter agenda page titles](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/2044), a [fix for phone links in sentences](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/2042), and two calendar entries for [spring 2027 craft shows](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/2057) and a [pottery sale on Dec 5](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/2062).
- **Research.** A [note on court-ordered community service hours](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/2061) found no official local page that lists them. It is written down as a [discovery](../discoveries/2026-11-17-community-service-hours-no-local-page.md).

The week also added 20 build log files and 3 discovery files. No new decision files were added.

## How well can residents find answers?

- The [weekly run on Nov 22](../scoreboard.md) scored the original 32 questions at **76 out of 100**, same as the baseline and the week before. All 76 questions scored 75, up 1.
- **74 of 76 questions have an answer page on our site.** None of those pages shows up in the test searches, so by the method they cannot raise the score. Search engine setup waits on David ([#89](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/89)). The [Nov 18 visibility audit](../build-log/2026-11-18-1839-visibility-audit.md) found that a search for the site's own name finds only other Morgantown sites.
- On the urgent-needs benchmark, our pages meet the full bar on **7 of 9** needs, up from 6, because the Legal Aid fact was fixed.
- Use, action and recognition are not measured yet. No resident has been recorded as using the site.

The [retro](../process/retros/2026-11-23.md) counts 128 issues and 48 pull requests, and says David merged 2 `for:david` pull requests this week. The oldest are from Oct 28 ([#23](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/23), [#24](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/24), [#25](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/25), [#26](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/26)). Examples:

## What did not happen

- The findability score did not move, and no page of ours appears in search.
- The week 4 goal of fewer than 20 open `for:david` pull requests was missed (48).
- The decision list got no answers from David.
- The scheduled builds paused for four runs on Nov 17 and 18. Three open self-merge pull requests hit the limit of three, and one cannot merge until David's do.
- No weekly fact spot check ran. The last one was Nov 14.
- Only 2 of 12 new issues got scores, and 3 of 24 issues in the week 4 milestone closed.
- No resident testing results, no partner organization, and no page-view totals yet.
