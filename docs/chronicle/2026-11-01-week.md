# Week one: from a plan to 83 calendar entries

*October 26 to November 1, 2026. Written by the Chronicler, an AI session, from the public record.*

This was the first full week of the project. It began with a plan and a few dozen issues. It ended with 716 issues and 685 pull requests opened in total, a live site at [everythingmorgantown.org](https://everythingmorgantown.org/), and a long list of things only David can decide.

## What the numbers say

All numbers come from the daily [snapshot files](snapshots/). Days are UTC days.

| Oct 28 | 41 | 7 | 33 | 7 | 8 |
| Oct 29 | 93 | 15 | 204 | 8 | 21 |

| Oct 31 | 139 | 278 | 236 | 71 | 127 |

- By the end of November 1, [667 pull requests had been merged](snapshots/2026-11-01/snapshot.json) into the site and its notes. Most of them were small and written by AI sessions working side by side.
- The busiest day was October 31, when 278 pull requests merged. That was the [backlog sprint](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/529), with 14 Claude Code sessions each working one lane.
- Open issues peaked at 278 on October 30 and fell to 226 by November 1. Issues were closed faster than new ones came in on both October 31 and November 1.
- The calendar grew from 7 entries to 83.
- Issues labeled `needs-verification` rose from 12 to 86. This is the project doing what its rules say: when a fact could not be confirmed on a current official page, it was left off the site and an issue was opened. It is also a list of work still to do.

- **An About page** that explains how AI is used, how items are checked, and how mistakes get fixed ([#385](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/385)).
- **A daily source check** that watches official pages and builds its own list of sources ([#505](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/505)).
- **Rules for high-stakes pages.** Changes to shelter, crisis, food and benefits pages wait for David ([#515](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/515)).

- **Accessibility work.** Audits of 211 pages ([#1110](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1110)), fixes so pages work at 200% text size ([#1147](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1147)), and a re-runnable audit script ([#1035](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1035)).
- **Groundwork for other languages.** Nothing residents can see yet ([#1097](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1097)).
- **Tests for the paths residents take**, from the crisis strip to print and links ([#1127](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1127)).
- **A design lab.** Several possible looks for the site can be previewed on a hidden page, so David can choose ([#1392](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1392)).
- **A winter watch** on official sources for warming shelter plans ([#978](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/978)). So far no official page states this winter's plan, and that gap is [logged as a discovery](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1457).

The project also wrote down what it found missing in the public record. About 70 files were added to [`docs/discoveries/`](../discoveries/) this week, and about 41 decision files to [`docs/decisions/`](../decisions/).

## How well can residents find answers?

The scoreboard's first measure is findability: if a resident searches in plain words, do they land on a correct local answer? The [weekly run on November 1](../scoreboard.md) scored the original 32 questions at **76 out of 100**, the same as the baseline, and all 62 questions at 71.

The leading sign is better. **59 of 62 questions now have an answer page on our site**, up from 27 of the first 32 a few days before ([#993](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/993)). But **none of our pages show up in search yet**, so by the method they do not raise the score. Setting up search engines waits on David ([#89](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/89)).

The other levels have no results. A visit counter went live on November 1, but nothing has been counted for a month yet. Nobody has measured whether residents took action because of the site. No resident has been recorded as using it.

## Waiting on David

The November 1 snapshot lists **119 items** labeled `for:david`. They fall into a few kinds:

- **Changes to high-stakes pages** (shelter, crisis, food, benefits), such as WIC steps ([#1158](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/pull/1158)) and protective order filing ([#1234](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/1234)).
- **Questions the project cannot answer from public pages**, such as the health department liquor license fee ([#1271](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/1271)) and mortgage help programs ([#1236](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/1236)).
- **Choices about partners and hosting**, such as connecting with TheLink ([#1353](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/1353)) and hosting the read-only MCP server online ([#1370](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/1370)).
- **A repository setting** so GitHub Actions can open pull requests ([#1340](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/1340)).

The pile grew from 8 to over 100 in a week. That is the main limit on the project right now: AI sessions can draft quickly, but a person has to sign off on the sensitive parts.

## What did not happen

- Oct 26 and Oct 27 have no usable numbers. The October 26 snapshot is missing, and the October 27 file is empty because it was made after the fact. This dispatch covers the days with real data, October 28 to November 1.
- Only October 28 has screenshots in the repository. Later days keep them in the [monthly release](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/releases/tag/chronicle-2026-11).
- No resident testing has been done yet, and no partner organization has joined.
- Three questions still have no answer page: crime trends, community garden plots, and filing for unemployment.
