Call 211, text your ZIP code to 898-211, or chat at wv211.org. It is free, open all day and night, and they know who has room.

About this mockup

  • Works with no script. A tiny script only adds the Open now badges, worked out from the phone's clock in Eastern time.
  • Hours always show in words, so the badge is never the only way to know.
  • Every icon has a word next to it. Every button is at least 48px tall.
  • Directions and Text it are placeholders here. On the real page they open the phone's map and texting apps.
