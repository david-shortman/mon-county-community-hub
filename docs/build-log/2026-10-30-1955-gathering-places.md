# 2026-10-30: Gathering places record and the first 19 places (#455, #456)

- **Considered:** the community centers section (#450) needs one record that also works for the groups and third spaces directory (#263). ADR 0001 names a `locations` type; `calendar/` already uses `place` for an address, so events link to a center with a new `venue` field instead.
- **Chose:** `content/places/<slug>.yaml` with a JSON Schema (`content/schemas/place.schema.json`, version 1). `check-content` validates every file against it with a small built-in validator, so the schema is the rule, not just a document. The stale rule (#39) lives in `site/src/lib/places.mjs`: an active place with no activity seen in 6 months reads "No recent activity seen, call first."
- **Shipped:** 19 places, every source re-read today: 13 community-run centers, senior centers and neighborhood houses (Smithtown, Arnettsville, McCurdysville, Westside, Clay-Battelle, Senior Monongalians, The Shack, Rock Forge, and the county buildings at Triune Halleck, Core, Blacksville and Daybrook), five public buildings (Wiles Hill, Marilla, Dorsey's Knob Lodge, Mason-Dixon Historical Park, Mylan Park, Mon County Center) and the Cheat Lake VFD hall.
