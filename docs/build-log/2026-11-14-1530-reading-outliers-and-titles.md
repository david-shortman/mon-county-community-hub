# Reading outliers and three shorter titles

**Reading grade outliers (#1554).** The last three page openings above grade 9 are now plainer: Identity theft (9.6 to 5.6), Choosing a care home (9.2 to 5.4) and Our checks (9.1 to 5.9). Each long sentence was split; no fact changed.

The community center flyers read at grade 9.8 to 10.6 for the whole page. The cause was the web address line ("More, with sources: everythingmorgantown.org/community-centers/...") and the source footer, not the flyer's own words. The web address line now has the class `flyer-url`, which the reading check skips like the Pass it on address. The footer now says "Facts from ..." and "Made by Everything Morgantown". Flyers now read at grade 5.3 to 8.4, and no page is above grade 10 for its whole text.
