# 2026-11-01: After you apply for the homestead exemption

- **Changed:** new page `/after-you-apply/homestead/`: the Assessor's review, the written denial notice by January 1, the 30 day appeal to the County Commission (decided by February 28), how the exemption shows on the tax bill, when to tell the Assessor about a sale, move or change in disability (before July 1, and within 30 days for disability benefits), and how to claim the Senior Citizens Tax Credit and the Homestead Excess Property Tax Credit. Part of #1148 (sprint #529).
- **Why:** the Property tax page explains how to file, but not what happens after, how to appeal a denial, or the two state credits many homestead owners may not know about.
- **Sources:** W. Va. Code §11-6B-3 to §11-6B-7, §11-6B-10, §11-21-21 and §11-21-23; the Monongalia County Assessor's Real Estate page and SmartFile guide; the State Tax Division's Senior Citizens Tax Credit page and 2025 Schedule HEPTC-1. Read November 1, 2026.
- **Gaps:** no official page says whether the Assessor sends an approval letter, and §11-6B-6 points to §11-3-25 for circuit court review, which is repealed, so no court deadline is stated. The WV Family Tax Credit was not given its own page: it has no application step beyond attaching Schedule FTC-1 to the state return, which `/free-tax-help/` already covers.
- **Cost:** $0.
