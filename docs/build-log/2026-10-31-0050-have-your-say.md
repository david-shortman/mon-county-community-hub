# 2026-10-31: Have your say at a public meeting

- **Considered:** #409 asks for a process-only "how to speak at a meeting" page for City Council, the County Commission, BOPARC, the Planning Commission and the Board of Education. Open seats are already on Serve on a city board (#290).
- **Chose:** one page, `/have-your-say/`, a card per body: when it meets, how comment works, where the agenda is, and who to call. Facts only from each body's own pages and agendas.
- **Found:** BOPARC's agenda sets a 3 minute limit and one speaker per group. The Planning Commission page sets 5 minutes and asks for written evidence 5 days ahead. City Council agendas have a "public portion" under rules Council sets by resolution, but we did not find those rules online; a May 6, 2025 Council policy allows comment by phone or in writing when a meeting has public comment. The County Commission and the Board of Education pages give meeting times but no speaking rules, so the cards say so and give the office number.
- **Also:** plain-language fixes from the #708 review ("direction signs", and "501(c)(3)" explained as registered charities).
- **Not yet:** the agenda calendar with short summaries from #409. It needs a "checked before it goes up" step and is a bigger build.
