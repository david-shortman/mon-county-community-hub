# Plain language: "Who it's for" on 6 low-stakes cards (round 5)

**What changed.** New wording for the "Who it's for" line on 6 resource cards whose first two sentences read above grade 9: Christian Help's Free Store, DMV lost plates and decals, County Clerk voter registration, Voter ID, the Medicaid 2027 help line and Toys for Tots. Wording only: no fact, number, name, date, phone or link changed, and quotes stay word for word.

**Result.** Their average opening grade went from 11.2 to 7.7. Cards above grade 9 on the build: 86 to 82 of 393 (one more is in the high-stakes PR).

**Left alone.** Special Olympics (a plainer split did not lower the grade), cards with a Spanish translation, and cards whose grade comes from a required name or term. High-stakes cards are in a separate PR that waits for David.
