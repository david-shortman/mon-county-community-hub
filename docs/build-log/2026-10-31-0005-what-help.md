# 2026-10-31: "What help might I get?" in two questions

- **Considered:** #188. Many people never ask a person, or need help at night. The food questions and the door pages' "pick all that fit" steps already cover what a person needs, but there was no single way in, and nothing asked who lives in the home.
- **Chose:** a two-question path at `/help/what-help/`: what do you need most (food, a utility bill, rent, a place to stay, cash or another bill, care, work), then does anyone at home fit a group (a child under 5, someone 60 or older, someone with a disability, a veteran, or skip). The answer page links to the existing pages that may help, including the door's own steps for that pick, and always offers 211. It says "you may qualify" and that only the program can say for sure. No name, nothing saved, works with no scripts: each answer is a plain link.
- **Shipped:** `src/lib/what-help.mjs` (the choices, no facts), three page files, and generated `llms.txt` lines.
- **Waits for David:** it is in the Get through this month door and names 211.
