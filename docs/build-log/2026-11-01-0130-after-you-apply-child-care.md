# 2026-11-01: After you apply for child care help

- **Changed:** new page `/after-you-apply/child-care/`: the interview within 5 days, papers due within 13 days, the certificate and sliding fee, checking status with the Child Care Resource Center, the 13 day notice and hearing rules (including when care does not continue), reporting changes within 5 days, and the 12 month status check due on the 15th. Part of #1148 (sprint #529).
- **Why:** the site says where to apply for child care help but not what happens next. The manual's short deadlines (13 days for papers and for keeping care during an appeal) are easy to miss.
- **Sources:** WV DoHS Child Care Subsidy Policies and Procedures Manual, dated October 1, 2024, linked from the Bureau for Family Assistance child care policy page. Read November 1, 2026.
- **Gaps:** the manual sets no number of days to decide an application, and it words the appeal deadline two ways (60 days "after the action was taken" and "of the notification"). The page quotes both and says to go by the letter.
- **Cost:** $0.
