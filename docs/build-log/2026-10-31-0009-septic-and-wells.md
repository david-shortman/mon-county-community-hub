# 2026-10-31: Septic systems and well water (#662)

- **Considered:** homeowners outside public sewer and water ask what to do about a failing septic system, a new well, or unsafe well water (#271). Nothing on the site covered them.
- **Chose:** four Monongalia County Health Department records (septic permit, well permit, well water test, and the septic and well check when a home is sold), each with the fee from the department's fiscal year 2026-2027 fee schedule, shown on a new `/septic-and-wells/` page that links to `/permits/`.
- **Separate:** the Wastewater Revolving Loan Fund goes in its own PR that waits for David, because it is about money, and the Health Department web page and the loan application show different income tables.
