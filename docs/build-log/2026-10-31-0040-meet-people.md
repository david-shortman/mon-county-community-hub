# 2026-10-31: Meet people and get a library card (#626, #627, #669)

- **Considered:** new residents and people who work from home ask how to get a library card and how to meet people. The site had no library card record and did not list Morgantown's neighborhood associations.
- **Chose:** one page for both, since the library is the first free stop for each. Neutral list of every association the city names, in the city's order, with meeting times in the city's words.
- **Shipped:** resources `mpls-library-card` (free for WV residents with ID and proof of WV address, $20 a year out of state, digital-only card for county residents) and `mpls-adult-programs` (free adult programs and the library calendar), from mympls.org; `morgantown-neighborhood-associations` from the city's page. Page `/meet-people/` with a short "if you work from home" section, and a pick on the Your community door.
- **Left off:** young professional and leadership groups, and library makerspace hours (#665). Community centers (lane 5) and volunteering (lane 9) are linked, not repeated.
