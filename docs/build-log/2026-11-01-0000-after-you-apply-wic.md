# 2026-11-01: After you apply for WIC

- **Changed:** new page `/after-you-apply/wic/`: the certification visit (proof of residence, identity and income; infants and children must come), 30 days to bring proof of pregnancy, decisions within 10 or 20 calendar days, keeping WIC (eWIC card, appointments, reporting changes), and fair hearings within 60 days with benefits continued if you appeal within the 15 day notice period. Part of #1148.
- **Sources:** West Virginia WIC policies 1.07, 2.01, 2.05 and 2.09, and the WIC Participant Agreement (WIC-05), read October 31, 2026. Policy 2.05 is dated 1997 but is the posted version.
- **Cost:** $0.
