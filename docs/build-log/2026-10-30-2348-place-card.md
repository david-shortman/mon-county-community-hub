# 2026-10-30: Place card says "Open now" and shows how to get in

- **Considered:** #176 asked for one card for any place a person can go: hours in words, an open now badge from the phone's clock, and word tags.
- **Chose:** `PlaceCard` reads the new listing fields (#177). With `opening_hours`, a badge says "Open now. Closes at 3 p.m." or "Closed now. Opens Monday at 5:30 a.m.", worked out in Morgantown time on the phone. It stays hidden without scripts, and the hours in words always show with "Call to confirm." With `get_in` or `languages`, the card shows word tags with a small drawn icon; they are plain text, not buttons. With `access`, it adds an Access line. Records without these fields look exactly as before, so the Stay tonight cards do not change.
- **First use:** the Mountaineer Transfer Station on `/recycling/` is now a place card, with `opening_hours` from the county's disposal page (read October 30, 2026).
- **Checked:** the badge at five set times, including the November 1 clock change; hidden with scripts off; `npm run check-a11y` clean on every page.
- **Cost:** $0.
