# Volunteer to prepare free tax returns

**What:** A record for the IRS's tax volunteer program (VITA and TCE) and a new "Use a skill you have" section on `/volunteer/`. Closes #1084, from the "more ways to act" list in #411 (idea 1, score 14), part of #398.

**Why:** Free tax sites need volunteers before filing season starts in January, and the IRS trains them. The volunteer page had no skill-based roles yet.

**Choices:**
- Topic `community`, not `money`: this is a way to give time, not a way to get money, so the page does not wait for David.
- We say only what the IRS page says: training online or in a classroom, sites generally open nights and weekends, flexible hours, and that the IRS shares your sign-up with groups near you. The page does not say training is free, so neither do we.
- Linked only. The sign-up form is the IRS's; this site collects nothing.

**Source:** https://www.irs.gov/individuals/irs-tax-volunteers (page last updated Aug 20, 2026), read 2026-10-31.

**Cost:** $0.
