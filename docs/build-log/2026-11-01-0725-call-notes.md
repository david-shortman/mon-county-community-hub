# 2026-11-01: Notes for this call, saved on the phone

- **Considered:** #1071 (sprint wave 9, phone design, #529). David asked for the "Before you call" ideas to show up where the Call buttons are, and for the page to work like a phone tool: jot down what an office says and keep it, with no account. Cards with a "What to have ready" list already show it under their Call button (`WhatHappensNext`, #159). Notes taken during the call, like a reference number or a promised callback date, had only a printable blank on `/before-you-call/`.
- **Chose:** a new `CallNotes` block under the Call button on every resource card that lists what to have ready (36 records). It has five boxes: when I called, who I talked to, what they said, a reference number, and what I do next. What you type is saved only in this browser on this phone (localStorage, one note per service), with Text to myself (an `sms:` link holding the service, the number, the notes and the page), Share or copy (the phone's share sheet, or copy), and Delete my notes. Nothing is sent to us and no tracking was added. The block says anyone who uses the phone can see the notes, and Delete asks first, since a note may be the only copy of a reference number. With no script the block stays hidden, since it could not save; the checklist and the printable notes still work. Added to the Building blocks table in `docs/design/presentation.md`.
- **Left for later:** the "have ready" list on door steps (Get through this month steps wait for David) and the call scripts next to utility, rent and hospital bill Call buttons (those pages wait for David).
