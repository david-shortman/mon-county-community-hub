# 2026-11-14: Family door, disability and aging choices (#248, #261)

- **Considered:** the Family and life changes door promises "disability and aging" on the home page, but its picker had one caregiving choice with no steps. A person with a disability, or someone whose parent needs help at home, had to scroll the long page list to find their page.
- **Chose:** four new choices and a step for the caregiving one. Each step points to a record already on the site, so the door states no facts of its own:
  - I have a disability and want help living on my own: LiveAbility.
  - I need a ramp, grab bars or other changes to my home or car: LiveAbility's CLSP.
  - I have a disability and want help finding or keeping a job: the DRS Morgantown office.
  - An older family member needs help at home: Senior Monongalians in-home care, then the Aging and Disability Resource Center.
  - I'm caring for a family member: the Aging and Disability Resource Center.
- **Waits for David:** the door file already holds the Veterans Crisis Line, so the High stakes check labels any change to it.
- **Measured:** simulated walks, before and after, in `content/findability/walkthroughs/2026-11-14-doors.yaml` (from the site plan and doors PR). Cost: $0.
