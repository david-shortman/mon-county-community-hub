# Sign up as an organ donor, on the IDs page

**What:** A record for organ, eye and tissue donor sign-up and a short section on `/ids-and-records/`. Closes #1085, from #411 (idea 2, score 14), part of #398.

**Why:** People already come to this page to get or replace a license or ID card, and the DMV asks about organ donation on that same application. One short section lets them read about it before they go, or sign up later online.

**Choices:**
- Neutral: the page says what the DMV offers and adds "It is your choice either way." No numbers or appeals.
- The online link is the Donate Life form (registerme.org). The DMV's page links to Donate Life for signing up or managing your status online.
- Left off: age rules, and news reports from July 2026 that West Virginians can also sign up when registering to vote or buying a hunting or fishing license. We did not find those on an official page we could read, so they are not on the site.
- No cost line: neither page states one.
- The DMV's old organ donor page address (transportation.wv.gov) now redirects to a page that is not found. The current page is at dmv.wv.gov.

**Sources (read 2026-10-31):** https://dmv.wv.gov/driver-services/drivers/organ-donor-registry and https://registerme.org/

**Cost:** $0.
