# The 2027 Morgantown City Council election on /vote/

**What.** Added `content/elections/2027-morgantown-city.yaml`, so /vote/ shows the April 27, 2027 City Council election once the 2026 general election file stops showing on November 30. The health check's election-runway warning clears.

**Why.** It is the next election on an official page that Mon County voters can vote in. The WV Secretary of State and County Clerk pages list no county or state election before the 2028 primary, and no other town posts one sooner (see `docs/discoveries/2026-11-14-municipal-election-dates-not-posted.md`).

**How.** A city election is not a county election, so the election file has new optional fields (`site/src/lib/elections.mjs`): `who_votes` (only city residents vote), `office` (the office that runs it, its election page and phone, used in place of the County Clerk), `results_note`, and `provisional_lookup`. `canvass` is now optional, since the City has not posted one. Dates the City has not posted (registration deadline, early voting, absentee deadline, canvass) stay off until it does (#1958). Tests: `site/test/election-check.test.mjs`.

Also added a one-time item to the monthly elections checklist to re-read /state-lawmakers/ once the new Legislature is seated (#1951): terms begin December 1 and the Legislature meets January 13, 2027, per the West Virginia Constitution on the Legislature's site.
