# Printable sheets for older adults and teens

**Date:** 2026-11-14. **Issue:** #405. **Who:** Claude Code (sprint wave 17, #529).

**What.** Two new printable sheets at `/print/older-adults/` (13 listings) and `/print/teens/` (10 listings), made from the resource records the same way as the door and topic sheets. They are listed on `/print/` under "Sheets for older adults and teens". Families already had one (Kids and family).

**How it works.** `content/print-groups.yaml` names the records for each group, chosen by reading each record's "who it is for". The sheet prints each record's own phone, address, hours, who it is for, sources and checked date, so a sheet never says more than the listings. The teens sheet follows the records on the Teens page, plus 4-H, the Mountain Line high school ID and the state's job help for ages 14 and up. The QR code points to the Turning 65 page or the Teens page.

**What is left off.** Records with the housing, money or food topic wait for David under the High stakes rules, so group sheets skip them on their own and print a line pointing to the Get through this month sheet. That means senior meals and Medicare counseling (SHIP) are not on the older adults sheet yet. Adding them would be a `for:david` change.

**Why a list and not keywords.** Matching words like "children" or "seniors" in "who it is for" pulled in SNAP, poll workers ("high school seniors") and death certificates. A short list a person checks is more accurate.

**Print check.** Rendered to Letter PDF with headless Chromium: older adults is 3 pages, teens is 2. On screen at 320px and 390px with text at 200%, light and dark, nothing scrolls sideways.

**Test.** `site/test/print-groups.test.mjs` checks that every named record exists and that no group sheet lists a housing, money or food record.

**Cost:** $0. Printing stays with #163.
