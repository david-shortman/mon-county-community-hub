# 2026-11-14: Follow one public records request, an explainer on /public-records/ (#1931, part of #1440)

- **Considered:** what happens after a SNAP application, an eviction notice or a utility shutoff notice. Those are benefits, housing and deadline pages: the motion guide keeps deadlines out of moving pictures, #1452 already covers benefit timelines and waits for David, and the food and housing lanes own those pages this wave. A public records request is a process people ask about, with a clear state rule, and nothing on the page needed new research.
- **Chose:** a five-step scroll story right under "How to ask": you send a written request, the office has five days not counting Saturdays, Sundays or legal holidays, one of three answers, what copies can cost, and asking the circuit court if the answer is no. A request marker moves from you to the office to the answer, and in the last step on to the court. A strip of days fills in five weekdays with the weekend left gray.
- **Accuracy:** every step quotes W. Va. Code 29B-1-3 and 29B-1-5, re-read on November 14, 2026, the same text `content/resources/wv-foia-request.yaml` already quotes. The law does not say which day the five-day count starts, so the strip has no weekday names and the drawing says it is an example week.
- **Shared shell:** `site/src/components/StepJourney.astro`, made from the complaint explainer (#1913): the still numbered list, `MotionSwitch`, `watchSteps`, the held drawing on phones and the side-by-side layout on wide screens. Each explainer brings only its steps, its drawing and the CSS for what each step shows. The complaint explainer can move onto it later; it was left as is to keep this PR small.
- **Checks:** still list with no script, reduced motion or "Turn off motion"; drawings `aria-hidden` with every fact in the words; 320px and 390px at 200% text, light and dark, with no sideways scroll. Test: `site/test/records-journey.test.mjs`. Cost: $0.
