# Secondhand cards: what a place takes, does not take, and drop-off

**Issue:** #690 (follow-up from #684)

Long `takes` lines in five entries (three Goodwill stores, the Ranch Community Store and ReTails) mixed what a store takes, what it turns away and when to drop off. The format now has two optional fields, `does_not_take` and `drop_off`, and cards show each on its own line. The words are the same as before, only split up, so the checked dates did not change.
