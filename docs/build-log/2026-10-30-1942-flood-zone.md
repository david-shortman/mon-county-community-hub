# 2026-10-30: Is my home in a flood zone or a slide area?

- **Considered:** flash flooding and landslides are among Mon County's most common hazards (#213 research), and people moving, renting or building had no plain page on how to check an address.
- **Chose:** a short page with only free public maps and official pages: the WV Flood Tool, FEMA's Flood Map Service Center, FloodSmart's insurance basics, the county floodplain permit, and the WV Landslide Tool with its own caveat that it is for planning, not one lot. Counts of flood events and buildings at risk were left off because we could not read the reports (#553).
- **Shipped:** `/flood-zone/` in the Living here door under a new topic, Storms and floods. Linked from Moving here and llms.txt.
