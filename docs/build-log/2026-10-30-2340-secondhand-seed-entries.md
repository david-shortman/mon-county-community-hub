## Choices

- Facebook yard sale groups are not listed: we could not open any group's own page (epic rule).
- Shops with no website link to the dated news story that confirms them, labeled "News story".
- No phone numbers in this folder. Each card links to the place's own page.
- Status follows the format in `content/README.md`: `confirmed` needs a 2025 or 2026 source or a current own page; `likely` cards say to check before you go.
