# 2026-11-01: The needs list now says where to get help on this site

- **Considered:** the needs register (`/needs/`, #984) named 12 problems, the groups working on each and the gaps, but never pointed to our own pages for that need. A reader who saw "Getting mental health care" had no link to `/mental-health/`. Several listed gaps were also out of date: we had since built `/medicaid-2027/`, `/drugs-and-alcohol/`, `/renting/` and the child care search link.
- **Chose:** two optional fields in `content/schemas/need.schema.json`. `get_help` lists our pages for the need, best first, and shows as "Where to get help on this site" near the top of each card. `site_coverage` says how well the site helps today (good, partial or thin) and why. `check-needs.mjs` fails if a `get_help` link points at a page that does not exist. Filled both for all 12 needs, removed or rewrote four gaps our pages have filled, and added 10 groups already on file to "who is working on it". Full review: `docs/research/needs-coverage.md`.
- **Measured:** pages linked from `/needs/` to help: 0 before, 46 links across 12 needs after. Coverage today: 8 good, 3 partial (housing, child care, wages), 1 thin (diabetes and heart health).
- **Cost:** $0.
