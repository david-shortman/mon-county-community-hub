# 2026-11-14: Titles no longer split words at big text on small screens

- **Considered:** a wave 15 check found that at 320px with text at 200%, a blog post title broke "Everything" mid-word with no hyphen. The cause is shared: the Press h1 is about 67px at that setting, so the column holds about 8 letters, and `main` lets any word break anywhere. A scan of all 1,015 pages at 320px and 200% text found 293 title (h1) words, 223 h2 words and 393 h3 words split across lines.
- **Chose:** one rule in `site/src/styles/press.css`. `main` becomes a size container, and when its column holds less than 14em of its own text (only when someone has made text big on a narrow screen), headings step down one size on the existing scale and shrink a little more with the column (`cqi`), and break long words at a syllable with a hyphen (`hyphens: auto`). A media query could not do this, because its em ignores the page's text size. Normal screens and normal text sizes are unchanged. The community center flyer has its own title size, so it got the same rule.
- **Shipped:** the rule, the flyer rule, and a before and after screenshot (`docs/design/screenshots/2026-11-14-big-text-titles-320.png`). Cost: $0.
- **Measured:** with hyphenation turned off (the worst case, a browser with no English hyphenation), words split across lines at 320px and 200% text fell from 293 to 83 in titles, 223 to 11 in h2 and 393 to 222 in h3. Where the browser can hyphenate (Chrome, Safari, Firefox), the rest break at a syllable with a hyphen. No page scrolls sideways. Checked at 320px and 390px, light and dark.
