# Borrow it or get it free

**Issue:** #429 (part of #422)

A page in the Get through this month door, `/borrow-or-get-free/`, built from `content/secondhand/`:

- **Borrow:** the City of Morgantown's MOTO Toolbox tool library and the Morgantown Public Library's Library of Things.
- **Get it free:** the Christian Help Free Store and Career Closet, the WVU Homestart Closet, the Statler and Davis College career closets, Little Free Libraries, and Morgantown Freecycle.
- **Get it fixed:** the Scotts Run Resonance Project's Repair Cafe, with its next date from the calendar once one is posted.

Each card says who can use it, what to bring, and when it was checked. Once this page exists, the sell or donate and thrift pages link their free program names to its cards (until then they linked to each program's own site).

The page is in the Get through this month door, as #429 asks, so it waits for David.
