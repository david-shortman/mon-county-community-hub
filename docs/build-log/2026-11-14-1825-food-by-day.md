# Free food and meals by day, with open today first (#60)

`/help/food/by-day/` is the first Day list from `docs/design/presentation.md`. It lists free food pantries and meals for each day of the week. On a phone with scripts on, "Open today" sits at the top, worked out from the phone's clock in Morgantown time. With scripts off, the day links jump to each day. A full card for each place follows, with its source and checked date.

What is on it, each read on the group's own page on November 14, 2026:

- Christian Help Food Pantry (new record, `content/resources/christian-help-food-pantry.yaml`): Monday, Tuesday and Thursday 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., Friday 9 a.m. to 2 p.m., one order every 30 days per household.
- Community Kitchen weekday lunch: start time only, because its two pages give different end times.
- Senior Monongalians lunch for people 60 and older, Monday to Friday at the Mountaineer Mall.
- Salvation Army Sunday family dinner, 6 p.m.
- The Rack, for WVU students (checked October 31; WVU blocks automated reads, so a person re-reads it).
- Scott's Run Settlement House, under "By appointment: call first", because its pages give different pantry days.
- One-off food dates from the calendar (like mobile pantries) in the next 7 days show on their day.

Left off on purpose: pantries whose days or hours disagree between sources or have no current official page (#932, #1413), including St. Ursula, Starting Points, the Salvation Army pantry and the small church pantries.

**Re-checked monthly.** `content/checklists/food-days.yaml` adds a monthly checklist that lists every entry, oldest check first (the `food-days` list in `site/scripts/checklists.mjs`). An entry not re-read in 45 days says "Call before you go" on the page by itself. The daily food watch (`content/watch/food.yaml`) still flags page changes in between.
