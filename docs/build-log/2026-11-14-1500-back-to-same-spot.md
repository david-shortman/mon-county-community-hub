# 2026-11-14: Back to the same spot on list pages (#1369)

- **Considered:** David found that going back to Topics on his phone landed at the top. Topics is plain HTML with no script that scrolls, re-sorts or folds anything, and no client router. In Chromium (Pixel 7 size, with and without the service worker) Back already returns to the same spot, on Topics, Home and Dates. There is no WebKit browser in the build sessions, so the iPhone Safari case could not be reproduced. Likely causes there are the page-to-page fade (cross-document view transitions) or the page being reloaded instead of kept in the back/forward cache.
- **Chose:** a small fallback on every page, in `site/src/components/AppShell.astro`, instead of guessing at one cause. While you scroll, the page keeps its spot in its own history entry (`history.replaceState`, not storage, nothing sent anywhere). On Back, if the browser opened the page at the very top, it scrolls to the saved spot. If the browser restores the spot itself, nothing happens. Links with a `#` and pages that were at the top are left alone.
- **Checked:** Playwright at Pixel 7 size, with the browser's own restore turned off and the back/forward cache blocked to copy the failure. Without the fallback, Back on Topics landed at 0 after scrolling to 2000. With it, Back landed at 2000. Normal browsers still land at 2000. e2e and check-a11y pass.
- **Left for a person:** confirm on a real iPhone (Safari) and Android phone (Chrome). If Safari still jumps to the top, the next thing to try is skipping the fade on Back.
