## Why some events are outside the county

The section plan (#422, #426) includes a "Worth the drive" list, because Mon County has no regular flea market and the nearest big sales are in Fairmont, Preston County and southwestern Pennsylvania. Those events are tagged the same way, and each title names its town so no one mistakes it for a Morgantown event. Christmas at the Barn publishes no street address; it asks visitors to follow the directions on its website, and the card says so.
