# Free help filing your taxes (#987, #209)

**What:** A new page, `/free-tax-help/` (Get through this month door), for working households before tax season:

- Volunteers who do your taxes free: Christian Help (existing record, refreshed), IRS VITA and TCE (new), AARP Foundation Tax-Aide (new).
- Filing your own return free: IRS Free File (new) and the West Virginia Tax Division's Free File Options list for state returns (new).
- Credits to check for: the federal Earned Income Tax Credit (new) and the West Virginia Family Tax Credit (new).
- New orgs: `internal-revenue-service`, `wv-tax-division`, `aarp-foundation`. A line in `llms.txt`.

**#209, Christian Help 2027 dates:** not posted. As of October 31, 2026, Christian Help's own programs page does not list tax help, and the only source is the Morgantown Magazine article from January 14, 2026. The record now says that, with the date, and says to check again in early January. It also gains a "What happens next" box (what to bring, the two visits) from the same article.

**Why:** Wages that cover the basics is need 10 in the needs register (#984). The site had one tax help listing and nothing on the EITC.

**No guessed dates.** Every 2027 date is left off. Where a program says when to check back (IRS Free File: "check back January 2027"; the IRS VITA locator: "updated regularly from February through April"; AARP: back "in time for the next tax season"), the card quotes it.

**Left off on purpose:**
- IRS Direct File. Its old IRS page now returns "Page not found", and no current IRS page says whether it will run in 2027. Not mentioned on the site.
- EITC dollar amounts. The IRS tables page only goes up to tax year 2025, which was last season.
- The names and income limits of each Free File company on the state's list. They change each year, and listing them would favor businesses.
