# 2026-11-01: "Going through a big change?" life event pages, part 1

- **Changed:** a new `/life-events/` index lists starting pages for life events, the way `/for/` lists pages by group. It shows every page under `/life-events/` from its tags, plus the life events that already had a page (new baby, a death, a move, a storm, caring for a parent, turning 65, retiring, starting college). Three new pages list steps in order (today or first, this week, the next few weeks) and "if this also fits" links: I lost my job, My child has a delay or disability, and I'm new to the country. `/for/` and `llms.txt` link to them. Tracked in #1178, part of #529.
- **Why:** people often search by what just happened to them, not by program name. The steps only link to our existing pages, so the pages state no facts of their own; every phone, rule and date stays on the linked page with its source.
- **Gaps:** the "File for unemployment" step shows once #790 merges, and "After you file" once #1161 merges. Other missing steps get their own `for:code` issues from #1178.
- **Cost:** $0.
