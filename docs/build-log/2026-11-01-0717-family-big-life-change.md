# 2026-11-01: "Big life change?" links near the top of Family and life changes

- **Considered:** #1287 (sprint wave 9, phone design, #529). In the November 1 simulated walkthroughs (made-up tasks, not resident feedback), the six life event pages sat 4 to 9 phone screens down `/family/`, in the "Every Family and life changes page" list. #1279 tried adding them to the picker, which made the picker longer and pushed other links down, so it was closed. The issue offered three options: a short line near the top, listing the pages first in the long list, or a home page row.
- **Chose:** a short "Big life change?" row right under the lede on `/family/`, with one link to each life event page in the Family door and "See all big changes" (`/life-events/`). The links come from the pages' own tags, so a new life event page shows up by itself. The door layout (`DoorPage.astro`) gained a `top` slot for it; no other door uses it yet.
- **Measured:** with `walk.js` at 390 by 844, all six life event tasks (n05, n06, n08, n09, n10, n11) now find their link on the first screen of `/family/` (screen 0). The row is about 290 px tall, so links lower on `/family/` move down about a third of a screen: w20 (caregivers) went from screen 2 to 3 on that step. w08, w13, w23 and w25 did not get worse. Checked at 360 px and at 200% text: no sideways scroll, and `check-a11y` on `/family/` and `/life-events/` found no problems.
