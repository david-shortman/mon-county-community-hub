# 2026-10-31: Monongalia County Schools 2026-27 dates on the calendar

- **Considered:** parents ask "when are the days off and early dismissals?" (#272). The county posts its 2026-27 At-A-Glance calendar as an image-only PDF linked from an April 10, 2026 news post (#616).
- **Chose:** one Dates entry per remaining no-school day or break, per two-hour early dismissal, and the last day, so parents who subscribe to the calendar feed get each one. Days already past are left out.
- **Checked:** the PDF (updated July 7, 2026, WVDE approved June 24, 2026) was read as an image on October 30, 2026. Days the calendar marks with a star say they can become make-up school days under state law (W. Va. Code 18-5-45), as the calendar's footnote says. Early dismissals and the last day carry the star too.
- **Shipped:** 10 no-school entries (Nov 3 to May 31), 7 early dismissals (Nov 20 to Apr 30) and the last day (May 28).
