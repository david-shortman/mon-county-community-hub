# Foster or volunteer with Animal Friends

**What:** A record for Animal Friends of North Central West Virginia's volunteer and foster jobs, shown in a new "Help animals" section on `/pets/` and with the year-round groups on `/volunteer/`. Closes #1087, from #411 (idea 3, score 12), part of #398.

**Why:** The pets page covered adopting, lost pets and spay and neuter help, but not how to help the animals waiting for homes. People who can't adopt can still foster or give time.

**Choices:**
- The jobs, the age rule (18 and older) and the volunteer phone line (leave a message) all come from Animal Friends' How You Can Help page. The card links that page for the email address instead of copying it.
- M-SNAP is all volunteer, but its site has no volunteer sign-up, so it is not listed as a place to volunteer.
- The county shelter's volunteer program and the Humane Society are not included yet: we did not read their pages for this PR (the Humane Society's site blocked us when the pets page was built).

**Source (read 2026-10-31):** https://www.animalfriendswv.org/how-you-can-help/

**Cost:** $0.
