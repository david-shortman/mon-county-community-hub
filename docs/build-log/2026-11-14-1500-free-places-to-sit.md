# Free places to sit: three county parks and a research write-up

**What changed.** Find your people's Free places to sit list adds Mason-Dixon Historical Park (Core), Camp Muffly Park (south of Morgantown) and Chestnut Ridge Park (near Bruceton Mills). Each county FAQ says the park is open to the public, 8 a.m. to dusk, with no reservation needed. `check-places` now lets a park building that sets `drop_in` go by a dawn-to-dusk note, the same as a park. The research is in `docs/research/free-places-to-sit.md`.

**Why.** #1439: the list had only libraries, one BOPARC center and one city park. The west, south and east of the county had no outdoor place listed.

**What we left off.** Westside Senior Center (needs a $10 membership), Senior Monongalians meal sites (no drop-in stated), BOPARC parks (no hours stated, see the discovery), Mylan Park (no public-entry statement), and the Mountainlair (its site refuses automated reads).
