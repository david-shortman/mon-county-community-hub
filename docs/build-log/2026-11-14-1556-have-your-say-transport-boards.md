# 2026-11-14: Have your say adds the transportation planning board and Mountain Line (#288, #411)

- **Considered:** `/have-your-say/` covered five bodies. Two more public bodies that decide road, bus, walking and biking spending publish their public comment rules but were only on `/who-decides/`, with no "how to comment" line: the Morgantown Monongalia MPO Policy Board and the Mountain Line Transit Authority board. Residents of the smaller towns had no pointer to their own council's meeting times.
- **Chose:** two cards on `/have-your-say/`, each fact a record in `content/facts/meetings/` with a quote and a source read 2026-11-14: the MPO's comment period (4 minutes, in person, phone, video or written; 45-day comment periods on major plans) and Mountain Line's rules (30 minutes, 5 per person). Where official pages disagree on Mountain Line's meeting day, the card shows both and says to call first. A line points to the town pages for town councils.
- **Discoveries:** `docs/discoveries/2026-11-14-mountain-line-board-meeting-time.md` and `docs/discoveries/2026-11-14-mpo-policy-board-place.md`.
