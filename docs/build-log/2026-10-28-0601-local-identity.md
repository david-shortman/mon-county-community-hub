# 2026-10-28: Local identity, first pass

- **Considered:** cues from the place, not its institutions: the hills around town, the Monongahela River and Deckers Creek, downtown and Wharf District brick, the rail-trails, the glassmaking history in Sunnyside, the PRT, and the rhododendron. Ruled out anything from WVU, the city, the county, or the state (logos, seals, gold and blue), plus coal and mining images, orange creek water, and Appalachian stereotypes.
- **Chose:** three quiet cues. A thin hand-drawn ridgeline of hills under the header, the existing hill green for links and dates, and a brick tone used only as thin edges (the "who to ask" box and the footer line). The page background moved a touch warmer, toward sandstone. Kept system fonts: no font download, no outside service. Layout, sizes, and hierarchy did not change.
- **Shipped:** [`docs/design/identity.md`](../design/identity.md) (sourced guide, palette tokens with measured contrast in light and dark, motif rules, voice notes, an "is it too much?" check), `RidgeLine.astro`, new tokens in `Base.astro`, `npm run check-contrast` (runs after every build and fails it if a text pair drops below 4.5:1), and a new criterion VD-local in the visual design rubric. Before and after screenshots are in [`docs/design/screenshots/`](../design/screenshots/). Part of #123.
- **Checks:** axe-core found zero violations on all four pages at 390px and 1280px in light and dark mode. Every text pair passes WCAG 2.2 AA (lowest is 5.94:1). No sideways scrolling.
- **Next:** a monthly identity refresh against this guide, and a `for:david` issue to ask a few local groups whether it feels like Morgantown.
