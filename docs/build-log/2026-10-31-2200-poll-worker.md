# Work at the polls

**What:** A record for the Secretary of State's poll worker sign-up, shown in a new "Work at the polls" section on `/vote/` and in "Use a skill you have" on `/volunteer/`. Closes #1086, from #411 (idea 4, score 13), part of #398.

**Why:** Running elections takes many poll workers, and the vote page only covered registering and finding your polling place.

**Choices:**
- Nonpartisan and official only: the link goes to the state's own sign-up form, and the rules are the ones on that form (registered in your county or an eligible high school junior or senior, read and write English, go to training, work all of Election Day, not a candidate or a candidate's close family).
- We say the county clerk contacts you only if your county needs more workers, because the form says not every county needs more.
- Left off: pay (each county commission sets it, and we found no official Mon County figure) and the 2027 Morgantown municipal election dates, which go on `/calendar/` once posted.
- The sign-up form asks for personal details. We only link to it; this site collects nothing.
- The poll worker address in #411 (sos.wv.gov/elections/Pages/PollWorkers.aspx) now returns "not found". The sign-up is linked from the GoVoteWV page instead.

**Sources (read 2026-10-31):** https://apps.sos.wv.gov/elections/PollWorkers/Register and https://sos.wv.gov/govotewv

**Cost:** $0.
