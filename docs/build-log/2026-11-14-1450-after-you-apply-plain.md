# After you apply: a plain sentence before each long quote

**What changed.** On eight After you apply pages (child care, homestead, LIEAP, Medicare Savings, SNAP, SSI, WV WORKS), 22 bullets that opened with a long quote from a manual, federal rule or state law now open with one short plain sentence, key number first. The exact quote stays right after it, word for word, so nothing about a deadline, a cost or who decides is lost.

Example (SSI): "60 days. You have 60 days from the day you get the letter to ask SSA, in writing, to look at it again. SSA will reconsider if you send "a written request ... within 60 days after the date you receive notice of the initial determination.""

**How we kept it exact.** Each new sentence restates only what its quote says, with the same number and the same condition. Where a quote counts days from something unusual (Extra Help's 31 days start when the state gets Social Security's file, not when the form reaches you), the plain sentence says so.

**Not changed.** No quote, number, link or source. The housing voucher page is in open PR #1750 and was left alone. The notes that name "the manual" are #1368 (PR #1820).

**Why.** #1230: the reviews of these pages all asked for this.
