# 2026-10-31: Phone-only cards point to the free 7-1-1 relay (#835)

- **Considered:** most listing cards end in "Call". 4.0% of residents have a hearing disability, and hearing is the second most common disability among people 65 and older (`docs/playbook/audience-facts.md`). The issue asked for a no-voice option, shown only when a source confirms it.
- **Chose:** one short line on resource cards that have a phone number but no text line: dial 7-1-1 for the free relay service. The fact is national, so it comes from one official page (ADA.gov, "The free nationwide telecommunications relay service (TRS), reached by calling 7-1-1"), cited on the line itself. The FCC and WV Relay pages could not be opened by our tools today.
- **Shipped:** `site/src/components/RelayLine.astro`, used in `ResourceCard.astro`. Cards with a text line (211, 988, the Veterans Crisis Line) and the 911, 988 and 211 numbers themselves skip it.
- **Not yet:** provider-specific text or online options per card; those need each provider's own page.
- **Waits for David:** it changes every resource card, including ones on food, housing and crisis pages.
