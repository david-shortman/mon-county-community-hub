# 2026-11-01: Help for parents of kids with disabilities

- **Changed:** a new page, `/kids-with-disabilities/`, with seven new listings: West Virginia Parent Training and Information, the state's four ways to settle a special education disagreement, SSI for children, Medicaid for a child with a severe disability (CDCSP), Children with Special Health Care Needs care coordination, respite and other help while waiting for the I/DD Waiver, and WVU CED parent mentors, Parent to Parent and Sibshops. Two new org records (WVDE, WVPTI). The child delay or disability life event page and Disability and aging link to it, and `llms.txt` lists it. Tracked in #1183, part of #529.
- **Why:** parents often need help with the IEP, money for a child's care, and a break. The life event page had no place to send them.
- **Left off:** WVPTI's services and address, the local Social Security office, the CDCSP age limit and the CSHCN toll-free number (#1225).
- **Cost:** $0.
