# "Your area" picker, with no tracking

**Issue:** #152 (part of #172)

A small "Your area" menu on the thrift and resale shops page. A person picks a part of the county (Westover, downtown, Mileground, University Ave or elsewhere) and that area's shops move to the top. The choice is saved on the phone only, in localStorage, and can be changed or cleared from the same menu ("Show all in the usual order"). The site never sees it: no location, no cookies, nothing sent.

With no script, or nothing picked, the menu does not show and the page lists every area in its usual order.

The picker is a reusable component (`AreaPicker.astro`): any page that groups places into sections can add `data-area` to each section. Other pages can use it as more records name their town (#395).
