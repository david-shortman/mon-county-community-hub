# 2026-10-31: A before-and-after benchmark: our pages against the guide, the city page and official pages

- **Considered:** the findability score depends on search engines indexing us, which takes weeks. David asked for a steady way to show whether the site is better than information stuck in PDFs, slow pages and lists of phone numbers (#183, #184).
- **Chose:** 8 urgent needs (findability ids q01 to q05, q08, q25, q26), 4 sources each: our page, the 2025 Monongalia County Resource Guide (PDF), the city's Community Resources page and its PATHWAYS to Help link, and the best official page from the findability run. A script takes the automatic measures; an agent scores an 8-point answer card, "must call to learn", and two facts per need against the provider's page. The "dramatically better" bar was written down before the run. The weekly Sunday findability check runs it from the scoreboard (#185): our pages weekly, outside sources monthly or when the PDF's hash changes.
- **Shipped:** `content/findability/benchmark/` (tasks, `measure.js`, `score.py`, the first run) and a "Source benchmark" section in [the scoreboard](../scoreboard.md#source-benchmark) with the method, the bar, the run steps and the results. The PDF itself is not stored, only its size, date and hash.
- **Result:** our pages beat every source on 7 of 8 needs and meet the full bar on 1 of 8 (where to stay tonight). Gaps: food this week has no pantry list with days and hours, a doctor without insurance has no page of its own (ties the city's page), eviction help is two taps in, and water shutoff help still needs a call. Three WVU pages refused our tools after the automatic check, so they were left out, not guessed. Comparing formats, not organizations: these sources are where much of our information comes from.
- **Scoreboard:** Level 1, new "Source benchmark" table. Check 2026-11-01 (first Sunday run).
- **Cost:** $0. Playwright and poppler are already in the environment.
