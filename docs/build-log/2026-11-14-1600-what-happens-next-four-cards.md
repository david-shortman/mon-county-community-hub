# What happens next on four more high-stakes cards

**What changed.** From each card's own source, re-read 2026-11-14:

- Legal Aid eviction help: `what_to_bring` (legal and money papers, pen and paper) and `what_happens` (the intake call asks about income, takes at least 15 to 20 minutes), from Legal Aid WV's Application Tips page.
- Free tax help (VITA and TCE): `what_to_bring`, from the IRS checklist for free tax return preparation.
- Summer EBT: `what_happens` (who is signed up without applying, the 15 business day decision, the 15 day deadline to send proof).
- Section 8 and public housing: `what_happens` (letter, group orientation, 60 to 120 days to use a voucher, help starts after inspection).

**Why.** #716 and #159: tell people what to expect before they act. These are housing, money and food cards, so the PR waits for David.

**Checked and left alone.** Grace Shelter and Catholic Charities emergency help (their pages do not say what to bring or what happens), Scott's Run baby pantry (by appointment, nothing on what to bring), AARP Tax-Aide (its locator is closed until next season), free credit reports (nothing on what happens after a request). The Child Care Resource Center site describes an office process, but it was not clear that it covers the Morgantown office, so we left it off.
