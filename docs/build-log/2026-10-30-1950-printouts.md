# 2026-10-30: Printouts made from the data, and cards with QR codes

- **Considered:** #405 asks for one-page sheets rebuilt from the data, and #160 for wallet cards and posters with a QR code, a short web address and 211. About 7% of households here have no internet, and many people reach help only through a helper.
- **Chose:** sheets by door and by big topic (4 or more listings), not by town: our records do not say which towns each place serves yet, so town sheets (Westover, Star City, Cheat Lake) would be guesses. Cards for the three pages people most need from paper: Where to stay tonight, Food help, Help paying a utility bill.
- **Shipped:** `/print/` (list), `/print/<door or topic>/` (sheets) and `/print/cards/` (wallet cards and half-page posters). Sheets list each record's phone, text line, address, hours, who it is for, source and checked date, plus "Made from our listings on" the build date, a note on any line not checked in over 6 months, and how to reach 211. QR codes are made at build time as inline SVG with the free, MIT-licensed `qrcode` package, so pages load no script or outside service. The short address is printed beside every code. Card words come from each page's own label and summary, so a card never says more than the page does.
- **Cost:** free. Printing and placing printouts stays with David (#163).
- **Waits for David:** the sheets and cards name 211 and list shelter, food and bill help, so the High stakes check holds this PR.
