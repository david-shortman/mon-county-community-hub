# 2026-10-31: Courts and legal help page

- **Considered:** the life areas coverage map (#222) ranked legal help that is not eviction as the biggest missing area. Legal Services Corporation's 2022 Justice Gap study found 74% of low-income U.S. households had a civil legal problem in the past year. The site had only eviction help, court interpreters and WVU Student Legal Services.
- **Chose:** `/legal-help/` in the Your community door (courts are city and county government), with 12 new records and 4 new orgs (Circuit Clerk, Magistrate Court, the West Virginia State Bar, ABA Free Legal Answers). Free help first: Legal Aid of WV, Free Legal Answers, the Tuesday night lawyer line, lawyer referral and Mountain State Justice. Then filing a civil case up to $20,000 in Magistrate Court, fee waivers, expungement forms, name changes, jury duty, court forms and case search, and the state's notary search.
- **Checked:** the $20,000 limit on the courts' magistrate page and in W. Va. Code 50-2-1 (two older courts pages still say $10,000), the $40 a day juror pay on the Circuit Clerk's site and the courts' jury page, and the State Bar's status as an agency of the state Supreme Court on its own page.
- **Left off:** the Magistrate Court filing fee (the statute's lines do not say how they add up), expungement fees and waiting periods (Legal Aid's page is from 2024 and the law has changed), the name change form and fee, the Magistrate Clerk's hours, WVU Law clinics (403), and a notary at the library or County Clerk (neither says so). A needs-verification issue lists each. The ABA Free Legal Answers "Other Help" page links a West Virginia Senior Legal Aid domain that now goes to a gambling site, so we do not link it.
- **Cost:** $0.
