# Start a swap, market or reuse store (draft for David)

**Issue:** #431 (part of #422)

A page in the Your community door, `/start-something/`, on what Mon County lacks for secondhand shopping and sharing: a regular flea market or swap meet, a building materials reuse store, a Buy Nothing group, and clothing, toy and kids' gear swaps. For each it says what we found and how we looked, what it would take, and a free or low cost guide where one exists. It ends with models already working here (the Scotts Run Repair Cafe and the county's Community Seed Library).

It suggests and never organizes. Nobody was contacted. Costs come from each guide's own page: the Buy Nothing license is $10, and the Repair Café starter kit is a voluntary one-time 49 euros.

The seed library is not listed as a gap, because lane 7 found the county's Community Seed Library at the Mon County Center.

The issue asks for a draft for David's look, so the PR waits for him.
