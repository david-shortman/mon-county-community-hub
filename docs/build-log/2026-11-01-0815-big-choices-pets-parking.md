# 2026-11-01: Pets and Parking lead with big choices

- **Considered:** #199 (sprint wave 9, phone design, #529): each guide page should lead with a block that does its one job, not a wall of text. Bill help waits for David, and Recycling and Dates already have lead blocks. Pets and Parking are everyday pages where most people come for one of four things, but the first phone screen was a paragraph and a list of links.
- **Chose:** a small `BigChoices` block: four big buttons under the lede that jump to the right section. Pets: found a dog, lost my pet, bitten or scratched, adopt. Parking: where to park, pay a meter, I got a ticket, towed or blocked in. They are plain anchor links, so they work with no script. Pets keeps "On this page" for its other sections. Added to the Building blocks table and the site map in `docs/design/presentation.md`. No new facts.
- **Checked:** 320 px and 430 px, and 200% text: no sideways scroll. Before and after screenshots are in the PR.
- **Cost:** $0.
