# 2026-10-31: Remove the stray "0" and put back the missing space on resource cards

- **Found:** while checking a new page at phone width, resource cards showed a lone "0" under their buttons, and the "who it is for" and "what to do" sentences ran together ("proof of age.Get the form"). On the built site, 51 of 53 resource cards had the "0" and 47 had the glued sentences.
- **Cause:** `WhatHappensNext` showed its box when `has` was truthy, and `has` ended in `helpers.length`, so a card with nothing to show rendered the number 0. Astro drops a plain space between two `{...}` expressions, the same bug #695 fixed on place cards and source lines.
- **Fixed:** `has` is now a true or false value. `ResourceCard` and `EventCard` use `{' '}` between the two sentences. After the fix, 0 cards have either problem.
- **Cost:** $0.
