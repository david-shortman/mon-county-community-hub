# 2026-10-31: Arts Center, Farmers Market and Mylan Park on Dates (#761)

- **Considered:** three local event sources publish working feeds (#218 research): the Monongalia Arts Center, the Morgantown Farmers Market and Mylan Park. Their events were not on Dates.
- **Chose:** one adapter per feed, the same pattern as the city and library feeds. Repeating events show only the date the feed gives, with a note; we never expand repeat rules. MAC cards send people to the center for cost, since many classes are paid. Mylan Park cards say the event is listed there and point to the event page for tickets.
- **Shipped:** three adapters and snapshots, rows in the feeds table, and a small guard so a feed item a hand-checked entry already covers (same first three title words, inside its dates) does not show twice.
- **Left off:** later dates of repeating markets and classes (the feed states a repeat rule, and we do not expand those).
