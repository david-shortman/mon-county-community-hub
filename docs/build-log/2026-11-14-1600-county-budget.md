# 2026-11-14: Mon County's 2026-2027 budget, as a phone page with one chart

- **Considered:** PDFs our pages already cite with no phone page yet (part of #393, sprint wave 16, #529). The County Commission's 2026-2027 levy estimate (budget) is a four-page scan with long columns of numbers. It answers a common question, "where does the county's money go?", but is hard to read on a phone. The epic names the county budget as a fit for "one plain chart per question."
- **Chose:** `/county-budget/`, from `content/pdf-pages/mon-county-budget-2026-27.yaml`. Every revenue and spending line in both funds is copied as printed. Each section's lines were added up and match the PDF's own section totals, so no number was misread. The chart is a plain HTML bar list of General Fund spending by area, built from the PDF's section totals in the record; the build fails if those totals do not add up to the PDF's grand total. The share of the total is our math, and the page says so. Linked from `/make-it-better/when-it-gets-decided/`.
- **Checked:** 320px with 200% text and 390px, light and dark, no sideways scroll.
- **Cost:** $0.
