# 2026-10-30: Make a list for someone

- **Considered:** #404. Many people with low literacy or no internet reach help only through a helper: a librarian, a pantry or church volunteer, a nurse, a neighbor. Precedents: SF Service Guide's curated guides and One Degree's referral tool.
- **Chose:** one page, `/make-a-list/`. The helper ticks up to 6 places and can add a short note. The list shows first, with Print, Text and Share. Picks and note live only in the web address, so nothing is saved or sent. A box warns not to type anything personal into the note. Without scripts the page shows every place, ready to print.
- **Shipped:** the page, built from resource records (phone, text line, address, hours, who it is for, source, checked date, and a flag past 6 months). A line in `llms.txt`.
- **Not yet:** the helper role cards ("If someone tells you X, do these 3 things") need content choices per role, so they stay open on #404.
