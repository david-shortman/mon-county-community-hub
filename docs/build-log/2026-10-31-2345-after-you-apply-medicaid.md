# 2026-10-31: After you apply for Medicaid or WVCHIP

- **Changed:** new page `/after-you-apply/medicaid/`: no interview, at least 10 days to answer a DFA-6, decisions in 13 business days for children and 30 days for adults, backdating up to 3 months (not for WVCHIP), the Medical ID card in the approval letter, letters, the 90 day fair hearing deadline and keeping coverage while you appeal, reporting changes and 12 months of continuous coverage for children, and automatic renewals with a 90 day window after closure. Part of #1148.
- **Why:** `/file-today/` covers applying and `/medicaid-2027/` covers the 2027 rules, but nothing covered the steps in between.
- **Sources:** WV DoHS Income Maintenance Manual (effective October 5, 2026) chapters 1, 9, 10 and 22, form DFA-FH-1 printed in it, and form DFA-SLA-1. Read October 31, 2026.
- **Cost:** $0.
