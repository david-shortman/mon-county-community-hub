# 2026-10-30: Before you call

- **Considered:** #406. Phone-only services are the hardest step for many people. In mRelief testing, people rated "documents I need" and "how to apply" as most useful.
- **Chose:** one page, `/before-you-call/`: a short checklist for any call, a printable place to write what the office said (with a reference number), and three call scripts with blanks: a utility bill, late rent with a landlord, and a hospital or clinic bill. The scripts are questions to ask and state no facts about any program. "What to have ready" for a named office comes only from records with `what_to_bring` (#159), so it carries a source and checked date and grows as Duet fills those fields (#716).
- **Waits for David:** it is a money page in the Get through this month door, and the scripts are new wording residents will read aloud.
