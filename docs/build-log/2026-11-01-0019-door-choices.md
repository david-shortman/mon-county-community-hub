# Three door choices residents looked for

**What.** The Family and life changes door gets two new choices: "I'm caring for a family member who is older or has a disability" (opens Caring for a family member) and "Someone in my family died" (opens When someone dies). The Living here door gets "I have a question about my property tax" (opens Property tax).

**Why.** In 30 simulated resident walkthroughs on October 31, 2026 (made-up tasks, not real residents), these three pages were only reachable by scrolling 11 to 17 screens down a door page to its full topic list. With the choices, each is a tick and one tap from the door, and about two screens of scrolling. No new facts. See `docs/reviews/2026-10-31-simulated-walkthroughs.md`. Part of #529.
