- **Considered:** the next missing life areas after the coverage map's ranked list was built (#222). Planning medical care ahead had no page: nothing on the site named a living will, a medical power of attorney, the POST form or the e-Directive Registry. It is not high stakes (topic `health`, no crisis line).
- **Chose:** `/advance-directives/` in the Get care door, with a new org record for the WV Center for End-of-Life Care (a state center on the WVU Health Sciences campus in Morgantown) and three resources: the free forms and how to sign them, the WV e-Directive Registry, and the POST form and DNR card. Rules are quoted from the Center's pages and its 2022 combined form PDF (two witness lines and a notary). It reuses the existing notary search record. The notary fee cap ($10.00 for each signature) was checked against the current text of WV Code 39-4-30 on October 31, 2026.
