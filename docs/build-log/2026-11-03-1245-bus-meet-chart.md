# 2026-11-03: Bus routes: a "where routes meet" chart instead of the network drawing (#1656)

- **Why:** feedback that the network drawing on /bus-routes/ was crowded and told no useful story. On a phone, 20 routes drawn as straight lines between 24 dots crossed everywhere, and only 7 dots had names.
- **Chose:** a plain table. One row per route, one column per stop on 5 or more routes (Pifer Terminal, Downtown, Towers, Colasante's Shelter, Mountainlair, Public Safety Building), a dot where the route stops. Rows are grouped by the first busy stop they reach (11 through Pifer, 4 campus routes through Towers, 5 others with their meeting stops listed). Three takeaways above it, all counted from `content/pdf-pages/mountain-line-routes.yaml`.
- **Dropped:** the scroll story, the moving bus and the route picker. A still chart says this faster (motion guide rule: if a still picture says it, use it). The full shared stop list and every route's meeting points stay, now inside "show more" sections.
- **Cost:** none.
