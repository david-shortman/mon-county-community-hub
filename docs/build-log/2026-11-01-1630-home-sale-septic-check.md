# 2026-11-01: Septic and well check when a home is sold, as a phone page

- **Considered:** PDFs linked from our pages or from official local pages that have no phone page yet (part of #393, sprint wave 8, #529). The health department's six-page home loan evaluation packet is the only place its rules for this check are written down, and buyers and sellers of homes outside public sewer and water need it.
- **Chose:** `/home-sale-septic-check/`, from `content/pdf-pages/monchd-home-loan-evaluation.yaml`. It quotes what the check covers, the fee ($225, $325 with radon, matching the 2026-2027 fee schedule), how to ask, how long it takes, and the well, septic and home aeration unit rules. Staff names and a staff email in the PDF are left out. Linked from `/septic-and-wells/`.
- **Found:** the department's Septic and Wells page links both this packet (memo dated October 1, 2025) and an older 2016 copy. Logged in `docs/discoveries/2026-11-01-monchd-two-home-loan-packets.md`.
- **Cost:** $0 (pdftotext, open source).
