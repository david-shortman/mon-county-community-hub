# Town pages: one page per town and census place

**Date:** 2026-10-31. **Who:** Claude Code. **Issue:** #1120 (sprint lane, see #529).

## What was built

- `/towns/`: an index of 11 pages, one for each place on the Census Bureau's list of places in Monongalia County. Five are towns and cities with their own government (Morgantown, Westover, Star City, Granville, Blacksville). Six are census-designated places with no town government (Booth, Brookhaven, Cassville, Cheat Lake, Osage, Pentress).
- `/towns/<slug>/`: each page shows who to call (police, water and sewer, trash), the town office, who decides and where they meet, places to gather, dates that name the town, and Mountain Line routes with a stop named for the place.
- `site/src/lib/town-hubs.mjs` holds the list and pulls almost everything from records we already have, so each fact keeps its own source. The Who decides bodies and the meetings page's town list moved into `site/src/lib/who-decides.mjs` and `site/src/lib/meetings/others.mjs`, so both pages and the town pages share one copy.
- New sourced facts, all checked 2026-10-31: Westover City Council's make-up and meeting days, and the Westover, Star City and Granville town offices, each from the town's own site. A calendar entry for the Star City Town Council meeting on November 17, from the town's home page.
- The area picker (#875) can link to a town page. The Thrift shops page uses it for Westover and Cheat Lake. Moving here links to `/towns/`. Every town page is in `llms.txt`.

## Why

People often know their town before they know which office does what. A page per town puts the answers they need in one place, and makes clear that a census place like Cheat Lake has no town government, so the county serves it.

## Left off on purpose

- Sabraton is not on the Census list, so it has no page. The index sends people outside any town to Who serves my address.
- No town is placed from a mailing address. Places to gather and dates show on a town page only when their own record names that town.
- Water and trash outside the towns, and water and police in Blacksville, are still unconfirmed (#524, #674). The pages say so and name the official place to ask.
- No pantry list by town: no pantry record names a town yet. Pages link to the existing help pages instead.
