# 2026-11-15: Your community door, a way in for helpers (#404, #529)

- **Considered:** the wave 20 persona walkthroughs on `main` (simulated, not real residents). The helper persona (a librarian, pastor or case worker who wants a list to print) scored 10 of 18. From the home page it took three taps through Your community and "Want to help? Where to start", a page about volunteering and giving, before reaching Make a list for someone.
- **Chose:** add Make a list for someone to the Your community door twice: as a choice in "Pick what is going on", and as a link next to "Want to help? Where to start". The link alone saves a tap: home, Your community, Make a list.
- **Left out:** dates or phones on the Make a list page itself. It is a tool: each place shows its phone, source and checked date once picked. The walk script cannot see those, so the page keeps low source and next step scores by design.
- **Measured:** simulated walk, helper persona: 10 to 11 of 18 (3 taps to 2). Results in the wave 20 persona results file (`content/findability/walkthroughs/`). Cost: $0.
