# 2026-11-14: How to apply at the Child Care Resource Center (#571)

- **Considered:** #571 was left open for a call to CCRC, because its home page and families page disagreed on how intake works (#569: "walk-in only" on one, a phone intake on the other).
- **Checked, 2026-11-14:** CCRC's home page (ccrcwv.org) and families page (ccrcwv.org/families/) now agree on the basics:
  - No appointments; intakes are first come, first served.
  - About 45 minutes with a case manager (families page: 30 to 45; home page: no less than 45). The card gives both.
  - The last intake is no later than 4:15 p.m. (families page).
  - Proofs are due within 13 days of the intake, or the application is withdrawn and the family owes the child care program.
  - The families page also says you can call your local office to ask for a phone intake. The home page no longer says "walk-in only", so this is stated as the families page's own words.
  - The Morgantown office has a drop box; changes are reported within 5 days.
