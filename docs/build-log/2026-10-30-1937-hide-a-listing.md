# 2026-10-30: Hide one listing without deleting it

- **Considered:** the only way to take an item off the site was to delete its file, and deleting published content waits for David. The one-week fix and removal promise (#502) needs a fast, safe way to pull one item, keep its history, and put it back once it is checked (#519).
- **Chose:** a `hidden` block on any org, resource or calendar record (`reason`, `since`, optional `note`). The shared loaders leave hidden records out, so every list, count, search result and `calendar.ics` drops them with no page edits. A page that names a hidden record on purpose gets a stand-in with the name and no facts, and the cards say "We are re-checking this listing." 211 and 988 cannot be hidden, because every page shows them.
- **Tested:** hid every org, resource and calendar entry at once (except 211 and 988). The site built, no page showed a hidden phone, link or date, and a new page check would have failed the build on any "undefined" text or empty call link.
- **Shipped:** the `hidden` field and its checks, a Health check line listing hidden items, the High stakes check skipping PRs that only hide, and the "Hiding an item" section in `content/README.md`.
- **Scoreboard:** Trust TR8 (open and accountable: a way to take a wrong item down fast).
