# 2026-11-03: Bus routes: plan a trip with a simple map (#1656)

- **Why:** feedback that the "How the routes fit together" drawing was crowded and told no useful story, and that the bus page should help people plan how to get somewhere without a car. A simple map gives a sense of place that text and tables do not, with text as backup.
- **Built:** "Plan a trip by bus" at the top of /bus-routes/. Pick From, To and the day. The answer names the bus, where to get on, where to change, and each route's first and last run that day, with up to two changes. The map draws the trip: first bus in green, the next in rust, numbered where you get on. Still version: an example trip (Sabraton to Ruby Memorial) and a list of every place and the routes that stop there.
- **Grounded in:** each route's stops in order and its hours from `content/pdf-pages/mountain-line-routes.yaml`. A change is only planned at a stop both routes list by the same name. The words say buses do not wait for each other and point to the trip planner and 304-291-RIDE for stop times.
- **The map:** 35 places, each with a source in `content/bus-map/places.yaml`: Census TIGER/Line points (towns, neighborhoods, hospitals, malls, the airport) and official addresses placed with the Census Geocoder (Pifer Terminal, the Mountainlair, Towers, Mountaineer Station, Mylan Park). River and I-79/I-68 from TIGER/Line (`content/bus-map/base.json`, made by `site/scripts/make-bus-map-base.py`). No OpenStreetMap data (images guide). Directions are true but distance from downtown uses a square-root scale, so the middle has room and Wadestown still fits a phone. Names are placed so they do not cover dots or each other.
- **Found:** Mountain Line's "Westover Terminal" page is about Pifer Terminal, so the two names are now joined (Routes 6 and 52 start there too). Many stops have no public location; see `docs/discoveries/2026-11-03-bus-stops-without-places.md`.
- **Kept as backup:** the old network drawing became a "where routes meet" table (routes by the 6 busiest stops). Its scroll story was dropped.
- **Not yet:** real streets and times at each stop wait on a current schedule feed (#407, #1447).
- **Cost:** none.
