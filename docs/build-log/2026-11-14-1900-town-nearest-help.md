# 2026-11-14: Nearest help on four town pages (#1814)

- **Considered:** the research from #1416 (`docs/research/nearest-help-by-area.md`), which measured the nearest pantry, clinic, library and benefits office for Brookhaven, Star City, Booth and the west end. Facts held in #1813 stay off: library branches (mympls.org refuses our reads), list-only pantries, Covenant Church, RideMon hours, and buses to Osage and Van Voorhis Road.
- **Chose:** a "Nearest help" section on the Brookhaven, Star City, Booth and Blacksville town pages (Wadestown and Core have no town page). Three rows each: food pantry, clinic, and the state benefits office. Only providers whose own page gives the address and either hours or "by appointment": Christian Help, Scott's Run (Osage site), the health department clinic, the Clay-Battelle health center and the DoHS county office. Every page was re-read on 2026-11-14.
- **How distances work:** straight-line miles (haversine) from the town's Census 2024 Gazetteer point to the address as the Census Geocoder places it, rounded to the half mile under 2 miles and to the whole mile above. The page says driving is longer. The DoHS address gave two geocoder matches about 0.2 miles apart; a test checks every rounded distance is the same from either one.
- **How buses work:** the existing trip planner logic (`plan()` in `site/src/lib/bus-trips.mjs`) on Mountain Line's route pages, from the town's stops to the stop area of the address. A bus is named only when the stop area is within half a mile of the address; otherwise the row says we could not confirm a bus. Routes 6, 8, 13 and 52 were re-read on busride.org on 2026-11-14 and matched our record.
- **Shipped:** `content/towns/nearest-help.yaml`, `site/src/lib/nearest-help.mjs`, `site/src/components/NearestHelp.astro`, the section and jump link on the town page, and `site/test/nearest-help.test.mjs`. Food and benefits, so the PR waits for David. Cost: $0 (Census Geocoder and our own data).
