# 2026-10-30: A new icon: two towns and a bridge

- **Considered:** the old icon (two plain peaks) said "hills" but nothing about Morgantown. David asked for a subtle local twist. We sketched a river line, a PRT car, a coal seam, a map of the city, town lights, and a bridge, then refined the bridge over several rounds with David.
- **Chose:** Westover and Morgantown on two hills facing each other across the Monongahela, joined by an open arch bridge, with ridges behind. It is local without naming or favoring any one institution, and it matches the project's "connect, don't compete" belief. Choices from review: pale houses on sage hills (so they do not blend), a few uneven houses on flat hilltops (evenly spaced roofs looked like gear teeth from far away), rounded roofs and corners, the bridge drawn behind the hills, and a flat waterline.
