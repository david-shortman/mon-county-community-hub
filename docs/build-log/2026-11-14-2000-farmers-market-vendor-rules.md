# Farmers market vendor rules as a phone page (part of #393)

`/farmers-market-vendor-rules/` shows the parts of the Morgantown Farmers' Market Growers Association's 37-page 2026-2027 Market Rules and Guidelines that a new seller needs first: who can sell, the three markets, when to apply, every fee from Table 2 and section 10, and how to pay. Everything is quoted from the PDF (`content/pdf-pages/mfmga-market-rules-2026-27.yaml`). The rules give two end dates for the Saturday market; both are quoted and logged as a discovery.

Linked from `/sell-what-you-grow/` under the market's vendor card.
