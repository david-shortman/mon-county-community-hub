# Links residents looked for and could not find

**What.** Three links that simulated walkthroughs looked for and did not find: School-age kids links to Shots and physicals for school and sports. Selling food links to the bake sale rules. The Living here door has a new choice, "My landlord won't make repairs", that opens the repairs part of Renting a home.

**Why.** In 30 simulated resident walkthroughs on October 31, 2026 (made-up tasks, not real residents), "my kid needs shots before school" ended on School-age kids with no link to the shots page, and a renter whose landlord won't fix the heat had to scroll 16 screens on Get through this month to find Renting.
