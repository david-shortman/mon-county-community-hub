# 2026-11-01: After you apply for help with Medicare costs

- **Changed:** new page `/after-you-apply/medicare-savings/`: Medicare Savings Programs (QMB, SLIMB, QI-1) from DoHS, with the 30 day time limit, 10 days for papers, when each starts, the 90 day fair hearing deadline and yearly renewals; and Extra Help from Social Security, with the approval notice, the 60 day appeal deadline, the 10 days to keep Extra Help during an appeal, and redeterminations. Part of #1148 (sprint #529).
- **Why:** Turning 65 explains how to apply for help with Medicare costs, but not what happens next. The two programs are run by different offices with different appeal deadlines.
- **Sources:** WV DoHS Income Maintenance Manual (effective October 5, 2026), chapter 1 section 1.16, chapter 9 and form DFA-FH-1, chapter 10; 20 CFR part 418 subpart D (eCFR); Medicare.gov pages on drug costs and Medicare Savings Programs. Read November 1, 2026. ssa.gov refused automated reads, so SSA's processing times and status steps are left off.
- **Cost:** $0.
