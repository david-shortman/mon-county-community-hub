# 2026-11-04: Bus maps: a transit diagram on the page, planning inside the full map (#1723)

- **Why:** David found the new full street map much clearer and asked for the simple map to match, even if more abstract: straight lines with curved bends, not exact geography. He also found that "Start here" and "Go here" in the full map took him out of it.
- **Simple map, now a diagram:** each place has a hand-placed `grid` spot in `content/bus-map/places.yaml`, true in rough direction, not distance. Lines run only straight or at 45 degrees with rounded bends; for each link the code picks the bend that passes no other place (`site/src/lib/bus-diagram.mjs`). The river is a plain band with its name. Names are placed so they never cover a dot or another name, and keep off lines where they can, so most places now have a name. Very long links (a route's trip back across the county) are left out of the plain lines and drawn only when a trip uses them. The caption says it is a diagram and points to Expand map for real streets.
- **Full map, planning stays in it:** the panel under the map always shows something. With nothing tapped it is the trip: From, To, day buttons, one line per bus with that day's hours, Swap, and "See full steps", which closes the map and goes to the card. Start here and Go here update the trip, keep the map open, and fit the map to the trip.
- **Tests:** `site/test/bus-diagram.test.mjs` checks one grid spot per place, only straight or 45 degree links, no plain line through a place it does not stop at, and the in-map trip panel.
- **Cost:** none.
