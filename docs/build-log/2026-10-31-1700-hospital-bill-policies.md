# Hospital bill help: quote each policy's income limit, with its date (#985)

**What:** On `/no-insurance/` (added in #707), the two hospital bill records now quote each hospital's own published income limit, name the policy date, and show a "What happens next" box with what to bring and what the hospital does after you apply.

- `wvu-medicine-financial-assistance`: the 2026 Plain Language Summary's "at or below 200% of the Federal Poverty Level may qualify for free care", the 2026 dollar amounts for 1 to 4 people, and two other routes from the full policy (revised January 1, 2025): eligible for SNAP, WIC or energy assistance, or medical bills of 10% or more of income with income from 200% to 300% of the guidelines. What to bring comes from the application form. Main link now goes to the Policies and Financial Applications page.
- `mon-health-charity-care`: the posted policy FIN021 (effective January 29, 2021) "at or below 200% of the federal poverty level may still be eligible for a 100% discount", kept next to the page's own "case by case". What to bring comes from Mon Health's application letter.

**Why:** Paying for care is need 2 in the needs register (#984). #707 linked both programs but left out the numbers. #985 asked for the limits as written, with the policy date.

**Left off on purpose:**
- How long a WVU Medicine approval lasts. The 2026 summary says 12 months, the FAQ page says six months, and the full policy says 240 days before to 365 days after approval.
- Mon Health's dollar amounts. Its page links the 2020 poverty guidelines, which are out of date.

**Sources:** all read 2026-10-31 (links in the two records).
