# 2026-11-02: Your year at home and in the garden, a year strip from frost to frost (#1448)

- **Considered:** `/home-calendar/` lists each yearly home date as a card, but not the shape of the year: when the growing window opens and closes, and where the tax, fire season and upkeep dates fall around it.
- **Chose:** a month by month strip on the scroll story kit (#1441), in four steps: winter, the last spring frost, the growing season, and the first fall frost with the fall dates. As each step reaches the middle of the screen, its dates pop into their months and the growing season fills in month by month. Everything comes from `content/home-year.yaml` (`site/src/lib/year-strip.mjs`), so every date on the strip is also a card below with its source.
- **Accuracy:** the issue described October 11 as the average first fall frost. NOAA's normals for the Morgantown airport station give October 11 as the date 1 in 10 years reach, and October 24 as the date half of years reach (read again on November 2, 2026). The strip uses October 24 and April 23, both stored as `half_of_years` on the two frost rules next to their NOAA source, and says "half of years" instead of "average". The October 11 reminder card is unchanged.
- **Shipped:** with no script, motion off or reduced motion, the whole strip shows filled in next to four numbered steps that name every date in words. The strip is hidden from screen readers. Weight: about 2.5 KB compressed added, including the shared kit file. The same strip on `/grow/` is left for a follow-up.
