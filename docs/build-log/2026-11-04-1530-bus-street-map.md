# 2026-11-04: Bus map: a clean street map, loaded on tap (#1721)

- **Why:** the drawn bus map was still hard to read on a phone. David compared Cincinnati Metro's map (easier to read, still unpleasant on mobile) and asked for a much cleaner, Mapbox-like map.
- **Decided with David:** MapLibre GL JS (free, no account), a Census-made base map hosted with the site, an exception to "no embedded maps" so it loads only on tap, and Mountain Line's route file for real bus lines later (decision `docs/decisions/2026-11-04-1500-street-map-on-tap.md`; the draft ask is on #26).
- **Built:** "Expand map" now opens a full screen street map: every street with its name, the river and creeks, parks and campuses, in the site's own colors (light and dark). Our places sit on top as dots with names; the trip shows as numbered markers where you get on, plus your end point. Tap a place to see its buses; tap a route in the panel to light up its places. Pinch or + and - to zoom. "Show my location" stays on the phone.
- **Data:** `site/scripts/make-street-map.py` turns TIGER/Line 2024 roads, water, area landmarks and the county outline into five GeoJSON files (about 430 KB compressed in all; local streets, the biggest, load last). Noto Sans label fonts (OFL) are hosted with them.
- **Kept:** the drawn map on the page, and as the fallback when WebGL or the download fails.
- **Not yet:** bus lines on real streets (waits on #26 and #1447).
- **Cost:** none.
