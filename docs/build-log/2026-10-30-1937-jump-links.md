# 2026-10-30: Jump links land with the heading at the top

- **Considered:** David tapped "Behind on rent" on the home page (#449). The money page opened the steps for that pick, then called `focus()` on "Your steps". Browsers scroll only far enough to show a focused element, so the heading sat near the bottom of the screen and the checklist filled the view.
- **Chose:** scroll "Your steps" to the top of the screen (focus without the browser's own scroll, then `scrollIntoView` with `block: 'start'`). Every element with an `id` also gets 16px of room above it, so a jump like `/partners/#fix` does not jam the heading against the top edge. Pages with the sticky crisis strip already keep 8rem clear.
- **Checked:** Playwright at iPhone 13 width, `/money/?pick=rent`, `/money/?pick=bill`, `/partners/#fix`, `/stay-tonight/#bed`.
- **Cost:** $0.
