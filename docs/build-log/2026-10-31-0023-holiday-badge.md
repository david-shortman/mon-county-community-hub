# 2026-10-31: The Open now badge knows the holidays a place closes

- **Considered:** #726. The Mountaineer Transfer Station closes on six holidays (named on the county's disposal page), but the place card's badge would say "Open now" on, say, Memorial Day.
- **Chose:** an optional `closed_on` list of the holidays the source names (`new-years-day`, `memorial-day`, `independence-day`, `labor-day`, `thanksgiving`, `christmas`). The badge works out each holiday's date by its fixed rule (for example the fourth Thursday of November) and says "Closed today for Thanksgiving Day". "Opens ..." skips a listed holiday. When a fixed-date holiday falls on a weekend, we don't guess whether the Friday before or Monday after is the day off; those days say "Holiday hours may differ today". The hours in words and "Call to confirm." still show.
- **Data:** the transfer station gets `closed_on` for the six holidays its source lists.
- **Checked:** Playwright clock at eight dates, including Thanksgiving 2026, Christmas 2027 on a Saturday, and July 4, 2027 on a Sunday.
- **Cost:** $0.
