# 2026-11-01: After you file for unemployment

- **Changed:** new page `/after-you-apply/unemployment/`: the deputy's decision (employer's 4 days, informal hearing within 5), the 8 calendar day appeal deadline, the appeal tribunal hearing (10 days' notice, ruling within 21 days), the Board of Review (8 days), the Intermediate Court of Appeals (30 days, 20 for labor disputes), and benefits during an appeal. Part of #1148.
- **Why:** filing is covered by #790; the 8 day appeal window is the deadline people most often miss.
