# Org entries for three groups already on the calendar

**What:** Org records for the West Virginia Botanic Garden, the Cheat Lake Volunteer Fire Department and M. T. Pockets Theatre. Each already hosts dates on our calendar (by `org_name`) but had no org record. The Cheat Lake fire hall's place record now links to the new fire department record. Part of #5, sprint wave 17 (#529).

**How they were found:** every `org_name` in `content/` was compared with `content/orgs/`. These three had their own readable sites.

**Left out:**
- Visit Mountaineer Country (the visitors bureau): its site answers automated reads with a bot check page, so it was not read. A person can add it.
- WVU Water Research Institute (runs the Mon River Sweep): `wvwri.wvu.edu` refuses to serve its robots.txt (403), so under our rules nothing on it is read.
- Monongalia County Child Advocacy Center: drafted, but it is a top-tier listing (family, health), so it goes in its own PR with a second check.
- No staff or board names, even where the group's own page lists them.

**Sources (read 2026-11-14):** each record's `sources`, with a quote copied from each page.
