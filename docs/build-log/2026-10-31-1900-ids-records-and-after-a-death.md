# 2026-10-31: Birth certificates, IDs and records, and When someone dies

- **Considered:** the coverage map (#222) found no page for getting official papers or for what to do after a death. Almost everyone needs a birth certificate for a REAL ID, a passport or some jobs, and every family deals with a death. The site only had the DMV office and new-resident license rules.
- **Chose:** two pages in the Living here door under the new `records` topic. `/ids-and-records/`: certified birth, death and marriage certificates from the Monongalia County Clerk ($5, born or recorded in Mon County) or WV Vital Registration ($12), marriage licenses ($57, or $37 with pre-marital counseling), WV ID and REAL ID, Youth ID, replacing a lost ID, fixing a certificate, and a veteran's DD-214. `/when-someone-dies/`: death certificates and who may order them, reporting a death to Social Security, the USAGov list of offices to tell, the County Clerk's Probate Office (will or no will, small estate affidavit under $50,000, fees and timeline), and VA burial benefits. 15 new records and 3 new orgs. Marriage license fees and the probate phone and paths were re-read by hand.
- **Left off:** the adult ID card fee (no DMV page we read lists it), mailed card wait times (DMV pages say 30 to 45 days and about 15 business days), Social Security card replacement and the $255 death payment (ssa.gov returns 403), passport places in Morgantown (the locators would not load), how many death certificates to order, and the FTC Funeral Rule (its page returns 404 to our reader, so we do not cite it). They are in needs-verification issue #1025.
- **Cost:** $0.
