# 2026-10-30: Center dinners and events on the calendar (#457)

- **Considered:** every dated public event at a listed center between now and the end of the year, from the centers' own pages and calendars and the Dominion Post's community and senior news listings.
- **Chose:** only events whose page shows the 2026 date (or a listing dated October 2026 that names the day). Each has `venue:` so it shows on its center's page and the section's "Eat with neighbors" list. Times only where the source states them in a way we trust; the Smithtown calendar's stored times are off on other entries, so its dinner has no time.
- **Shipped:** 8 entries: Arnettsville bag bingo (Nov 1) and holiday bazaar (Dec 6), Smithtown steak dinner (Nov 7), Westside movie night (Nov 13) and music bingo (Nov 20), and Cheat Lake VFD black light bingo (Oct 31), craft and bake sale (Dec 5) and children's Christmas party (Dec 13).
- **Left out:** Mason-Dixon and Chestnut Ridge "Evening With Santa" (the county page shows no year), private hall rentals on the Cheat Lake calendar, board meetings, a church dinner in Preston County, and phone numbers printed in listings that may belong to volunteers.
- **Cost:** $0.
