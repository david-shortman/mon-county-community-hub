# 2026-11-01: Dates at a known place get its town

- **Considered:** 60 of 84 dates had no town, so the Dates page could not group them (#395, #529). Most are countywide deadlines or school days that have no one town, and many others give only a "Morgantown, WV" mailing address, which does not say the town (content/README.md). Two were held at a place in `places/` whose record names its town.
- **Chose:** set the town on those two (the BOPARC glassmaking workshop at Wiles Hill Community Center, and the M. T. Pockets Theatre play). `npm run check-content` now warns when a date's `venue` names its town (`community: Westover` or `Wiles Hill, Morgantown`) and the date has no town or a different one.
- **Left off:** dates with only an address. Their town waits for a source that names it.
- **Cost:** $0.
