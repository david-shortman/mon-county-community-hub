# 2026-11-14: Follow one estate through probate, an explainer on /probate-steps/ (#1934, part of #1440)

- **Chose:** a five-step scroll story above the Clerk's lists on `/probate-steps/`: open the estate, the appraisement within 90 days, the notice in the newspaper (after the appraisement is recorded, or after 120 days), 60 days for creditors from the notice, and closing. A marker moves along a line of days and the 90-day and 60-day clocks fill in as bands. People settling an estate often ask how long it takes and which clock is running; the Clerk's PDF answers that, but as text.

After #1925, no total is drawn: the last step uses the Clerk's own "at the minimum four months, but can take much longer". Fees stay in the lists below, not in the drawing.
