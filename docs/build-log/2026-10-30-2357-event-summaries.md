# 2026-10-30: Event cards from the city and county feeds say what they are

- **Chose:** in the city and county feed readers, use the feed's own description as the card's summary when it says more than the title (first sentences, about 40 words at most, links removed). The county feed has some junk times (1:00 a.m., 11:18), so only quarter-hour times from 6 a.m. to 10:45 p.m. are used. The city's end times are placeholders (11:59 p.m., or three hours), so city cards show only the start.
