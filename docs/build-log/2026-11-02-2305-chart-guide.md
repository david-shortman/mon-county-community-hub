# 2026-11-02: How we're doing, a guided look at one chart (#1451)

- **Considered:** the unemployment by month chart on `/how-we-are-doing/`. People who find charts hard need to know where to look first. The issue planned four steps, including the state line next to the county line.
- **Chose:** three steps on the scroll story kit (#1441): the newest month, the same month a year before, and how to read any chart here. A ring moves to the point each step names; a dashed bracket shows the gap between the two months; the last step lights up the axes. The words for each step are worked out at build time from the same monthly points (`site/src/lib/chart-guide.mjs`), never typed in. They point at values only and never say why a number moved.
- **Left out:** the state line. The site has only the county series, and BLS asks robots not to read its data sites, so a state series cannot be added automatically (see `docs/operations/automated-reads.md`).
- **Shipped:** with no script, motion off or reduced motion, the chart stays as it was and the three steps read as a numbered list under it. Weight: about 2.2 KB compressed added, including the shared kit file.
