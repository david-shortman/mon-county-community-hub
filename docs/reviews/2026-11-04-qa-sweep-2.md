**Reviewer:** Claude Code (sprint wave 14, QA sweep 2 lane, part of #529)
**Scope:** every page built from `main` at `59d1e15` (November 3, 2026, about 12:50 UTC), plus the same code built as if it were the morning of November 4, the first daily build after Election Day. The first sweep is [`2026-11-03-qa-sweep.md`](2026-11-03-qa-sweep.md).

## Summary

The site is in good shape. On 527 pages at three sizes, nothing scrolls sideways, nothing sticks past the edge, there are no script errors or failed requests, no internal link or anchor is broken, no two pages share a title, and no unfinished text (TODO, placeholder, lorem, "undefined") is left. Everything the first sweep fixed is still fixed.

What this sweep found:

- **Phone numbers in sentences still split across two lines** at 390px: 192 splits on 96 pages. The first sweep fixed numbers that are links, not numbers written inside a sentence ("call 1-" / "877-322-8228").
- **Two Election Day listings would read as upcoming on November 4.** The /vote/ page switches to results on its own, but the polling places and absentee ballot listings stay on the County Clerk group page, Make a list and two printouts, still saying "voting on Tuesday, November 3."
- **Small polish:** a "?:" after question headings on Which office handles it, and a few soft corners left from the old look.

For each one it recorded sideways scroll, any element past the right edge, text cut off by a box, script errors, console errors, failed requests and 404s.

- **Screenshots** of those 10 new pages at 390px in light and dark, read by eye.
- **Phone numbers in text:** every phone number in the text of every page at 390px, checked for a line break inside the number.
- **Corners:** every element in `main` with a corner over 3px at 390px.
- **Static checks** on the built HTML: every internal link and `#anchor`, past `<time>` dates next to "coming up" words, Election Day wording, duplicate titles, "New" labels, leftover text, and name variants (the same name spelled two ways).
- **Content cross-check** on `content/`: the same address with different phone numbers or hours, the same org with two spellings of one address, one phone number listed for different orgs.
- **Tomorrow's build:** the site built again with the clock set to November 4, 10:20 UTC (the daily build runs at 10:17 UTC), then the same static checks with November 4 as today.

The crawl scripts were run from a scratch folder and are not part of the repo.

## What is fine

- **Layout:** 0 of 1,581 page views scroll sideways or have an element past the edge. 0 of 30 at 320px with 200% text. The only "cut off" text is the screen reader copy of the header weather, which is hidden on purpose.
- **Errors:** no script errors, console errors, failed requests or 404s on any page view.
- **Links:** every internal link and anchor resolves, on both the November 3 and November 4 builds.
- **Dates:** every past date next to "coming up" is the start of a window still open ("Thu, Oct 15 to Mon, Dec 7, open now"). On the November 4 build, /vote/ says "Polls are closed" and points to results, and the calendar's Election Day entry has dropped off. The "not being kept up" notice stays hidden.
- **Leftovers:** no TODO, TBD, FIXME, lorem, placeholder, "undefined", "NaN" or "[object Object]" in any page's text. No "New" labels.
- **Duplicates:** no two pages share a title.
- **Conflicting facts:** in `content/`, the same address never has two different hours for the same office, and every shared phone number is a real shared line (a front desk for several programs, or a utility that bills for a town).
- **Names:** "Monongalia County Health Department" (46 pages) and the short form "Mon County Health Department" (3 pages), "Mountain Line" and "Mountain Line Transit", "MUB" and "Morgantown Utility Board" are all used, and each is clear.
