# QA sweep: the whole site after the Press redesign, November 3, 2026

**Reviewer:** Claude Code (sprint wave 11, site QA sweep lane, #1531, part of #529)
**Scope:** every page built from `main` at `a9edd19` (November 3, 2026, 00:00 UTC), after two days of several hundred PRs and the switch to the Press design (#1425).

## Summary

The site held up well. On 954 pages at three sizes, nothing scrolls sideways, nothing sticks past the screen edge, no text boxes overlap, no internal link or anchor is broken, every page is under its weight budget, and every page that should have a source line has one. No classes or switches from the removed looks are left.

What the sweep did find: a script error on 11 pages, phone numbers that split across two lines on 65 pages, leftover soft corners from the old look, a sentence that said "Morgantown" twice on community center pages, 42 finder pages that share one heading, one listing whose date has passed but still reads as future, and a set of pages above the reading grade target. The small ones are fixed in four PRs; the rest are issues.

## How it was checked

- **Build:** `npm ci`, `npm run check-content`, `npm run build` in `site/` (all postbuild checks pass: pages, contrast, weight, API, reading, label space, personas, tests).
- **Crawl:** Playwright (the preinstalled Chromium) served `dist/` locally and opened all 954 pages at **390px light, 390px dark and 1280px light** (2,862 page views). For each one it recorded: sideways scroll, any element past the right edge, text clipped by a box, overlapping blocks in `main`, corners over 3px, script errors and failed requests.
- **Reflow sample:** 25 pages at **320px with 200% text** (home, the five doors, `/calendar/`, `/topics/`, `/search/`, `/start-a-business/`, `/utility-bills/`, `/thrift-shops/`, `/community-centers/`, `/meetings/`, `/property-tax/`, `/bus-routes/`, `/how-we-are-doing/`, `/holidays/`, `/groups/`, `/towns/`, `/recycling/`, `/snap/`, `/about/`, `/data/`, `/pulse/`).
- **Phone numbers:** every `tel:` and `sms:` link on 506 pages (all but embeds and the API) at 390px, checked for a line break inside the number.
- **Static checks** on the built HTML: every internal link and `#anchor`, links that point back to their own page, `<time>` dates before today near "coming up" words, past dates in future-tense sentences, duplicate titles, H1s and descriptions, and name variants (the same capitalized name spelled two ways).
- **Reading grades:** `dist/reading-grades.json`, written by `check-reading.mjs`.
- **Weight:** the `check-weight.mjs` output from the build.
- **Screenshots** of a few page types not in the Press audit (`/home-calendar/`, `/needs/`, `/who-serves-me/`, `/medicaid-2027/`, `/tonight/`, a What help results page) at 390px in light and dark.

The crawl scripts were run from a scratch folder and are not part of the repo.

## What is fine

- **Layout:** 0 of 2,862 page views scroll sideways or have an element past the edge, in light or dark. 0 of 25 at 320px with 200% text. No overlapping blocks. The only "clipped" boxes are table header rows that are hidden on phones on purpose (`/community-centers/halls/`, `/how-it-works/`).
- **Links:** every internal link and anchor resolves (the only hits were `webcal://` subscribe links, which are correct). No internal link points to one of the three moved group pages. No requests failed.
- **Dates:** every past date near "coming up" is the start of a window still open ("Sun, Nov 1 to Fri, Jan 15, 2027, open now"), and the page script keeps "today" and "ends in N days" current. Only #5 above reads wrong.
- **Sources:** every indexed page has a source or checked line.
- **Weight:** all 955 pages are within budget.
- **Duplicates:** no two pages share a title. The 19 community center pages and their flyers share an H1 on purpose. Similar topics (`/meet-people/` and `/find-your-people/`, `/find-a-job/` and `/work-and-learning/`, the selling food pages) answer different questions and link each other.
- **Old looks:** no `data-look`, `?look=`, Field guide or Ledger classes left anywhere in the built site or `src/`.
