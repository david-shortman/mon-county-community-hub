This is the first post on this blog. It covers what the site is for, how it gets made, and what it does not try to be.

## What it is

Morgantown and Mon County already have a lot of help. It comes from county and city offices, state programs, nonprofits, churches, the university and neighbors. But the details are spread across many websites, PDFs and phone lines. This site gathers them in one place and sorts them around the questions people ask, like where to get food or where to recycle old electronics.

Every listing links to its source, most often the group's own page. It also shows the date it was last checked. You deal with the group, not with this site. There are no sign-ups, no payments and no ads.

The first pages went up on October 28, 2026, starting with a page of local dates and deadlines ([build log](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/blob/main/docs/build-log/2026-10-28-0359-first-site-and-calendar.md)). [The idea behind this site](/the-idea/) lays out the plan in four parts, with links to check each one.

## How AI is used

Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, does most of the research, writing and building. The rule is that it only repeats facts it found on a public page and read that same day. If it can't find a source, the fact stays off the site.

Before a change goes live, automatic checks run on it, and a second AI run checks it against the project's rules for sources, plain words, fairness and privacy. AI can still get things wrong. That is one reason every listing shows its source: so you can check it yourself. [How this site works](/how-it-works/) follows one real listing through each step.

## A person is responsible

One local resident, the owner, sets the direction and the rules. The owner checks changes about shelter, crisis help, food and benefits before they go live, and looks over other pages after they are up. Every post on this blog also waits for the owner before it goes up.

On its own, the AI may never contact anyone, spend money, take sides or collect personal information it does not need. [About this site](/about/) says who the owner is and lists these limits.

## What it is not

- It is not a government site. It does not speak for any office or group.
- It does not replace local groups or help lines. It sends people to them.
- It does not take sides. No candidates, no parties, no favorite businesses.
- It never tells you that you qualify for a program. The program decides.
- It does not track you. It counts page visits, with no cookies.

[Hard questions](/hard-questions/) answers these and more, including where the site falls short. As of November 14, 2026, that page says no one who grew up in Mon County reviews the pages, and the project has not asked local groups what they think of the site.

## What this blog is for

Posts here are dated notes about the project: what changed, how it works, and what the work turns up in the public record. Phone numbers, hours and deadlines change, so posts link to the pages that keep them current instead of repeating them.

If something in a post or on the site is wrong, the fix form is linked at the bottom of every page. Fixes to facts, links and dates are listed on the [Corrections page](/corrections/).
