Each listing on this site takes the same path before you see it. This post follows one real listing, the county recycling drop-off center, through that path. The steps come from [How we check](/how-we-check/) and [How this site works](/how-it-works/), as those pages read on November 14, 2026.

## 1. Start from a public page

An AI opens the group's own page, or a government page, and reads it that day. Nothing is filled in from memory. If the page does not say something, it stays off the site.

For the recycling center, there were two sources: the center's own website and the county Solid Waste Authority's page. They list closing times a few minutes apart. The listing does not pick one. It says which page says what, and points to the center's site to check before you go. That is the rule when sources disagree.

## 2. Write it down with a date

The facts go into a small public file, one per listing, along with each source link and the date it was read. That date shows on the listing as "last checked". The recycling center's file is [public on GitHub](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/blob/main/content/resources/mon-county-recycling-drop-off.yaml), like every other listing's.

## 3. Programs check every change

Before any change goes live, programs run on it. A listing with no source or no date stops the change. So does a broken link or a page that does not work on a small phone with large text.

## 4. A second look

Next, a second AI run reads the change and scores it against the project's written guides for sources, plain words, fairness and privacy.

A separate program, the High stakes check, looks for changes to pages about shelter, crisis help, food and benefits. It labels those for the owner. They do not go live until the owner has checked them. The recycling listing is not one of those.

## 5. Check again every day

Once a listing is up, a program visits every outside page the site uses, every day. It flags pages that are gone, pages that moved, and phone numbers that are no longer on the source page. Flagged listings go on a public to-do list to be read again.

Listings also have a recheck date. For health, money, housing, family, taxes, voting and dates, it is 90 days. For the rest, it is 6 months. Past that date, the listing itself says it may be out of date.

## What this does not do

As of November 14, 2026, nobody calls the groups or visits them in person. The checks read public pages. If a group's own page is wrong or old, the listing can be wrong too. [How we check](/how-we-check/) says this plainly and compares it with how other directories check.

When a listing is wrong, the fix is made in the open. Fixes to facts, links and dates are listed on the [Corrections page](/corrections/).
