The first pages of this site went up on October 28, 2026. This post looks back at the first two weeks, through November 8, using the two weekly reports the project wrote about itself: [week one](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/blob/main/docs/chronicle/2026-11-01-week.md) and [week two](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/blob/main/docs/chronicle/2026-11-08-week.md). Every number below comes from those reports and the daily snapshot files they link.

## What was built

- **A name and an address.** The site became Everything Morgantown, at everythingmorgantown.org, in the week of October 26.
- **Dates in one place.** The [calendar](/calendar/) grew from 7 entries on October 28 to 92 on November 8. You can add it to a phone's calendar.
- **Answer pages.** The project tests a set of everyday questions about life here. By November 8, the site had an answer page for 63 of 65 of them.
- **Checks that run on their own.** A daily source check started watching the official pages that listings come from. Other checks test every change for broken links, reading level and large text.
- **A page about how it works.** An About page explains how AI is used, how facts get checked and how mistakes get fixed.
- **Rules for serious pages.** Changes to pages about shelter, crisis help, food and benefits wait for the owner to check them first.

By the project's count, 667 pull requests had merged by November 1, and 908 by November 8. A pull request is one public change, with a note on what changed and why. Most were small and written by AI sessions working side by side.

## What has not happened yet

**The site was not in search results.** The main measure asks: if someone searches in plain words, do they land on a correct local answer? The [scoreboard](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/blob/main/docs/scoreboard.md) scored the first 32 questions at 76 out of 100 on October 28. It was still 76 on November 1 and November 8. The site's pages showed up in none of the test searches, so they could not raise the score. Setting up search engines waits on the owner ([#89](https://github.com/david-shortman/mon-county-community-hub/issues/89)).

**No resident was recorded using the site.** A visit counter went live on November 1. Its first monthly totals are due in early December. Nobody has measured whether anyone acted because of the site.

**No resident testing and no partner group.** As of November 8, no one outside the project had tested the pages, and no local group had joined.

**A lot waits on one person.** On November 8, 124 open items were labeled for the owner. The week two goal was fewer than 80. AI sessions can draft quickly, but a person has to sign off on the sensitive parts.

## Facts left off the site

When a fact can't be confirmed on a current official page, it stays off the site and an issue is opened to track it. Those issues grew from 12 on October 28 to 100 on November 8. Many end with a phone call that only the owner can make.

That list is work still to do. It is also a record of places where the public record has holes, which a later post will cover.
